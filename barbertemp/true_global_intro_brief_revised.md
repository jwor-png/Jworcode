**What We're Building**

BarberRepublic is a two-sided labour marketplace for the professional barbering industry. It connects barbershops needing temporary or emergency cover with qualified, verified barbers available for short-notice work.

The model parallels Workrise—not Uber. Workrise built a defensible, professional-grade platform for skilled workers in a specialist industry. BarberRepublic does the same for barbering: we solve a structural problem, not a temporary one.

---

**The Problem**

When a barber calls in sick, a shop loses revenue immediately. The industry runs on informal arrangements and personal contacts. There is no professional platform. No verified pool. No standard. The labour gap is structural, not cyclical.

Working from home and flexible work patterns have broken the predictable busy/quiet cycles barbershops relied on for decades. Walk-in trade is less reliable. Permanent staff scheduling is harder. Emergency cover has become a permanent operational need—and the industry has no solution.

**Unit Economics**

*Barber perspective:*
- 2 shifts per week through BarberRepublic at average £180 per shift
- Monthly gross revenue: approximately £1,440 from platform work
- Super-professionals can condense their client base into 3 days, freeing 2 days for travel and platform bookings
- Guarantee of work through the platform matters more than marginal leakage risk

*Shop perspective:*
- Emergency cover cost: £250 per shift
- Premium fully-booked day revenue: approximately £550
- Emergency cover at £250 is easily acceptable on a once-off basis—much better than the alternative of losing the customer
- Emergency cover is a reactive problem-solver, not a profit center

*Platform perspective:*
- Commission-based model on cover bookings
- Break-even occurs at approximately 900-1,500 bookings per month (illustrative 12-15% take rate at current day rates)
- Clear path to profitability as booking volume grows

---

**The Platform**

MVP scope (Weeks 1-14, £80k-120k):
- Seven-screen app: shop posts chair, barber fills it, payment processes, both rate
- Stripe payment processing for transparent pricing
- Twilio SMS notifications for real-time cover requests
- Rules-based matching (location, barber qualification, shop requirements)
- London scope initially: 20-30 barbers, 15-20 shops
- Guaranteed cover mechanic: if a barber cancels, automated backup barber assigned

Future layers (Phase 2+):
- AI-native demand forecasting by location and day
- Surge pricing for emergency and same-day cover
- Loyalty layer for established barbers
- Tiered marketplace by market level (local, city, national)
