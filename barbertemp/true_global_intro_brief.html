BarberTemp
The labour exchange platform for the barbering industry
What We're Building

BarberTemp is a two-sided labour marketplace for the professional barbering industry. It connects barbershops needing temporary or emergency cover with qualified, verified barbers available for short-notice work. The model parallel is Workrise, not Uber. Workrise built a defensible, professional-grade platform for skilled workers in a specialist industry. BarberTemp does the same for barbering.

The Problem

When a barber calls in sick, a shop loses revenue immediately. The industry runs on informal arrangements and personal contacts. There is no professional platform, no verified pool, no standard. The labour gap is structural, not cyclical.

The Market

The UK barbering industry is growing, culturally significant, and entirely underserved by technology. High staff turnover and unpredictable demand are constants for every shop owner. No one has built the infrastructure layer.

The Platform
  • Tiered marketplace by market level -- local, city, national
  • Surge pricing for emergency and same-day cover
  • VIP and celebrity barber section
  • Loyalty layer for established barbers
  • Unmanned kiosk capability as a future layer
The AI Layer

Behavioural pattern matching, demand forecasting by location and day, and automated shift cover are AI-native opportunities built into the architecture from the start. This is not a bolt-on.

The Team

Johnny Shanahan is the founding industry authority. Manchester-based, regular London presence, two decades in the barbering world. He knows the shops, the barbers, and the culture. Commercial and technical advisory support is in place.
