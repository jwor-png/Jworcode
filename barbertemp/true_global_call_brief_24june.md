## BarberTemp in Her Language

Do not frame this as a staffing app. Frame it as:

**An intelligent labour exchange that changes the operating behaviour of the professional barbering industry.**

The structural problem: when a barber calls in sick, a shop loses revenue that day. Every shop owner in the UK has this problem. There is no professional solution. BarberTemp builds the infrastructure layer that does not yet exist.

The model parallel is Workrise (formerly RigUp) -- which built a defensible, professional-grade platform for skilled workers in a specialist industry and became the dominant player in that niche. **Do not use the Uber comparison. Silvana will hear "gig economy commodity race to the bottom." Workrise is the right reference.**

The AI layer is not a bolt-on. Demand forecasting by location and day, behavioural pattern matching, automated cover matching -- these are native to the architecture.
