AMENDED & RESTATED LOAN AGREEMENT

Date: 25 June 2026

---

PARTIES

(1) PAUL CANDON (the "Lender")

(2) JOHN WEBB-O'ROURKE (the "Borrower")

Together (the "Parties")

---

BACKGROUND

a. The parties entered into a loan agreement originally dated 2 November 2023.

b. Funds were applied to MiDentalCare Limited.

c. It was envisaged that the business would secure funding from third parties which would facilitate repayment of the loan.

d. Despite extensive efforts over an extended period, the anticipated funding did not materialise.

e. MiDentalCare Limited subsequently ceased trading.

f. The parties wish to restate and regularise the repayment terms applying to the loan.

---

AGREEMENT

1. Restated Loan Amount

The parties agree that the sum of €208,000 represents the full amount due and owing by the Borrower to the Lender as of the date of this Agreement.

2. Interest

No further interest shall accrue on the Restated Loan Amount up to 31 December 2026.

3. Repayment

The Borrower shall repay the Restated Loan Amount in full on or before 31 December 2026.

The Borrower may make repayments at any time before that date without penalty.

There shall be no obligation to make interim payments unless otherwise agreed in writing between the parties.

The Borrower shall use all reasonable endeavours to reduce the Restated Loan Amount during the period up to 31 December 2026 and shall apply such funds as become available to him towards repayment of the loan, with the intention of discharging the balance in full on or before 31 December 2026.

4. Bank of Ireland Facility

The Borrower confirms that he is engaging directly with Bank of Ireland in relation to the relevant loan facilities and agrees to take such reasonable steps as may be required to regularise and assume responsibility for those facilities, subject to agreement with Bank of Ireland.

5. Default

If the Restated Loan Amount remains unpaid after 31 December 2026, the outstanding balance shall become immediately due and payable and shall thereafter carry interest at 8% per annum until discharged in full.

6. Settlement

Upon payment in full of the Restated Loan Amount, all obligations arising from the loan shall be fully discharged and satisfied.

7. Personal Loan

The parties acknowledge that this is and remains a personal loan arrangement between the Lender and the Borrower.

8. Governing Law

This Agreement shall be governed by and construed in accordance with the laws of Ireland.
