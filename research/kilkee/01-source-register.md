## Evidence tiering standard

Applied throughout this pack.

- **Tier A.** Primary document obtained and read in full by Cuan.
- **Tier B.** Reported consistently by two or more named outlets, or stated on an official body's own page.
- **Tier C.** Single source, or a claim advanced by one party to the dispute and not independently confirmed.
- **Tier D.** Cuan analysis or inference. Not a sourced finding. Must be tested against primary documents before it is relied on.

**Current Tier A holdings: none.** No primary document has been read. Everything below Tier B in this pack rests on media reporting or on party statements.

## Conflicts in the public record

Flagged because they matter for credibility and because at least one side is wrong.

1. **Number of submissions.** Reported variously as "over 140 public submissions", "almost 90 objections" and "over 80 objections". These may be measuring different things, total submissions versus objections. The Council file settles it.
2. **Distance to the cliff walk.** Reported as 620 metres from the cliff walk (Clare Echo, Clare FM). Uisce Éireann is reported as saying the existing outfall is approximately 225 metres from the end of the cliff walk. Those are different measurements to different points and are being used interchangeably in public debate.
3. **Site area.** 4.5 hectares and 4.56 hectares both appear.
4. **Design population.** 7,926 p.e. is the figure in circulation; some reporting says "up to 7,000". The campaign's 20,000 summer figure is a peak visitor claim, not a p.e. figure, and the two are not the same unit.
