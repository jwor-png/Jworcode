# Kilkee: Document Retrieval List

Prepared 8 August 2026. Cuan.

Ordered by value per hour of effort, not by tidiness. Items 1 to 4 are free, online, and will answer most of what matters. Everything after that costs money or time.

## Priority 1. Free, online, do these first

### 1.1 ACP 321258, the Direction and the Amended Direction
Source: https://www.pleanala.ie/en-ie/case/321258
Why: the only document that records why the Commission departed from its own inspector. Determines whether the reasons ground is real or dead.
Read for: whether it addresses each of the inspector's refusal grounds, whether it invokes section 37(2)(b) of the Planning and Development Act 2000 for material contravention, and what changed between the Direction and the Amended Direction.

### 1.2 ACP 321258, the Inspector's Report
Likely direct URL if the case page will not load: https://www.pleanala.ie/anbordpleanala/media/abp/cases/reports/321/r321258.pdf
Read for: the inspector's stated reasons for recommending refusal, the EIA screening conclusion, the Appropriate Assessment conclusion, and the treatment of alternative sites and design population.

### 1.3 ACP 321258, the Board Order and Amended Order
Read for: the conditions attached, whether any condition addresses future upgrade to secondary treatment, whether odour limits are conditioned, and the stated reasons and considerations.

### 1.4 Clare County Council 24/60002, the planner's report and the notification of decision
Source: Clare County Council ePlanning. On iPhone, if only the case summary appears, select "Documents" or "View Scanned Files".
Read for: the four stated reasons for refusal in their exact wording, and the planner's assessment of capacity and visual impact.

### 1.5 EPA licence D0078-01, licence and 2024 Annual Environmental Report
Source: EPA licensing portal.
Read for: the specified improvement schedule. Confirm or disprove that it records a secondary treatment plant with a 2027 completion date. This is the single highest value verification in the whole pack, because it either creates a documented contradiction between two state bodies or removes an argument.

### 1.6 ACP 322828, the CPO file
Source: https://www.pleanala.ie/en-ie/case/322828
Read for: the deposited map and schedule, the plots and owners affected, objections lodged, whether an oral hearing is to be held, and the current stage. This sets the real project timeline regardless of the appeal.

## Priority 2. Technical documents from the Council file, 24/60002

Pull these together, they only make sense read as a set.

- Population and hydraulic loading calculations. The p.e. derivation, the peak flow figure, and the basis for the thirty year horizon.
- Process design for the treatment plant. Expected removal efficiencies for suspended solids, BOD and bacteriological indicators.
- Outfall dispersion modelling. Whether bathing water compliance is achieved by treatment or by dilution.
- Emergency storage calculation. What return period event fills the 951 cubic metres, and where flow goes when it is full. Establishes the Victoria Stream question as fact or fiction.
- Natura Impact Statement. Whether overflow events were modelled against the European sites, or excluded as exceptional.
- EIA screening determination. Whether location sensitivity and cumulative effects were assessed, or whether the sub threshold conclusion was reached on the numbers alone.
- Odour assessment and its modelled receptor distances.
- Landscape and visual impact assessment, and any site selection or alternatives report.
- Uisce Éireann's response to the request for further information.

## Priority 3. High Court, 2025 JR 1205

Not yet located in the public judgment search as at 8 August 2026. Further orders on certification and costs are reportedly outstanding, which is the likely reason.

Two routes:
1. Wait for publication on the Courts Service site. Free. Likely to follow the September rulings.
2. Request from the High Court Central Office quoting record number 2025 JR 1205. Copying charges apply and some pleadings may require confirmation of sufficient interest. The applicants' solicitors are the faster route if John has or can obtain an introduction through the campaign.

Documents, in order of value:
- The written judgment. Establishes whether the court found legal error and declined relief on discretion, or found no error at all. Nothing else in the appeal analysis can be settled until this is read.
- The perfected order.
- The statement of grounds. Shows exactly which grounds were pleaded, and therefore which grounds are preserved for appeal and which were never run.
- The certification or leave ruling, expected September 2026.
- The costs ruling. Relevant to whether the campaign can afford to continue, and whether the special costs rules for environmental proceedings were applied.
- Opposition papers from An Coimisiún Pleanála and from Uisce Éireann, and the written submissions.

## Priority 4. EU law verification

Confirm from the operative text of Directive (EU) 2024/3019 rather than from summaries:
- the Article and paragraph imposing secondary treatment on agglomerations of 1,000 p.e. and above, and its compliance date;
- the exact wording and conditions of the derogation to 31 December 2037 for coastal agglomerations of 2,000 to below 10,000 p.e. applying Article 7 appropriate treatment on 1 January 2025;
- whether that derogation is conditional on treatment actually being applied on that date, which is the point on which the Kilkee argument turns;
- the transposition deadline and the current state of Irish transposition.

Source: https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/dir/2024/3019/oj/eng

## Notes on access

Every host in this list was blocked from the session in which this pack was prepared. None of it needs specialist access. It is all public. It needs a browser on an unrestricted connection and roughly a day of reading.

If John wants the reading done rather than the fetching, the efficient split is: he pulls the PDFs from items 1.1 to 1.6 and drops them into Drive, Cuan reads and marks them up. That converts the whole pack from Tier B to Tier A in one pass.
