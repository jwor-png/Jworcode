What Meridian Is

World-class supporting material. Never the adviser of record.

Meridian produces decision-support material for the professional who reads it, judges it, and decides how to use it with clients and businesses. It never steps in front of that professional as the adviser of record — the output is supporting material, not advice issued in its own name.

Grounded

No claim, figure or authority reaches the desk unless it was retrieved from a primary source and the source actually supports it. Nothing is shipped from memory.

Checked

Every output that matters is reviewed by an independent check before it is consolidated — a genuinely adversarial second look, not a glance over its own work.

Accountable

The output always carries a status: draft, in review, or decision-ready. Only decision-ready means it can be relied on — and the record of how it got there travels with it.

How It Works

One point of contact. A team behind it.

The brief goes in plain language. Meridian classifies it, routes it to the specialists it needs, consolidates what comes back, and returns one clean answer — never a pile of disconnected outputs to reconcile.

  1. 01

    Intake

    Restate the intent in one line. Name the domain it touches. Separate known fact from assumption. List what's missing.

  2. 02

    Route

    Send the work to whichever specialist managers genuinely apply — often more than one — and run them in parallel.

  3. 03

    Produce, then check

    Each manager drafts its piece. An independent check reviews it against primary sources before anything moves forward.

  4. 04

    Consolidate & return

    One clean answer, carrying its status and a short verification record — sources used, what the check found, confidence, any escalation.

The Governance Floor

Nothing reaches the desk that can't show how it was reached.

Every output runs through the same discipline before it's relied on. This is what makes the work defensible — and what makes it worth trusting.

The grounding rule

No statute, ruling, valuation figure or title fact appears unless it was retrieved from a primary source and the passage actually supports the claim. Every authority and hard number is tagged verified, reported, or unverified — only verified is load-bearing.

The independent check

High-stakes output, and anything that will be acted on, gets a fresh, adversarial re-read against the primary sources — never a glance over its own work.

Status labelling

Draft, in review, or decision-ready — every piece says exactly where it stands, and exactly what's missing if it isn't ready yet.

The audit record

An append-only record of who produced what, from which sources, what the check found, and at what confidence. Every answer can show its own lineage.

Meridian informs. It does not represent, file, or sign on anyone's behalf. Anything creating a binding obligation goes to the appropriate qualified professional before it's acted on.
