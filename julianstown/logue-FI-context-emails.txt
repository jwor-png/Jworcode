=== THE DENSITY DISPUTE — bears on Logue points 16-21 ===
VERIFIED. Bronagh Clarke, 13 Nov 2025, internal candid assessment:
"Mr. Logue's calculation of density is based on rigid application of the methodology as set out in Appendix B of the Compact Settlement Guidelines, specifically Table 1... The Guidelines state, inter alia, that areas of land that are subject to flooding and/or are in use for larger regional, district and public parks should be excluded from the net site area. As previously highlighted to the Design Team - if the H1 lands are excluded from the net site area then this leaves a net site area of 0.9 hectares which in turn does yield a significantly higher density in the region of 70 dph. If the area subject to flooding is then deducted then the density is extremely high. Such an approach would in our view be completely nonsensical but we do have to point it out as that is what the Guidelines state."

VERIFIED. Same email: "We believe, however, there is a robust justification for including the H1 lands in the net site area and also including the flood area, taking into account the site's constraints, the unique site characteristics and the proposed development of the full extent of H1 lands as a 'local' recreational amenity. Meath County Council would appear to support this view based on the feedback received at pre-planning stage and to date. As I say to do otherwise would in our view be nonsensical... but we can't predict how ACP or the High Court at JR might interpret these."

IMPLICATION: Logue's density methodology (exclude H1/flood land from net site area) is, on Ward's OWN reading, what Appendix B Table 1 of the Compact Settlement Guidelines literally says. The applicant's position is a REASONED DEPARTURE from the literal methodology, justified by site-specific characteristics, which they believe MCC supports. This is a genuine, contestable planning-judgement dispute — NOT a knockout for either side. Ward themselves flag the ACP/JR interpretation risk. Density gets a HIGH-attention rating and its rebuttal is a matter of planning judgement Ward must carry, not a fact Meridian can resolve.
