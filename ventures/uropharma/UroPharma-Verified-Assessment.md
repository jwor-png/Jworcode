## OVERSTATED OR COLLAPSED UNDER SCRUTINY

1. **US$18bn market not credible.** Sub-figures are whole drug markets: UTI ~£8bn, OAB/urgency ~£4bn, NMIBC cancer ~£2bn; "pain & inflammation £4bn" matches no real market of that size. UroPharma sells the delivery device, not the drug. Real addressable delivery niche = low single-digit billions, small share achievable. Deck aggregation, not diligence-grade TAM.
2. **"First Licensed" contradicts "awaiting CE Mark."** Both cannot be true. Device is NOT CE-marked = not lawfully sellable in the EU; UK route leans on the same CE recognition or a separate UKCA assessment. Binary gating risk.
3. **Novelty is narrow; field is crowded and validated.** Standard of care already uses intravesical instillation (BCG, mitomycin). Cleared competitors: UroGen Jelmyto (FDA 2020), J&J/TARIS TAR-200/Inlexzo (FDA approved Sept 2025), Combat Medical HIVEC (commercial EU). A dual-channel drain-then-instil catheter is incremental, not a new modality.
4. **£3.5m raised + grant funding: NOT independently corroborated.** No trace found; the one public reference is a US$25m ambition (different figure/currency). "Multinational patent protection" unconfirmed; only 10% of the raise to "patent development" suggests filings in progress, not a granted portfolio, in a crowded prior-art field (freedom-to-operate risk).
5. **No comparative clinical outcome data.** The cited commentary supports the concept, not this device's safety/efficacy/superiority.

## REGULATORY GATE (the binary risk)

Not CE-marked. Under EU MDR (2017/745), a drug-delivery catheter may be a drug-device combination (Article 117) requiring Notified Body conformity assessment and, if the medicine is integral, a medicine-component consultation or its own authorisation. Months to years, and can fail. **Decisive DD question: what exactly is instilled and who supplies it?** Device-only (light path) vs integral drug-device (heavy path, drug-approval risk). UK: MHRA registration + UK Responsible Person required; GB currently leans on CE recognition (transitional; MHRA consulting Feb 2026 on indefinite CE recognition).

## INVESTMENT READ

- Pattern: £3.5m consumed, still pre-clearance and pre-revenue, now a small £500k bridge before a £3-4m Series A. Bridge behaviour; possibly weak position. Heavy future dilution for a fresh angel.
- £500k use of funds: 50% first commercial batches, 20% product dev, 20% staff, 10% patents. "Commercial batches" implies near-market yet the device is "awaiting CE mark" — probe what milestone £500k actually reaches.
- Valuation sense-check: defensible pre-money ~£2-4m; above ~£4-5m = overpriced given no clearance, revenue or data.
- **EIS is the real driver:** ~30% income tax relief + loss relief roughly halves the downside for a top-rate taxpayer. Conditional on the company qualifying, HMRC advance assurance in hand, and the investor's tax position. EIS reduces loss; it does not create return. Do not let the tax tail wag the dog.
- Honest probability (judgement bands): majority chance of substantial/total loss; small chance of a strong outcome (CE mark + data + trade sale).
