**The one-line version to hold onto:** you are not a geopolitical generalist with good contacts; you are potentially the one person who can move credibly between the Holy See, European statecraft and serious capital. Narrow to that, prove the access is live, and turn it into two or three things a client can buy, and it is a genuinely strong business.
