# MERIDIAN INTELLIGENCE — One-Page Brief
## Ed Lawton's £50k Question — Barber Republic MVP Funding
*Prepared for John Webb O'Rourke ahead of the 10:30am Monday call with Johnny Shanahan and Ed Lawton. Business-only, time-sensitive quick view across Commercial, Legal and Finance lenses.*

---

**The question on the table.** Ed is offering a £50k cheque — effectively covering the full Phase One MVP build (£42k–£50k per the Velocity AI proposal). He has floated staged equity: the more Barber Republic grows, the more his equity is worth, capped at a point. No loan-for-coupon structure has been discussed. Johnny has asked what the offer should be.

---

**Commercial & Deal.** £50k against a pre-revenue platform with no proof point yet is a big ask to price today. Phase One's entire purpose is to *generate* the evidence — real matches, completed bookings, repeat-booking rate, a trust record — that doesn't exist yet. Pricing equity before that evidence exists means negotiating from the weakest possible position. The fix is to defer the valuation question to a point where real evidence exists, not fix it now.

**Legal & Governance.** "Staged equity that grows with the business, capped at a point" is not a standard instrument — it is a bespoke performance-linked ratchet, and it needs a clearly defined trigger metric and cap or it becomes a dispute later. Ed's verbal reassurance ("he won't call shots," "doesn't want to do any work") is not a legal protection. Once he holds equity, he holds whatever rights the share class and any shareholders' agreement actually grant — intent today does not bind behaviour in year two. Whatever is agreed needs to be in a proper instrument, not a WhatsApp understanding.

**Finance & Restructuring.** The standard instrument that achieves exactly what Ed is describing — cheap money now, more value if Barber Republic succeeds, without pricing it today — is a **convertible loan note with a valuation cap and a discount**, not bespoke staged equity. It converts to equity at the next priced round, so Ed's actual stake is set by real evidence rather than a guess made this week. A cap protects his upside if it goes well; a discount rewards him for going in first. Critically, it is an instrument any future institutional investor (Phase Two/Three, per the proposal's own capitalisation path) will recognise instantly — a bespoke equity structure invented this week could complicate or deter that later raise.

---

**Meridian's recommendation for Monday's call.** Do not agree a specific equity number live on the call. Propose the convertible-note structure as the mechanism that delivers what Ed is actually asking for — upside participation, downside protection, no need to price the company today. Get alignment on the concept in the room; take the specific terms (cap level, discount rate, whether any interest applies, maturity/next-round trigger) away to work through properly with a solicitor before anything is signed.

**One thing worth naming plainly in the room:** this decision sets the precedent for how all future Barber Republic capital comes in. A clean, standard instrument now keeps the door open for the VCs Johnny wants to avoid needing later; an unusual, bespoke equity arrangement agreed under time pressure this week could complicate that door being open.
