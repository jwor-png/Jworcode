It converts automatically at the next priced round (the seed round below), at a discount to that round's price, capped so Ed's ownership doesn't get diluted away if the seed round prices high. This is the standard instrument every future institutional investor recognises — a bespoke staged-equity structure invented this week would not be.
**Timeline pressure:** the build proposal is a 12–14 week build starting immediately, paid in monthly thirds from day one. The bridge needs to close before or at the same moment as build kickoff — there is no room for a drawn-out negotiation.
