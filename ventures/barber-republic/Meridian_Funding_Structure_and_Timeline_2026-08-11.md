## THE FOUR-STAGE STRUCTURE

### Stage 0 — Bridge (now, needs to close within days)
**What:** Ed's £50k (or whoever provides it), structured as a **convertible loan note with a valuation cap and a discount**, not priced equity and not a plain loan-for-coupon.
**Why this instrument specifically:** it gives Ed the upside participation he's asking for (equity that "grows with Barber Republic") without pricing the company today, before Phase One has produced any evidence. It converts automatically at the next priced round (the seed round below), at a discount to that round's price, capped so Ed's ownership doesn't get diluted away if the seed round prices high. This is the standard instrument every future institutional investor recognises — a bespoke staged-equity structure invented this week would not be.
**Timeline pressure:** the build proposal is a 12–14 week build starting immediately, paid in monthly thirds from day one. The bridge needs to close before or at the same moment as build kickoff — there is no room for a drawn-out negotiation. Recommend proposing the note structure today, with term-sheet detail (cap level, discount %, maturity/conversion trigger) finalised with lawyers next week, in parallel with the build starting.
**Ed's lawyers, Monday the 10th:** worth having your own solicitor's view on cap/discount ranges ready before that meeting, not reacting to whatever his lawyers propose first.
