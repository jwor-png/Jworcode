# Perimenopause Wellness Venture — Meridian Analysis

Concept for **Johnny Shanahan** (Barber Barber UK / Barber Republic platform): a wellness and aesthetic business for women in perimenopause, built on nutrition, training and medically supervised care. UK primary, Ireland secondary.

**Private and Confidential — decision-support for John. Not legal or medical advice.**

## Bottom line
Qualified yes, but only for a **reframed** version. "Medically supervised peptides" fronted by a non-clinician is **not lawful** in the UK or Ireland (three verified red lines: unlicensed peptides cannot be supplied for human use; prescription medicines cannot be advertised to the public; a non-clinician cannot prescribe or supervise). The defensible business is a nutrition + training + education brand, fronted by a **female clinical lead**, with a **regulated medical partner (Model B JV)** carrying all prescribing. Johnny is the engine and backer, not the face (his audience is male; the customer is not).
