## As pasted (summary of headings and positions; full text held by John)
1. Re-registration as a PLC is Part 20 (s.1284 onwards), not Part 17. s.1291: balance sheet within seven months plus unqualified auditor's report. s.1000: authorised minimum €25,000. s.1112: PLC secretary tests. s.1294: PLC allotment rules apply once the resolution passes. Reported only: one quarter paid up; two directors minimum (s.1088). Re-registering is not listing.
2. No standard "IPO option" clause. s.88: class rights in the constitution with no variation clause need every member's consent, and inserting a variation clause is itself a variation, so draft the A/B variation mechanism and a conversion-on-qualifying-listing provision from day one. A hard no-dilution rule is incompatible with a float; use pre-emption plus defined protections.
3. Drag-along and IPO cooperation usually sit in the shareholders' agreement (bind signatories only; deed of adherence). Put the listing carve-out wherever the restriction lives. Add a Noesis reserved matter on re-registration, listing or any subsidiary share issue.
4. IP owned by Shane personally: whichever entity floats must acquire it by assignment or licence. Decide the destination before any transfer to avoid two transfers and two tax events. s.238: member approval for acquiring a non-cash asset from a director (thresholds: at least €5,000 and above €65,000 or 10% of relevant assets). An assignment into a subsidiary may need a Noesis members' resolution, which could involve Class B holders. Licence (control stays with Shane, weaker for investors) versus assignment (stronger, raises consideration, valuation and tax for Shane).
5. Vehicle choice (flags only): Noesis carries the whole group; a subsidiary creates related-party dealings; a carved-out NewCo takes IP in a single transfer.
6. Drafting today: Asterial clauses protect the company's rights under its IP arrangement with Shane, not ownership of ODIN; Noesis reserved matters cover any IP rights dealing involving Shane; Shane recused, company has its own solicitor, Shane separately advised; John's shareholding and secretary role declared; identify "Carol" first.
