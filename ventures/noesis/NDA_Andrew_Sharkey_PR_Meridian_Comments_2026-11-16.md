# MERIDIAN COMMENTS: ASTERIAL / NOESIS CONFIDENTIALITY AGREEMENT WITH ANDREW SHARKEY (PR ADVISER)

16 Nov 2026. Two-page review copy read in full. Status IN REVIEW. Meridian is not a solicitor. Asterial's solicitors should check before anyone signs. No signature without Shane McCarthy's alignment.

## Overall
Sound, fair and tight for a one-way PR confidentiality agreement. It covers the existence of the discussions, fundraising and investor information, the 14 November call, media and AI-tool restrictions, onward disclosure, return and deletion, and Irish law and courts.

## Immediate comments, in priority order
1. **Fill the placeholders and check the entities.** Both company names, numbers and registered offices are blank. Use the correct legal entity for each, and confirm who signs for each company and with what authority.
2. **The gap since 14 November.** Clause 2 covers pre-signature disclosures, but the obligations "apply from the Effective Date". Anything he did with the call information before signing is not clearly a breach. Make the obligations apply to pre-signature disclosures from the moment of disclosure, and have him confirm in writing that nothing has been shared since the call. Sign promptly.
3. **Who is bound?** He signs personally. If he works through a consultancy, the firm is not a party and cannot be bound by him. Confirm his engagement vehicle and make the firm a party, or require signed joinders from anyone who sees the information.
4. **Check the investor-side confidentiality first.** Disclosing the raise and the investor to a PR adviser may breach any confidentiality undertaking given to the investor, the intermediary or the investor's lawyers. Check before more is shared.
5. **A public announcement of a private raise needs legal clearance.** Announcing a funding round can raise public-offer and financial-promotion issues and breach investor and term-sheet confidentiality (UNVERIFIED). Gate any announcement planning on the signed term sheet, the investor's consent and a solicitor's review.
6. **Add a no-contact clause.** He must not contact the investor, its advisers, the intermediary or journalists about the raise without written consent.

## Smaller points
- Add "and its group companies" so any group company can enforce.
- Add that he has no authority to speak for or bind either company.
- Add a conflicts confirmation: no other client in AI or a competing field.
- Add that damages may be an inadequate remedy, supporting injunctions.
- Add a short personal-data clause if names or contact details are shared.
- The Purpose wording ("proposed funding announcement") reveals the raise. Consider neutral wording.
- The engagement itself needs a separate letter with scope, fees, approval rights over every public statement, and an embargo.

## Fine as drafted
Standard exceptions, reasonable safeguards, the legally protected reporting carve-out, return and deletion with backup allowance, three years plus trade secrets for as long as they qualify, Irish law and courts.
