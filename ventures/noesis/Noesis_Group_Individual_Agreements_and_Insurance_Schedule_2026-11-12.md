# 5. Insurance: directors, officers, executives and the companies

## 5.1 Why insurance carries more weight in Ireland
- Section 235 of the Companies Act 2014 makes void any provision that exempts an officer from liability or indemnifies an officer against it, with exceptions. A company can generally indemnify costs of successfully defending proceedings, and the Act permits the company to buy and maintain insurance for directors and officers. **CONFIRMED (search)** in part. The exact exceptions in s.235(2) to (6) were not retrieved, so a solicitor must confirm them.
- **Result:** an indemnity deed from the company cannot be the whole protection. A properly structured D&O policy is the main protection for the individual.
- A Marsh briefing says Irish rules restrict non-admitted insurers, so Irish companies commonly place D&O in Ireland or the EU. **CONFIRMED (search)**, single source.

## 5.2 Directors and officers (D&O) liability insurance
D&O policies have three insuring agreements: **Side A** (the individual, when the company cannot or will not indemnify), **Side B** (the company, reimbursed when it indemnifies the individual) and **Side C** (the company itself, usually for claims brought alongside its directors). **CONFIRMED (search).**

| Design point | Recommendation |
|:--------------------------|:-----------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Structure** | One group policy with Noesis as the named policyholder and every group company as an insured entity, with **automatic cover for new companies** (check the wording and any time limit). Ask whether the limit is shared across the group. If so, consider a **separate Side A "difference in conditions" layer** for the individuals so they are not left with an exhausted limit. |
| **Who is insured** | Every director and shadow director, the **company secretary**, the interim and any future non-executive Chairman, executives who are not directors (CFO, Chief Operations and Security Officer, Head of Investments), and senior employees. Include past and future officers. |
| **Outside roles** | John's Velocity role, and Noesis nominees on any other board, need **outside directorship cover**. Confirm that John's separate AHL and UHL policies are not relied on for group roles and vice versa. |
| **Investor-appointed director or observer** | Investors usually bring their own cover and ask to be named under the company's policy as well. Agree the order of the two policies and the indemnity terms in the investment documents. |
| **Insured versus insured** | This exclusion bars claims between insureds and is the main trap for venture-backed companies, because investor directors and officers can fall out. Ask for carve-backs for derivative claims, whistleblower claims and claims by a liquidator or insolvency practitioner. **CONFIRMED (search)** on the exclusion and carve-backs. |
| **Entity cover** | Many private-company policies cover the company only when it is sued with an individual. Ask what entity cover is offered and whether it includes regulatory investigations, employment claims and securities-type claims from investors. |
| **Prior acts and retroactive date** | Full prior acts cover back to formation of each company, so earlier conduct (including the period before the raise) is covered. |
| **Defence costs** | Advancement of defence costs before a final outcome, with a clear order of payments that protects the individuals first. |
| **Severability and non-rescindable Side A** | One person's misstatement in the application must not remove cover from the others, and Side A should not be voidable. |
| **Run-off and change of control** | A tail of several years on a sale, a listing or a wind-down. Ask the broker for the options. (Not covered by my search, UNVERIFIED.) |
| **Application and disclosure** | Disclose accurately, including the Saudi raise, the group structure, the ODIN ownership position and any known circumstances. Material non-disclosure can allow the insurer to avoid cover. **UNVERIFIED on the Irish rules.** |
| **Investor requirement** | Investors often make D&O a condition of the investment (**CONFIRMED (search)**), so plan to bind cover before completion. |

## 5.3 Other covers to put in place

| Cover | Protects | Note |
|:----------------------|:----------------------------------|:----------------------------------------|
| **Employment practices liability** | Company and individuals against claims by staff (discrimination, unfair dismissal, harassment). | Often an extension to D&O. Check that it covers the Workplace Relations Commission process. |
| **Professional indemnity or technology errors and omissions** | Claims that AI outputs, software or advice caused loss. | AI exclusions are common. Read them closely. |
| **Cyber** | Breach response, ransom, business interruption, regulatory defence and notification costs. | Link the policy requirements to the information security policy. |
| **Crime or fidelity, including social-engineering fraud** | Employee dishonesty and payment fraud by impersonation. | Important for a company that moves investment funds. |
| **Key-person** | The company if Shane or another critical person dies or is incapacitated. | Investors often require it. |
| **Public liability and property** | Third-party injury and damage, the office and equipment. | Once the office opens. |
| **Employers' liability** | Employee injury claims. | Sources disagree on whether it is compulsory in Ireland. Ask the broker. UNCONFIRMED. |
| **Business travel and personal accident** | Individuals abroad. | For international travel. |
| **IP infringement defence** | Costs of defending infringement claims. | Availability varies. UNVERIFIED. |

## 5.4 Insurance and the individual agreements
- Each director letter and executive agreement should say that the company will **maintain D&O cover** at an agreed level, supply a copy of the policy, give notice of changes, and keep **run-off cover** for a stated period after the person leaves.
- The **indemnity deed** should be drafted within s.235 and should sit behind the insurance, not in place of it.
- Cover should be **bound before** the Asterial completion and ideally before the group begins to trade or hire.
