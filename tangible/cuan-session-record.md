## 5. Governance note

A claim in the first draft did not survive John's own challenge and was corrected.

The draft stated that a €2.5m SuperValu redevelopment in Kilmallock included repairs to the medieval town wall. That was a conflation of two unrelated sources: a reported Liam Ryan investment scheduled for 2026, and a separate, earlier ACP Group conservation project which conserved three medieval arches and involved archaeology by Frank Coyne of Aegis Archaeology during a previous rebuild of the store.

Corrected in both deliverables. The €2.5m figure is now carried as "understood to be", because neither source page could be opened directly and the publication date could not be confirmed. The arch conservation is now stated separately as an earlier project.

This is the verification standard in `governance.md` working as intended, but it was caught by John rather than at source. Worth noting as a pattern: a construction firm's projects page describes completed work, and legacy URL formats on local news sites signal old stories. Both should trigger a date check before a claim is used.
