# AI Governance Readiness Checklist
## Ambrion | AI Driven, Human Led

A practical self-assessment for Irish business leaders. Use this to understand where your organisation stands today, where the gaps are, and what to prioritise before the EU AI Act obligations tighten.

---

## How to use this

Work through each section. Mark each item:
- **Yes** — this is in place and working
- **Partial** — this exists but is incomplete or informal
- **No** — this is not yet in place
- **Don't know** — you are not certain

Be honest. A gap is not a failure — it is a priority. The value of this checklist is in what it surfaces, not what it confirms.
