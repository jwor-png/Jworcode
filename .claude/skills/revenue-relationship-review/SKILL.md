This skill follows `cuan/skills/SHARED_STANDARDS.md` and the common
structure in `cuan/skills/_TEMPLATE.md`. Read `SHARED_STANDARDS.md`
before producing any output under this skill.

## 1. Purpose and trigger
Turns scattered updates across John's revenue streams (AHL, UHL,
Ambrion AI, Barber Republic, Julianstown, Velocity AI, Meridian's
commercial threads, Legacy Financial Matters, and any other live
stream) into one prioritised, actionable view: what's actually moving,
what's stalled, what fees are due, and what the single next action is
on each. Trigger weekly, or on request for a consolidated revenue
picture.

## 2. Required inputs
- Current state of `board.md` and `revenue_streams.md` (or equivalent
  live tracker), plus any fresh updates John has given in this
  session.
- For each item: what was last confirmed to have happened, and on what
  date.
- Where a stream's status is genuinely unclear or contradictory across
  sources, that must be surfaced, not silently resolved by picking one
  version.

## 3. Workflow
1. List every live revenue stream/opportunity, not just the ones that
   feel most active — a stalled item is still worth surfacing if money
   or a relationship is sitting untouched.
2. For each, extract: current status, the most recent dated
   development, any fee/proposal/payment outstanding, and who (if
   anyone) is waiting on whom.
3. Distinguish clearly between:
   - **Live and moving** — has had confirmed forward motion recently.
   - **Stalled** — no confirmed movement in a defined period, someone
     is waiting on a reply or action.
   - **Awaiting John's decision** — the next step depends on John
     specifically, not a counterparty.
   - **Thin/unclear** — genuinely too little information to assess
     properly (state this plainly rather than inventing a status).
4. Prioritise by: commercial value (real or reasonably estimated, not
   invented), urgency (a real deadline beats a vague one), and strength
   of evidence (a confirmed fact beats an assumption every time,
   regardless of how much money is involved).
5. For every item, state one concrete next action — not "follow up,"
   but who does what, by when, based on what's actually known.
6. Flag explicitly anything that cannot be prioritised properly because
   the underlying information is contradictory or missing (e.g. two
   different figures for the same fee, or an item referenced only as
   "[ASK]" with no detail) — do not guess a priority for these, name
   the gap instead.

## 4. Output format
A single ranked list (or ranked-within-category list), each item
showing: status tag, last confirmed development with date, value/
urgency basis for its ranking, and the specific next action. Genuinely
unclear items go in their own clearly marked section rather than being
force-ranked alongside confirmed ones.

## 5. Quality checks
- No revenue figure, fee amount, or deadline appears in the output
  unless it traces to something actually stated or documented.
- Every "next action" names a real, specific step, not a vague
  placeholder like "keep monitoring."
- Contradictory information across sources is shown, not silently
  resolved.
- Genuinely thin/unclear items are labelled as such, not given a false
  sense of completeness by being ranked as if they were well
  understood.

## 6. Handling of missing information
If a stream's current status can't be determined from what's
available, say so and ask, rather than carrying forward a stale status
as if it were current, or inventing plausible-sounding progress.

## 7. Completion criteria
Done when John can look at the output and know, for every live
revenue stream, exactly where it stands, what's overdue, and what the
next concrete action is — with anything genuinely uncertain flagged as
such rather than smoothed into false clarity.
