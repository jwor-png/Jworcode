# "New EU charge will disappoint online shoppers", Caitriona Redmond, Consumer Watch, Irish Examiner (John's photo, 17 Nov 2026; edition date not shown). Consumer column, figures as reported, UNVERIFIED against Revenue.

- **Before July 2026:** parcels under 150 euro (including shipping) from outside the EU had no customs charge (the "de minimis exemption"). From 1 July 2026 a customs charge of 3.69 euro (3 plus VAT) per unique item in a low-value parcel applied; some retailers charge it at checkout, others do not.
- **Parcels arriving without the customs charge already paid:** a customs administration fee by the shipping partner (An Post: 6.95 euro per parcel, payable before delivery).
- **From 1 November 2026:** a new EU handling charge of 2 euro per distinct item in a parcel, not refundable even for a faulty item. Customs charge on items into Ireland now 6.15 euro per item including VAT (an increase of 18.45 euro on the clothing-parcel example since June). The handling charge also applies where charges were not paid at checkout; An Post delivery in that case costs 25.40 euro more than last year (example). Gifts from friends and family, samples and subscription freebies are not exempt.
- **UK:** "outside the EU" includes the UK. Small parcels from Northern Ireland are generally exempt; anything shipped from Great Britain incurs customs and handling charges from November.
- **Advice (Redmond):** buy directly from Irish or EU retailers; check the website's delivery and returns policy for where the shipping hub is; if buying outside the EU, check whether customs and handling charges are paid at checkout, and calculate per unique item (6.15 euro per item); be prepared for charges on items ordered now but arriving from 1 November; beware phishing texts and emails from "shipping handlers" (ask for a tracking number). Also: Amazon Family sharing in Ireland.
