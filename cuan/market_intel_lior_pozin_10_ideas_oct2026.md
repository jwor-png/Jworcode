# "10 startup ideas" — Lior Pozin (second list)

Source: Instagram carousel, verified account "lior" (Lior Pozin) — same author as the earlier "10 Startups That Don't Exist Yet" list logged 2 Oct 2026 (`market_intel_startup_ideas_lior_oct2026.md`). UNVERIFIED — an individual's opinion/idea list, not market-researched.

## What it says

1. **AI Bookkeeper** — connects to your bank and does your entire bookkeeping for you, instead of selling you software you have to learn.
2. **Weeding Robots** — field robots that find and remove weeds one by one, so farmers stop spraying chemicals over everything.
3. **AI Agent Recorder** — records everything an AI agent does, so when it spends money or makes a mistake you see exactly what happened.
4. **AI Travel Agent** — plans your whole trip, books it, and rebooks you when flights get cancelled; charges an app price, not a commission.
5. **Trades Hiring** — a simple way for construction and repair companies to find, check, and hire skilled workers fast.
6. **Clinic No-Show Fix** — predicts which patients will skip their appointment and fills the empty slot before it happens.
7. **AI Agent Chips** — chips designed for AI agents that loop and branch, not just chips built for simple question-and-answer AI.
8. **AI Compliance Aide** — tells European companies exactly what to do to follow the new AI laws and avoid heavy fines.
9. **Fast Prototyping** — a service that turns a product idea into a real manufactured sample in days instead of weeks.
10. **Advisor for Savers** — a real financial planner for people with less than $100k, the group most wealth managers ignore.
