# "EU's AI act 'ahead of curve', but help needed" — McGrath, 14 Oct 2026

**The event:** the International AI Summit, Dublin, 14 Oct 2026, held at
the RDS as part of Ireland's EU Council Presidency — around 1,500
delegates expected. The summit concluded with a keynote address from
**Michael McGrath**, Ireland's EU Commissioner.

**The core claim:** McGrath said the EU is "ahead of the curve" in
regulating AI through the AI Act, but cannot manage this alone, since
the technology crosses borders — help/cooperation is needed beyond the
EU's own regulatory reach. Per a secondary account (an opinion blog,
treat with some caution — not independently verified against the full
RTÉ text, which could not be fetched directly), he argued the AI Act
should help Europe build trust in AI so it can thrive and innovate,
framing regulation as an enabler of adoption rather than a brake on it.

**Counterpoint, same event window:** the Communications Workers' Union
ran its own "counter summit" the same week, to highlight AI's impact
on jobs — a live tension between the official pro-regulation/
pro-adoption framing and union concern about displacement.
