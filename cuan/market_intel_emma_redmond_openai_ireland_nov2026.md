# Eoin O'Hara: "Business Leader of the Month: Emma Redmond, OpenAI Ireland"

Source: Business Post, 1 Nov 2026 (Business Leader of the Month, with Grant Thornton). Captured by photograph from John, 1 Nov 2026.

## What it says

- Emma Redmond, associate general counsel and head of OpenAI Ireland, joined OpenAI in 2023 as its first employee in Ireland, after senior positions at Stripe, LinkedIn and Ancestry. She has overseen its rapid expansion, culminating in plans (announced in July) to create **200 additional jobs** and establish an **88,000 sq ft European headquarters in Dublin's Docklands**, taking the Irish workforce from about **160 to 360 over two years**.
- More than **one million people in Ireland** now use ChatGPT, according to the company.
- Redmond was a member of the government's **AI Advisory Council** and is an adjunct associate professor of law at University College Dublin; her career spans technology, privacy and regulation.
- The paper frames OpenAI's expansion as a vote of confidence in Ireland's technology sector at a time of intensifying international competition for AI investment.
