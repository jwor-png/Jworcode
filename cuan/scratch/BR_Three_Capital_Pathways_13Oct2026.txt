Exit discipline. New primary funding belongs to the company. Personal liquidity requires a separately agreed secondary sale or acquisition. Actual proceeds depend on
debt, preferences, consent rights, deal structure and tax. Raising again should improve expected net proceeds enough to justify dilution, time and execution risk. [5]


Market calibration. Carta’s US Q2 2026 reference medians are 19.4% dilution at seed and 18.7% at A; they do not price this UK business. UK seed deal count fell 27% in
2025. Fresha’s 2026 growth investment and Booksy’s 2021 Genbook purchase support the adjacent-sector logic. Their scale and business models differ from Barber Republic.
[1–4]

Before choosing the raise. Confirm a monthly cash plan, current ownership and option commitments, pricing and contribution assumptions, and Neil’s preferred exit horizon
and minimum cash outcome. The scenarios model zero opening cash and no operating receipts when sizing pre-seed spending capacity.


Dilution hurdle. After a round selling 20%, comparable distributable exit proceeds must rise 25% just to preserve an existing holder’s gross payout. This arithmetic excludes
preferences, debt, tax, elapsed time and changes in execution risk.
