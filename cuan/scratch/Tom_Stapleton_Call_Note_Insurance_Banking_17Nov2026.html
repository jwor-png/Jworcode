2. The deposit guarantee scheme, and what it does not do

  • Deposit guarantee: EUR 100,000 per depositor per bank (CONFIRMED (search)). A large investment receipt far exceeds it in any one bank.
  • So: a board treasury policy with a limit per bank, accounts at both banks, reserves placed under the policy. Whether temporary higher cover applies to investment receipts: UNVERIFIED, ask each bank.
  • It protects deposits if a bank fails. It does not cover fraud, error or an impersonation payment. That needs crime and cyber insurance plus payment controls.
