6 Market price and disputes
Protected Market Price is the actual interest’s proportionate share of whole-company equity market value. Its valuation date is the Holder’s written transfer offer for a voluntary sale, or the event or purchase notice for a compulsory sale, before protection ends. Reflect assets, IP, liabilities, funding, prospects and class rights. Apply no minority, illiquidity, forced-sale or personal-performance discount. Business performance may affect Company value.
The price shall be at least any higher genuine written cash offer from an independent, funded buyer for the same interest on materially equivalent terms. An offer does not override transfer restrictions. Unearned interests are not valued as already earned; enforceable rights are valued on their actual terms. The valuer shall adjust for distributions between valuation and completion to prevent double counting.
The parties have ten business days to agree value or jointly appoint an independent accountant or corporate-finance professional experienced in private-company valuations. A jointly appointed expert shall report within twenty business days. Each party may give evidence and comment on the other’s material submissions.
If no joint appointment is agreed, each shall appoint a qualified independent valuer within ten further business days. Reports are due within twenty business days of appointment. Two timely reports determine value at their arithmetic mean; a sole timely report determines value alone. Valuers must apply identical instructions. Required information shall be supplied promptly under confidentiality.
The written valuation binds the parties except for fraud or manifest error. A challenge must identify the error within ten business days of receipt, for correction or explanation within ten further business days. Legal disputes and unresolved errors may be referred to the Irish courts. No interest is transferred before the price is final and paid.
The parties bear equal reasonable joint valuation costs, or their own nominee’s costs. If both valuers fail to report, the first-refusal right expires for that sale. Any sale still needs to satisfy clause 5. Price disputes confer no general governance right.
