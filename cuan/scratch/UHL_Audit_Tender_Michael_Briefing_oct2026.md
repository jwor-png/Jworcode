May 2027 AGM — legal context, not just a target date:

The Chairman has directed a May 2027 AGM. Worth knowing going into the
Teams meetings: this is UHL's own commercial decision, not a statutory
requirement — company law only requires the AGM within 15 months of
the last one, which given our 30 Sept 2026 AGM gives a legal outer
limit of end of December 2027. May is the right target for us
commercially, but it means we're holding each firm to a date by
contract, not by law, so we need it committed to explicitly, not
assumed.

This also surfaces a real mechanical question for the Committee to
settle before we can compare the four fairly:

- **If we change auditor for FY2026**, Forvis Mazars has to resign and
  the board appoints a replacement to fill the vacancy — this needs
  deciding this month to fit any of the four firms' interim
  timetables, and a new firm takes on real first-year risk (opening
  balances, professional clearance, the two previously-unaudited
  subsidiaries BDO flagged) against a May deadline.
- **If we change auditor from FY2027** (Mazars completes FY2026, new
  firm appointed at the May 2027 AGM itself), that's the cleaner
  route for hitting May reliably, but it requires extended notice —
  at least 28 days to the company, with members notified at least 21
  days before the meeting, so paperwork needs to go out by late April
  2027 if we go this way.

Worth putting to the Committee directly: which route are we actually
planning for, since it changes what we're asking the four firms to
commit to.
