1. The need in one paragraph

Vinny runs Sales Process Advisors and sells LSOS (Lean Sales Operating System): a method for how an SME manages its sales (priorities, pipeline rules, weekly reviews, forecast, coaching), delivered as a paid seven-day Kickstart, a roadmap and then improvement work. He wants to turn it into an AI-enabled sales management service for SMEs: the method stays his; AI agents do the routine work inside it (research, qualification checks, meeting preparation, pipeline alerts, reporting). He is not building the AI. He needs a technical partner to design, build, run and secure it, and to share a monthly recurring fee. So: yes, an AI-driven lean sales system, with him owning the method and the client, and the AI partner owning the technology. Confirm that is how he sees it.
