UHL draft 2027 budget: review checklist

For John, Tue 17 Nov 2026, as chair of the Audit and Risk Committee. A general checklist: Cuan has not seen the draft budget, so tick against the document. Market figures are from the Grafton report, as reported by the newspaper and unverified (market_intel_grafton_trading_update_nov2026.md).

1. Market assumptions

☐ Irish growth assumption: how does it compare with Grafton's like-for-like Irish growth (4.7pc over 10 months, 6.5pc over the last four)? What is behind UHL's figure?

☐ Interest rates and energy costs: stated assumptions and a sensitivity (Grafton says both still pressure household budgets).

☐ Housing and infrastructure exposure: how much of member demand depends on it? Grafton's UK was down 5.2pc: any downside case?

☐ Digital ordering: does the plan reflect the shift online (CSO: about 46pc of goods and services ordered or delivered online in 2024)? Portal and ordering changes costed?

2. Revenue, margin and rebates

☐ Sales by main category and by member group; basis of each growth rate; price-inflation versus volume split.

☐ Gross margin by category and supplier terms; supplier price increases already notified (for example 1 Dec increases circulated to members).

☐ Member rebates: method, accrual, timing, and consistency with this year.

3. Costs

☐ Payroll, pensions and employer costs; headcount plan; new statutory costs.

☐ Energy, premises, logistics and fuel; insurance renewals.

☐ Professional fees: audit fee (Mazars appointed 12 Nov; confirm the figure), legal, advisers; directors' travelling expenses.

☐ One-off items and capital spend: listed separately, with approval route.

4. Cash, funding and risk

☐ Monthly cash flow, working capital and debtor days; headroom and any covenants.

☐ Bad-debt and credit-limit assumptions; concentration in large members or suppliers.

☐ Scenarios: base, downside, upside, with the triggers for action.

☐ Risk register link: top risks, owners, insurance cover (D&O limit noted as unresolved), cyber and payment fraud controls.

5. Governance, as chair of Audit and Risk

☐ Who prepared and reviewed the draft; what changed from the 2026 budget and from actuals to date.

☐ Approval timetable (board Wed 25 Nov 10:00; conference Thu 3 Dec); what the committee must recommend.

☐ Related-party items and conflicts declared; Keith Cronin's committee invite.

Questions to put to the executive: What are the three assumptions that move the result most? What would we do if sales are 5pc below plan by March? Where is cash tightest in the year? What is not in this budget that we should expect?
