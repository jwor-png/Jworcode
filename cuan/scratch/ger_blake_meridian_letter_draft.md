**What we actually deliver — seven products**

1. **Decision Brief** — one high-consequence decision, answered in five working days. Up to three domains, an 8-15 page brief, a 45-minute call. Built to sit inside a managing director's own signing authority, so a client can say yes without a board paper.

2. **Executive Intelligence Review** — our flagship. The whole business interrogated across all seven domains in ten working days: where it's exposed, where value is being left on the table, what the board should do about it. A consolidated brief, the five key risks ranked, sequenced actions, and a 90-minute presentation.

3. **Transaction Intelligence** — due diligence grade analysis before a client signs, structured the way the other side's own adviser will attack it. Valuation and dilution modelling, instrument analysis, a term-by-term negotiating position, a read on the counterparty.

4. **Intelligence Partner (retained)** — a standing seat at the decision table across a year rather than a one-off project, in three tiers from quarterly briefs through to direct, ongoing access to me.

5. **AI Equity Structuring** — AI capability contributed to a business in exchange for equity, structured so it actually survives a later funding round rather than being unwound.

6. **AI Dependency and Resilience Review** — mapping a business's real dependence on its AI and technology suppliers, the contractual and regulatory position on each, and what the realistic adverse cases would actually cost — before it happens, not after.

7. **Board Paper Review** — a board's own papers read in advance of the meeting, with the unanswered questions handed back before the room does.
