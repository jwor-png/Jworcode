**What Meridian actually is**

Meridian helps business owners, directors, investors and leadership teams make better decisions through structured executive intelligence. Every engagement is personally led by me, combining more than forty years of commercial experience across law, finance, property, business development, restructuring and, more recently, applied AI, with Meridian's own Executive Intelligence Framework. **What we actually deliver**

**1. Decision Brief**
One decision. Five days. A written position you can take to your board.
Answers one specific, high-consequence question with a yes or no at the end of it. Up to three domains, five working days, an 8 to 15 page brief and a 45-minute call. It is the entry product and the door into everything else, built to sit inside a managing director's own signing authority so a client can say yes without a board paper.

**2. Executive Intelligence Review**
Your whole situation, interrogated from seven senior angles at once, in ten days.
Answers not one question but a position: where the business is exposed, where value is being left on the table, and what the board should do. All seven domains, ten working days, a consolidated brief, the five key risks ranked, sequenced actions, the questions the board must answer before committing, and a 90-minute presentation. It is the flagship, for the business that suspects there is a problem it has not yet named.

**3. Transaction Intelligence**
Due diligence grade analysis before you sign, structured the way the other side's adviser will attack it.
Answers whether to do the deal, at what value, on what instrument, and what you will regret in eighteen months. Ten to fifteen working days. Valuation and dilution modelling, instrument analysis, a term by term negotiating position, a read on the counterparty, and availability through the negotiation. It does not replace the solicitor or accountant. It means the client walks in already knowing their position.

**4. Intelligence Partner (retained)**
A standing seat at the decision table, not a project.
Answers everything, as it arises, across a year, in three tiers: Board Seat, Intelligence Partner, and Chair's Counsel. This is the rung that makes Meridian a business rather than a series of engagements, because a retained relationship compounds and removes the need to win each client from scratch.

**5. AI Equity Structuring**
AI capability contributed for equity, structured so it survives a funding round.
Answers how AI capability can be contributed to a business in exchange for a shareholding in a form a later investor will accept rather than unwind. Bespoke scope and timing. It is the slowest to convert and hardest to sell cold, but it is the line that turns Meridian from a fee business into an asset business.

**6. AI Dependency and Resilience Review**
What happens to your business if your AI stack fails, is withdrawn, or turns adversarial — mapped and costed before it happens, not after.
Maps a business's real dependence on its AI and supplier technology, the contractual and regulatory position on each, and what the realistic adverse cases would actually cost.

**7. Board Paper Review**
The papers for the next board meeting, read beforehand, with the unanswered questions handed back before the room does.
A bounded, single-meeting-scope review — faster turnaround than a Decision Brief, built for a board or chair who wants the hard questions surfaced before they're asked in the room.
