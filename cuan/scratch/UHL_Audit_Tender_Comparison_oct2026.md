## Like-for-like comparison traps

1. **Fitzgerald Power's headline saving is real but the firm is
   materially smaller** (~100 people vs Mazars' 950+/BDO's/RSM's
   international scale) — the cheaper price likely reflects genuinely
   lower overhead and a boutique/SME-audit specialism, not
   necessarily reduced service, but bench depth for a complex,
   multi-entity, cross-border group is the fair question to test
   directly rather than infer either way.
2. **BDO's tax-fee discrepancy (€16,250 vs €17,500+5%) needs
   resolving before BDO's total can be trusted at face value** — could
   go either direction.
3. **RSM's headline total already includes their most generous
   "no-charge for ad-hoc advice" framing**, which is a genuine value
   point but not separately costed, making true cost-of-service
   comparison harder than the raw totals suggest.
4. **Mazars' iXBRL exclusion and RSM's onboarding fee are both
   realistic extras that would apply on top of any multi-year total**
   — worth asking both firms to quote an all-in figure rather than
   comparing headline totals as if they were final.
5. **BDO's explicit first-year "unaudited opening balance" flag on
   two entities is actually useful honesty, not a weakness** — but
   it's unclear whether the stated €72,500 audit fee already reflects
   that extra first-year work or whether it will be billed as a
   change/overrun once work starts. The other three proposals are
   silent on this point rather than explicitly addressing it, which
   isn't necessarily better — it may just mean it isn't priced in and
   would surface as a surprise later.

## What's missing from Dave's criteria, worth weighing separately

- **Audit rotation/independence governance** — Mazars is the only one
  that proactively addresses it (partner rotation already built into
  their proposal). Given BDO's own current FRC scrutiny thread
  (logged separately, Man City/audit-quality piece) and the fact BDO
  already audits AHL, worth asking BDO directly about their own
  rotation policy for UHL specifically, not assuming parity with
  Mazars on this point.
- **Group consolidation experience specifically** — all four quote
  consolidation fees, but only Mazars and RSM explicitly reference
  multi-entity/complex-group audit experience in their own words;
  worth asking Fitzgerald Power and BDO to speak to this directly
  given UHL's post-Expert-Hardware group structure.
- **Continuity through an auditor change** — none of the three
  challengers (Fitzgerald Power, BDO, RSM) addressed handover risk
  from the incumbent (Mazars) in what was extracted; worth asking
  directly, since a change this year would be a first-year transition
  regardless of firm chosen.

## Bottom line for the Audit & Risk Committee discussion

Not a simple lowest-price decision — per the Committee's own
assessment-criteria document: *"A proposal should not be selected
solely because it has the lowest fee... independence and audit
quality should be treated as minimum requirements rather than areas
that can be offset by a low price."* Fitzgerald Power's saving
(~€30-36k/year vs the other three) is large enough to deserve serious
consideration, but given UHL's scale (post-Expert-Hardware, ~200
stores, €500m+ turnover, NI exposure, ongoing group consolidation
complexity), the honest next step is testing Fitzgerald Power's depth
directly in the Teams presentation rather than assuming either way,
and resolving BDO's tax-fee inconsistency before including BDO's
total in any like-for-like ranking.
