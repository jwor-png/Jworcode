2. Suggested wording

Reg 5.2: accept Shane's definition of "group" and "subsidiary" (section 7) as drafted.

13.2 (Shane's 19 Nov wording) For the purposes of section 228(1)(d) and (f) of the Act, a director who holds A Ordinary Shares may hold interests in, and carry on, any business or venture outside the group, and is released from his or her duties to the Company to the extent necessary to do so. Suggested add: "This does not release any other duty under the Act, including disclosure under section 231, or the duty to keep the Company's confidential information confidential."

13.3 (Shane's wording) Nothing in this Regulation permits the use of any property, confidential information or intellectual property of the Company or any member of the group outside the group without the Company's agreement. Suggested change: "...without the prior written agreement of the Company given by directors other than the director concerned (and, for intellectual property, A Consent under Regulation 28)."

Optional (investor comfort): 13.4 The director shall give the directors written notice of any Outside Venture that competes with an active trade of a member of the group, and a register of such notices shall be kept by the secretary.

28.1 As Shane's wording, plus (d) allot or issue any share in a subsidiary, or any right to acquire one, other than to the Company, except as approved by A Consent.

28.4 (new) This Regulation does not require A Consent for a non-exclusive licence granted to a customer in the ordinary course of business on the group's standard terms.

28.5 (new) The Company shall procure that the constitution of each subsidiary contains provisions equivalent to this Regulation, with the Company (acting by A Consent) as the consenting party. For Asterial this must be in force before the IP transfer.
