2. Suggested wording

Reg 5.2: accept Shane's definition of "group" and "subsidiary" (section 7) as drafted.

13.2 For the purposes of section 228(1)(d) and (f) of the Act, a director who holds A Ordinary Shares (or is designated by A Consent) may hold interests in and carry on any business or venture outside the group (an "Outside Venture") listed in Schedule 1 or disclosed under 13.3, without breach of those provisions. This does not release the director from any other duty under the Act, including disclosure under section 231.

13.3 Where an Outside Venture is (a business offering products or services to the same customers as, or using the same technology as, an active trade of a member of the group), the director shall disclose it in writing to the directors. The directors other than the disclosing director (or, if there are none, an independent person appointed with A Consent and approved by the chairman) may, by written notice within 30 days after disclosure, require the director to sell or license the Outside Venture to the Company on arm's-length terms, the price being set by an independent valuer if not agreed within 14 days. If no notice is given in time, the Outside Venture is released from this Regulation 13.3. A venture not disclosed is not released.

13.4 As Shane's wording. Keep unchanged.

28.1 As Shane's wording, plus (d) allot or issue any share in a subsidiary, or any right to acquire one, other than to the Company, except as approved by A Consent.

28.5 (new) The Company shall procure that the constitution of each subsidiary contains provisions equivalent to this Regulation, with the Company (acting by A Consent) as the consenting party. For Asterial this must be in force before the IP transfer.
