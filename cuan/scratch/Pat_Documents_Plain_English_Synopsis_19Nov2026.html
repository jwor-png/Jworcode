1. Noesis: what Pat sent and why

  • New constitution. Four types of share. A shares carry all the votes (Shane). B, C and D shares have no vote but get the same dividends and the same share of the proceeds. D is for employees. No limit on how many shares can be issued, though any new issue needs A holder consent. If someone buys control by buying A shares, they must offer everyone else the same price (14 days to accept).
    Why: the old rulebook had one type of share and a limit of 100,000. An investor, employees and Diarmuid cannot be given different rights unless the rulebook allows different types.
  • Signing pack. Shane signs, as sole director and sole member, to adopt the new rules and relabel his 100 ordinary shares as A shares (no new shares). You, as secretary, record it, update the registers, seal the new share certificate with Shane, and sign the filing at the Companies Registration Office within 15 days.
    Why: the law requires these steps and records so the change is valid and visible to the public register.

2. Asterial: what Pat sent and why
