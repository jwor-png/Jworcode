# Opportunities Brief: template (from the Stargas brief, anonymised)

Status: template only. Built 5 Nov 2026 from the structure of the Meridian Stargas Opportunities Brief (footer date 12 Aug 2026). Nothing in the original is reused as fact. Every figure in a live brief must be sourced and checked for that company.

Before use:
- Meridian is not incorporated. Decide the issuing name (Meridian Intelligence as a working brand, or Velocity AI) with Shane first. No fee, contract or commitment in the brief.
- No claims like "nobody else has this". Say what was done and how it was checked.
- Do not name UHL or any client in a brief without their consent.
- John or Cuan sends it. Cuan puts any email in Gmail drafts.

---

## Cover
[COMPANY NAME]
Opportunities in [core business], and available grant aid
Prepared for [NAME, ROLE]. Private and confidential. [DATE]

## The opportunity (3 to 4 lines)
Who the company is: independent or not, size, location, how it sells, years trading (source for each).
The two or three separate questions this brief covers, kept distinct.

## Part One: the owner's own ambition (use the owner's words)
Quote the ambition once. Then 2 to 4 numbered items:
1. The claim and the test: what is thin or crowded in the field, with the evidence found (name the sources).
2. The new revenue line this could open.
3. Grant-funded route, if one fits (see grant section).
For each item end with the one open question worth settling first.

## Part Two: core business opportunities
Up to five, ranked by evidence strength and ease of near-term action. For each:
- The gap or opening, in one sentence.
- Evidence (figure and source) and what the larger competitors already do.
- Effort: low, medium or high, and the first practical step.
- Honest flag where evidence is weak or unconfirmed (say "not confirmed" and what would confirm it).

## Grant aid intelligence
Only schemes confirmed current at the date of the brief, each with: scheme name, who it suits, what it funds, the cap and percentage, the deadline or window, and the source link. Check at least:
- Local Enterprise Office (Business Expansion Grant and related supports).
- Enterprise Ireland (for example capital investment and decarbonisation supports).
- SEAI (energy efficiency supports).
- Invest NI or UK schemes only if NI or UK expansion is real.
- Skillnets and training supports where AI or skills training is relevant.
Recommended path: one near-term route first, a larger route second.
(In the Stargas example the quoted caps were a 50% LEO grant capped at 80,000 euro, Enterprise Ireland up to 1m and SEAI EXEED up to 3m; these are examples and were not verified by Cuan. Re-verify before reuse.)

## Overall read (3 lines)
Plain summary: what is live, what is the sharpest near-term win, the one fact to settle first.

## Footer
[Issuer]. [Company]. Topic. Date. Private and confidential. Prepared for [name].
All figures sourced; list sources on a final page. Planning figures are not offers or advice.

# Template v2 (5 Nov 2026, evening): adding the UHL proposal structure

Source: Shane McCarthy's "AI at UHL: the year ahead" (UHL AI 2027.pdf), emailed to Paul Candon 4 Nov 2026 22:52, for the 2:45 meeting on 5 Nov. Cuan read it in full on 5 Nov evening (Drive copy). Two different documents, used at two different stages:

## Stage 1: opening brief for a prospect (no history yet)
Use the Stargas structure above for the company-specific research, plus this lead-in from the UHL proposal:
1. What is changing and how fast (generic, 1 paragraph).
2. What the last four months have shown: 3 or 4 dated, sourced examples, chosen to fit that sector (the UHL paper used an industrial-supplies distributor breach, an agent test breakout, the PHP flaws, and a fresh disclosure). Cite each source and date; Cuan re-checks each before reuse.
3. Three areas to have covered: AI capability and continuous learning; defensibility, systems and resilience; cybersecurity.
4. The hook question: "A new model lands at five o'clock, or something happens inside the business at five. What happens at 5:01?"
5. The company-specific opportunities and grant aid (Stargas sections).
No retainer ask and no fee at this stage.

## Stage 2: retainer proposal (only once there is a track record)
Follows the UHL paper: journey so far (training by role, readiness profile, orchestrations on the client's own accounts, read and draft only, second-model check); where it goes next; a monthly retainer in five parts (key people, core systems, ahead of the models, continuous learning, embedding AI with governance); value measured as it comes through; sources; prepared for [name] by [issuer], confidential.
Notes: the UHL paper states no fee. Do not use Stage 2 for a prospect with no live work.
