Asterial: independent chair and two non-executive directors, fee guide

For John only, Wed 18 Nov 2026. Proposal: an independent chair, one industry-specific NED, one commercial NED; Dublin-based; about 6 board meetings a year. Published figures are tagged (S) sourced. Planning ranges are Cuan's own estimates (E), not from a published source, to be tested with the IoD Ireland survey or an adviser. Not tax, legal or HR advice. Nothing is agreed without Shane.

1. What published sources say (S)

• IoD Ireland's 2025/26 Board Director Remuneration and Governance Survey covers 230+ directors and 385 paid directorships across private and listed boards, including by company stage; the figures sit behind membership. Reported in the Irish Times (13 Nov 2025): NED median in financial services firms 60,000 to 70,000 euro; listed non-financial companies 55,000 to 97,000 euro, average just above 70,000.

• Spencer Stuart's Irish board index (listed company disclosures): average NED fee about 70,943 euro (2023); average chair fee 292,059 euro in 2020, trending down. These are listed-company levels and are not a guide for an early-stage private company.

• IoD (UK) guidance: there is no fixed rate; pay should reflect company size, time commitment, responsibilities and the individual's experience; committee work adds to it. Annual fees (not salary) are usual for a part-time role.

3. Expenses and other costs (E)

• Expenses: Dublin-based directors keep travel low: about 500 to 1,500 euro each a year (public transport, mileage, parking, meals); more if they attend investor meetings abroad. Reimburse documented costs within an agreed policy.

• Other costs: D&O cover extended to each director (premium effect unknown: ask Marsh); letters of appointment and indemnity (solicitor); induction; board papers and minutes (secretary time); search or recruitment fees if an adviser finds them (often a share of first-year fees: UNVERIFIED); tax and employer PRSI treatment of directors' fees to confirm with Pat. Allow roughly 5,000 to 15,000 euro a year over the fees.

• Indicative total a year: about 65,000 to 125,000 euro for the three, before any equity.
