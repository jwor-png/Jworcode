BARBER REPUBLIC - PHASE ONE MVP
DEVELOPMENT AGREEMENT
17 AUGUST 2026

This Agreement is between Mainly Velocity Limited, Company No. 804908, trading as VelocityAI ("Velocity"),
and BarberBarber Limited, 53 King Street, Manchester M2 4LQ, United Kingdom ("BarberBarber"). It records
the revised commercial arrangement for the Barber Republic Phase One MVP build and is to be read together
with the Barber Republic Build Scope Proposal.

1. Phase One Scope
Velocity will undertake the Phase One MVP development programme over the anticipated 12-14 week build
and delivery period set out in the Barber Republic Build Scope Proposal. The intended deliverables include
barber and shop onboarding and verification; professional barber tiering; barber availability and shop chair
requirements; intelligent and explainable matching; booking and confirmation; payment functionality through
an appropriate regulated provider; mutual ratings and trust records; operator and administration functionality;
testing, refinement and preparation for pilot deployment. Technical architecture, security, data handling and
governance remain integral to the build.

2. Revised Payment Programme
The parties have agreed a revised three-payment structure for the 12-14 week Phase One build and delivery
period. £15,000 is payable before commencement of the first development period. £15,000 is payable before
commencement of the second development period, anticipated to begin at approximately Week 5. £25,000 is
payable before commencement of the third development period, anticipated to begin at approximately Week
9. Total Phase One programme provision: £55,000. The payment points operate within the overall 12-14 week
build timetable and this revised payment schedule supersedes the original payment timing in the Build Scope.

3. Payment Before Work Commences
Each payment must be received before Velocity commences work for the relevant development period. If a
payment is delayed, Velocity will not be required to commence the corresponding development period until
payment has been received. Any such delay may result in a corresponding extension of the anticipated 12-14
week project delivery timeline.

4. What the £55,000 Covers
The £55,000 programme provision comprises the core Phase One development programme, agreed
additional technology, compute or tooling requirements where required, and provision for the planned UK
travel and associated expenses connected with delivery of Phase One.

5. Commitment to Complete
The initial £15,000 is the commencement payment for the overall Phase One programme and is not intended
as a standalone four-week development engagement. Velocity will commit the required development
resources on the basis that BarberBarber provides an assurance acceptable to Velocity that the subsequent
Phase One payments will be made when due.

6. Working Responsibilities
Shane McCarthy / Velocity will lead the technology architecture, development and delivery of the platform.
Johnny Shanahan / BarberBarber will provide industry and operational input and will work in parallel to
assemble the initial barber and shop cohort required for market testing. John Webb O'Rourke will support the
project as commercial lead, including commercial strategy, funding and route-to-market development.

7. Phase One Outcome
The objective at completion of Phase One is a working Barber Republic platform prepared for an initial market
pilot and capable of generating genuine evidence around barber adoption, shop adoption, successful
matching, booking conversion, completed transactions, reliability, repeat usage and other marketplace
behaviour. Phase One is an MVP development and validation programme and does not guarantee commercial
adoption, investment, revenue or valuation.

8. Phase Two and Phase Three
Phase Two is expected to focus on development and scaling following initial pilot evidence, including wider
market deployment and product refinement informed by actual user and transaction data. Phase Three may
include broader geographic expansion, further technology development and, where commercially justified by
evidence from barbering, consideration of applying the underlying marketplace architecture to appropriate
adjacent workforce markets. Phase Two and Phase Three are not included in the £55,000 Phase One fee and
will require separate scope, third party funding and agreement.

9. Confidentiality and Intellectual Property
All confidential commercial, technical and business information exchanged in connection with Barber
Republic will be treated as confidential by both parties.
Barber Republic is being built by Velocity on commission for BarberBarber. On receipt by Velocity of the full
Phase One fee, ownership of the intellectual property in the Barber Republic platform as delivered, including
its software, code, product design and the Barber Republic brand, will transfer to BarberBarber. Until the full
Phase One fee has been received, Velocity retains ownership of that intellectual property and grants
BarberBarber a limited licence to use the platform for the Phase One pilot. If the full Phase One fee is not
paid, ownership remains with Velocity.
Velocity retains ownership of its pre-existing intellectual property and of the underlying frameworks, tools,
libraries, methods and general marketplace architecture used to build the platform. Velocity grants
BarberBarber a licence to use that underlying material as embedded within the Barber Republic platform.
Nothing in this Agreement transfers ownership of that underlying material, and any application of it to markets
or sectors beyond Barber Republic will be the subject of separate agreement.
This clause relates to the Phase One deliverable. Intellectual property arising in Phase Two or Phase Three will
be governed by the separate scope and agreement referred to in clause 8.

10. Agreement and Priority
This Agreement records the revised commercial understanding for the Phase One MVP programme and is to
be read with the Barber Republic Build Scope Proposal. Where the payment timing or anticipated delivery
period stated in this Agreement differs from earlier discussions, the terms of this Agreement and the 12-14
week build and delivery period in the Build Scope Proposal will apply.
