What the evidence does and does not say

• No public evidence shows either bank is better for this business. The anecdote (a business moving about 25m euro a month to AIB) is one company's experience (R, unverified). "Hungrier" is not a service commitment: test speed, pricing and flexibility in writing. Credit strength, ownership and regulatory history do not separate them. Deposit guarantee: 100,000 euro per depositor per bank in either (C), so set limits per bank.

Recommendation

• Dublin corporate teams of both banks, in parallel, with the same pack and the same questions; this is not a delay (onboarding is the long pole; vendor guides say roughly 3 to 8 weeks for foreign-linked structures, a rough guide, U). Make AIB the lead if it wins on the written test, Bank of Ireland standby; reverse if Bank of Ireland's proposal is better. Operating accounts at the lead bank, reserves under the board treasury policy with limits per bank. Agreed with Pat on 17 Nov: both banks.

The written test: the same ten questions to both

1 Which team and named relationship manager own the group; where do credit and compliance decisions sit? 2 Time from complete documents to open accounts for each company, committed in writing? 3 What is needed for the UK vehicle, its owners and the investment manager; can it start now? 4 Can you receive a large foreign-currency payment; FX margins and cut-off times? 5 Interest and fee terms on large operating and reserve balances; short-dated placement? 6 Online payment and approval controls (dual approval, limits, payee lists, call-back, token holders)? 7 Payments to and from Saudi Arabia, the UK and the US; correspondent banks? 8 Appetite for AI and IP businesses with an overseas-linked investor; conditions? 9 Escalation contact if onboarding stalls; response time? 10 A reference from a comparable client; what you need on use of proceeds?

Scorecard (1 to 5): named team and escalation; committed onboarding timeline; treasury and FX terms; payments and Saudi corridor; sector appetite; controls and online tools; pricing. Weight timeline and sector appetite highest.
