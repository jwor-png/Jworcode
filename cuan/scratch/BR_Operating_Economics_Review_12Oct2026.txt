What gets updated,

The platform is intended to accept bookings at any hour. The technical contract must
separately define automated monitoring, human response and customer-support hours.




CONTINUOUS               Keep the service available
                         Monitor availability and payment failures, run backups and route urgent
                         alerts. The contract must name the responder and their response time.




MONTHLY                  Maintain and check
                         Schedule routine software fixes, review access and run
                         booking/payment/privacy checks. Urgent security issues do not wait for the
                         monthly review.


MONTHLY                  Review useful AI changes
                         Compare a worthwhile new model with the current one on saved business
                         and safety tests. A half-day review would cost £262.50 at the benchmark
                         rate; this is an option to scope, not proven sufficient time.


QUARTERLY                Test recovery and review access
                         Practise restoring data and recovering from failure. Arrange independent
                         security review to an agreed scope, including after material changes.




WHEN REQUIRED            Upgrade or migrate
                         Act when a provider retires a model, a security issue arises or a tested
                         improvement warrants the change. Budget substantial migrations
                         separately.



Costing rule: ask for one itemised monthly package showing included work, availability,

   A WORKED EXAMPLE

   At 15%, each shop earns £90 commission per month. A £20,000 total
   monthly budget therefore needs 223 active shops. That is 1.21% of the
   18,411-shop historical market benchmark.




ICAEW repeats a May 2025 report of 18,411 barber shops. This is a published historical
benchmark, not an official October 2026 census and not the number of immediately reachable
customers. [10]

If bookings fall from four to two per shop, the required shop count roughly doubles. If the bill
rises as shops join, use the higher bill. The formula is: monthly cost ÷ revenue per active shop.
