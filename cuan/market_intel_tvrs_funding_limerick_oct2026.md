# Town and Village Renewal Scheme (TVRS) 2026 — Limerick, 9 Oct 2026

**Official Limerick City and County Council post** — genuinely
actionable, not social-media chatter.

- Limerick City and County Council are seeking **Expressions of
  Interest (EOIs)** under the **Town and Village Renewal Scheme (TVRS)
  2026**, now open. Funded under Project Ireland 2040 / Department of
  Rural and Community Development and the Gaeltacht / "Our Rural
  Future."
- **Four information sessions scheduled, 3pm-7pm each:**
  - **Thursday 15 October** — Deebert House Hotel, Deebert, Kilmallock,
    V35 KV62.
  - **Tuesday 20 October** — Patrickswell Community Centre, Main
    Street, Patrickswell, V94 3K59.
  - **Tuesday 10 November** — The Millenium Centre, The Square,
    Caherconlish, V94 CC60.
  - **Thursday 19 November** — The Devon Inn Hotel, Templeglantine
    East, Templeglantine, V94 H489.
