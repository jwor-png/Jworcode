## 1. "CEO Checklist" (account chriztopherzine, a marketing consultant; "AI content" label visible; 80 shares)
Twelve headings, six or seven tick boxes each (paraphrased):
- **Strategic direction:** clear 3 to 5 year goals; know market and risks; strategy aligned to finance and operations; review key initiatives monthly or quarterly; long view alongside short; one vision across teams; communicate it.
- **Revenue engine:** revenue and growth targets; predictable pipeline; weekly sales, conversion and value metrics; pricing and offers against the market; scale the best channels; marketing and sales aligned; realistic, data-backed forecast.
- **Cash and capital:** healthy cash flow and runway; 3 to 6 months operating expenses in reserve; burn rate; review budgets; evaluate investments; monitor debt, equity and funding; efficient use of capital.
- **Teams and culture:** hire and retain talent; positive culture; clear roles and accountabilities; regular feedback; invest in learning; remove blockers; transparent two-way communication.
- **Scalable operations:** simplify; operations can handle demand; systems and automation; review workflows; measure performance; redundancy and resilience; prepare for growth and new markets.
- **Customers and retention:** listen to feedback; retention, churn, NPS, lifetime value; consistent value; fix pain points; loyalty; advocates and referrals; keep improving.
- **Technology and data:** right stack; data for decisions; dashboards; evaluate new technology (AI, automation); data security, privacy and regulation; data-driven culture; keep systems current.
- **Risk and compliance:** key business, financial, operational and market risks; regulatory compliance; privacy and ethics policies; risk management plans; prepare for disruption; insurance, contracts and legal protections; culture of integrity.
- **Brand and market presence:** positioning; consistent messaging; credibility and thought leadership; online presence; marketing for awareness and leads; competitor moves; keep brand current.
- **Leadership and self-mastery:** focus and discipline; manage energy and attention; keep learning; seek feedback; calm decisions under pressure; lead by example; look after physical and mental wellbeing.
- **Board and advisors:** strong board or advisory network; diverse expertise; structured updates and meaningful discussions; use their guidance to challenge decisions; keep the board aligned; be transparent and prepared; use their networks.
- **Exit and long-term options:** clear long-term vision (grow, exit, legacy); explore exit or liquidity options early (acquisition, IPO); build a valuable, investor-attractive business; key processes and people run without you; document knowledge and decisions; keep options open (partial exit, new venture, succession); build lasting impact.

## 2. Ross Harkness, "If your business still relies on you, fix these 5 mistakes" (13 slides; slides 2 to 13 seen)
1. **Misusing SOPs:** an SOP is how you want things done taken out of your head and onto paper; people follow it, rely on it less, and after about 10 repetitions do not need it; keep it for new hires and to change outputs by changing the SOP.
2. **Blaming everything but yourself:** blaming the market or the team changes nothing; take ownership ("I'm not being clear enough, how else can I get this across?"); cites Jocko Willink's Extreme Ownership.
3. **You break your own rules:** if you block 9am to 12pm for deep work and then reply instantly, the rule does not exist; hold the line and set the example.
4. **Not using data:** the leaky bucket; track and use data; map systems first, then a system-level scorecard and a business-level scorecard; learn spreadsheets.
5. **Never working on yourself:** the business is the result of your decisions; you cannot grow a business from 500k to 5 million without becoming a better owner; focus, decisions and execution.
Closing slide is a sales pitch ("comment 'perform' and I'll send info"; 7 and 8 figure owners).
