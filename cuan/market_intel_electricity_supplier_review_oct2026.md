# Electricity supplier review — flagged 10 Oct 2026

John flagged wanting to review electricity supply costs — **currently
with "SSC electricity"** (almost certainly **SSE Airtricity**,
treating as a likely dictation mishearing rather than a confirmed
different supplier — worth a one-line confirmation from John). Energia

## What's actually known, honestly — the data is thinner than it looks

**Residential rate comparisons found conflict with each other,** which
matters for giving honest advice rather than a confident-sounding
wrong answer:
- One Selectra snapshot (September 2026) puts **SSE Airtricity
  cheapest**, 26.41c/kWh unit rate, ~€1,492/year estimated — Energia
  at 27.39c/kWh, no annual figure given.
- A separate Selectra comparison page (undated) shows the **opposite**
  — Energia from 14.10c/kWh vs SSE Airtricity at 19.25c/kWh — with no
  stated date or customer-type basis, so it can't be reconciled with
  the figure above.
- A third source (Selectra's broader July 2026 guide) names **Yuno
  Energy** as cheapest on a standard 24hr plan (€1,554/year) and SSE
  Airtricity as cheapest specifically for **smart meter** customers
  (€1,484/year); Energia's NightStorage plan comes in at €1,334/year
  for customers on that tariff type.
- Electric Ireland's own comparison table is from **November 2023** —
  too old to use.

**No reliable current business/commercial rate data found at all.**
Both Energia and SSE Airtricity confirm they serve commercial
customers with 100% renewable electricity, but neither supplier's
actual business pricing showed up in search — commercial rates aren't
published the way residential ones are, and depend on usage profile,
contract length and any negotiated business discount.

## What this means for honest advice right now

**Cuan is not going to recommend a specific switch off conflicting,
dated comparison-site data** — that would be exactly the kind of
confident-but-wrong answer John has explicitly said he doesn't want.
The right next step is direct quotes, not a desk-research conclusion:

1. **Confirm the actual current supplier and whether this is
   household, a specific venture premises, or both** — "our
   electricity supply" wasn't scoped. AHL/UHL retail premises,
   Barber Republic, and the household account would likely all have
   different usage profiles and different switching economics; worth
   knowing which is actually in scope before doing more work on this.
2. **Get a like-for-like quote directly from Energia** (since they've
   already invited contact) **and from the current supplier as a
   retention offer**, rather than relying on comparison-site headline
   rates — comparison sites commonly exclude welcome credits/cashback
   and may not reflect actual contract terms.
3. **Use the CRU's own independent comparison tool** (the Commission
   for Regulation of Utilities, Ireland's energy regulator — not a
   commercial/affiliate site like Selectra) as a neutral cross-check
   once actual quotes are in hand, rather than trusting any single
   comparison site's numbers.
4. **For a business premises specifically**, a direct conversation
   with Energia's commercial team (not the residential switching
   process) is the right route — business tariffs aren't the same
   product as the household ones quoted above.
