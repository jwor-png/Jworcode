# Echelon (Niall Molloy) to draw further capital from Starwood Capital

**The deal:** Starwood Capital — the US investment firm whose Starwood
Distressed Opportunity Fund XIII closed in July 2026 with over $3bn of
committed equity (20+ transactions already closed) — is planning to
deploy further money into Echelon, the Irish data-centre operator led
by founder and chief executive **Niall Molloy**, from Starwood's latest
**$10.2bn (~€9.1bn) real estate debt fund**, led by Barry Sternlicht.

**Existing stake and scale:** Companies Registration Office filings show
Starwood has already invested just over €390m in Echelon. Earlier this
year Starwood and Molloy reported undertaking a strategic review of the
firm, including the possibility of a full or partial sale. Separately,
Starwood is understood to be looking to raise a new debt fund of $850m
over the next six months, with a mix of European and global assets.

**Background (Feb 2024):** Starwood pledged $850m to Echelon to become
a 50% shareholder.

**Fund focus:** residential, industrial and hospitality assets, with
30–40% of Starwood's previous equity funds deployed in Europe. The fund
is focusing on additional sites to build out.
