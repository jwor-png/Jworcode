# Alexandr Wang — Meta's AI wunderkind, FT profile

**Who:** Alexandr Wang, 29, head of Meta's Superintelligence Labs and
Chief AI Officer. Son of two Chinese-American physicists, grew up in
New Mexico, maths prodigy, graduated high school early, worked at
Quora, enrolled at MIT at 18, dropped out a year later to found
**Scale AI** (2016) — a data-labelling company that signed deals with
OpenAI (Sam Altman, a former flatmate of Wang's) and Anthropic (Dario
Amodei).

**The Meta deal:** Zuckerberg brought Wang into Meta via a **$14.3bn**
transaction involving Scale AI, to overhaul Meta's AI team structure
and leadership after an underwhelming Meta AI model release triggered
internal upheaval.

**Rocky start, now recovering:** early months were difficult — a
"vibe report" to Meta executives late last year was described by CFO
Susan Li as inadequate ("if this doesn't work, this is going to be...
very, very bad in financial terms"); Wang spent early months poaching
star researchers from rivals and triggering a Silicon Valley talent
war, with the first models under his leadership not keeping pace with
competitors. Described by one evaluator (Anastasios Angelopoulos,
CEO, Arena) as "not as good as models from Anthropic and OpenAI yet,
but the strides are massive."

**The turnaround moment:** launch of **Meta's Muse Wonder AI model**
lifted Meta's stock; this followed five weeks after Meta's Muse AI
agent became the most-downloaded free app in both Apple and Android
app stores. Meta also announced a partnership with Apple to deploy
Meta's open-weight AI models on Mac computers. Zuckerberg says this
has returned Meta to the "frontier" of AI.

**Positioning/succession angle:** Wang is now being touted as a
potential successor to Zuckerberg — one former colleague quoted:
"He has sometimes viewed himself as a second-class citizen in the AI
space... now he's getting to prove he's in the same league as Sam,
Dario and the others." Zuckerberg himself has called Wang "the
fucking guy" and given him unusual latitude within Meta.

**Controversy noted:** Scale AI faced a lawsuit alleging it required
staff to evaluate extreme material as part of its data-labelling
work (later voluntarily dismissed); Wang also clashed with Scale
co-founder Lucy Guo, whom he fired in 2018.

**Relevance:** a notable, citable data point for Meridian's AI
Strategy & Governance domain — a concrete, named illustration of how
fast frontier AI leadership and competitive position can shift (Meta
going from an underwhelming release and internal crisis to a stock-
moving turnaround within roughly a year under new leadership), and of
the data-labelling/training-data layer (Scale AI) as a serious
commercial AI business in its own right, distinct from the model
developers themselves.
