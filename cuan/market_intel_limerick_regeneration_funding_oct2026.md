# "€22.95million regeneration funding will support 'game changer' Limerick projects"

Source: Limerick Post. VERIFIED mainstream local press report, citing named ministers, TDs and council statements.

## What it says

- Minister for Housing, Local Government and Heritage James Browne announced €22.95m in Limerick city/county funding under the new **Towns and Cities Regeneration Investment Fund** (replacing the former Urban Regeneration Development Fund), part of an overall €409m tranche to almost 120 projects nationally.
- **Limerick city allocations:** Southside Connectivity Project €6m (walking/cycling/public realm, safer connections between neighbourhoods/schools/services/amenities, stronger links between Southill regeneration community and key destinations); €150,000 for design proposals/planning for the Limerick Fashion Incubator (former Mary Street Garda Station, King's Island); €9m for the Nicholas Street Public Realm Scheme (major facelift for King's Island — Nicholas Street, Church Street, Castle Street, St Augustine Place, the Parade — new paving, lighting, street furniture, accessibility improvements, supporting Limerick's Medieval Quarter); €150,000 for a municipal sports facility at Prospect, Hyde Road (within the wider Colbert Quarter regeneration area).
- **County allocation:** €150,000 to the **Newcastle West Town Centre Public Realm Enhancement Project**, to advance future streetscape/public realm improvements.
- Limerick received the highest local authority allocation outside Dublin City Council.
- Cross-party praise: Limerick City TD Willie O'Dea called it a "game changer"; Minister Patrick O'Donovan praised the Newcastle West funding specifically; Minister Niall Collins echoed support; Limerick City TD/Minister Kieran O'Donnell called it "transformational"; Mayor John Moran said it would "help address some longstanding challenges" and "support bricks and mortar upgrades."
- Fund criteria prioritise redevelopment of vacant/underused sites, public realm enhancements, and community/civic infrastructure.
