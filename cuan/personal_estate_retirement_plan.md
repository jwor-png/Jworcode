## Track 3 — Estate, retirement paperwork, and end-of-life planning

This is the genuinely new content John asked for — a template of what
"the plan" should cover for someone in his position. Structured as a
checklist to work through, not yet populated with John's actual
answers/decisions.

### 3.1 — Will
- [ ] Confirm whether a current, valid will exists and when it was
  last reviewed/updated.
- [ ] If it exists: does it reflect current assets, family
  circumstances (Majella, children, Helen's situation at Portauns),
  and the shares-held-beneficially question below? If not, it needs
  updating, not assumed still accurate.
- [ ] If no current will exists, or it's out of date: engage a
  solicitor to draft/update one. **Cross-reference:** the PIP/
  solicitor engagement already open from the Birmingham council tax
  matter (item 8 above) and the "[ASK — heard as 'Marrick Power']"
  personal solvency practitioner/solicitor note in `master_plan.md` —
  worth establishing whether the same professional can handle both, or
  whether separate specialists are needed (insolvency vs. wills/estate
  planning are different specialisms).

### 3.2 — Enduring Power of Attorney (EPA)
- [ ] Appoint an attorney (or attorneys) for both **financial affairs**
  and **personal welfare/healthcare decisions** — Irish EPA law (Assisted
  Decision-Making (Capacity) Act 2015 framework) allows these to be
  combined or separated; needs a decision on who John wants in each
  role (not assumed — Majella is an obvious candidate but not decided
  here).
- [ ] Confirm this is done via solicitor, registered appropriately, and
  reviewed alongside the will (they're usually drafted together).

### 3.3 — Funeral/end-of-life wishes
- [ ] Appoint or identify a preferred funeral director in advance —
  purely practical, removes a burden from family at the time.
- [ ] Document any specific wishes (burial/cremation, service
  preferences, location) in writing, held alongside the will — not
  necessarily *in* the will itself, since wills are often read after
  the funeral has already taken place.

### 3.4 — Full asset/liability register ("what I own and owe")
- [ ] A single, maintained list of: property (Portauns, Bantry, any
  other), bank accounts, pensions, investments, shareholdings
  (including the **beneficial-but-not-in-his-name shares** John
  flagged — needs clarifying which company/companies this refers to,
  and the legal mechanism, e.g. nominee arrangement or trust, before
  this can be resolved properly), business interests (UHL, AHL,
  Meridian, Ambrion, Velocity, ODIN, Barber Republic, KEDL, etc.), debts
  (the Legacy Financial Matters tracker), and any other assets.
- [ ] This register is the practical companion to the will — a
  solicitor/executor needs it to actually administer the estate
  efficiently; keeping it live now avoids a scramble later.

### 3.5 — Beneficial shareholdings not in John's own name
- **Flagged directly by John, not yet detailed.** Needs a follow-up

  (nominee, family member, trust, company vehicle), and what the
  correct treatment is for both the will and the asset register. **Not
  guessed at here — genuinely open, to revisit.**

### 3.6 — Tax/financial planning specific to retirement
- [ ] Pension contribution planning for the remaining ~4 years — cross-
  reference the age-banded tax-free contribution limits already logged
  in `market_intel_farmers_journal_batch_sept2026.md` (age 60+: up to

- [ ] Confirm State Pension (Contributory) position — PRSI contribution
  record check via mywelfare.ie, per the same reference material.
- [ ] Decide, with a financial adviser, the target "clean financial

  enjoy retirement) but not yet a *figure* — worth putting a number on
