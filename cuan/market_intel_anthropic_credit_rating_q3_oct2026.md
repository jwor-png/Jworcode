# Anthropic's credit rating and Q3 numbers — FT, 17/18 Oct 2026

**The headline:** Anthropic secured an issuer rating of **double-B
plus (BB+)** from Kroll Bond Rating Agency (KBRA) in June — one notch
below investment grade, the highest possible speculative-grade score
— ahead of securing its **$35bn chip financing package led by Apollo**.
Not previously reported before this FT piece.

**Why it matters:** this rating shows Anthropic is close to achieving
full investment-grade status, which would let it lower borrowing costs
and access a far wider pool of institutional investors, including life
insurance companies and pension funds. Both Anthropic and rival OpenAI
have been lobbying the largest rating agencies to secure investment-
grade ratings once they go public.

**How the rating was actually produced — a real transparency caveat:**
KBRA's rating came despite **Anthropic's limited financial
disclosure** — based only on select information Anthropic chose to
provide (revenue, adjusted operating income, annualised run-rate
revenue for Q1 2026, and an internal forecast for Q2). **Anthropic's
disclosure specifically excluded free cash flow and EBITDA.** KBRA's
own analysts had to build estimates based on assumptions given the
limited information, and produced a "risk score" rather than a
traditional full financial model. KBRA and Anthropic both declined to
comment to the FT.

**Debt structure detail:** of the $35bn financing package, **about
$4.4bn is junior debt that exposes lenders directly to Anthropic's own
credit risk**; the rest is secured by a guarantee from chipmaker
**Broadcom**.

**New financial figures, updating the thread:**
- **Q3 2026 revenue rose nearly 50% to $16.8bn** (per Anthropic's own
  pre-IPO investor presentation, Wednesday) — this is a new, more
  current figure than the $9bn annualised run-rate and $3.2-4.6bn S1
  revenue figures already logged from the 4 Oct Sunday Times piece.
- **Profitable on an adjusted basis for two consecutive quarters**,
  per Anthropic's own presentation — not independently verified.
- **IPO valuation expectation confirmed again at more than $2
  trillion** this year — consistent with, and reconfirming, the figure
  already locked in from the 4 Oct Sunday Times piece (which itself
  resolved an earlier $1tn/$2tn ambiguity).
- **Anthropic expects to spend more than $500bn on computing resources
  and infrastructure over the next decade** — a new figure, not
  previously logged.
