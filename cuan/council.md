## The core distinction (John's Council's own words)

> Cuan holds what you are actually doing and becoming. The Council tests
> whether the way you're living is coherent with what you say matters. You
> decide what any of that means about who you are.

Cuan = operational memory and execution (the creed, revenue streams, the
day-to-day). The Council = a cognitive/character testing layer, deliberately
kept distinct — six members, each a different lens, not six flavours of the
same coach.

## The six Council members

- **Bob Geldof** — attacks false calm and excuse language.
- **Michael Collins** — interrogates viability, information, and what
  survives contact with reality.
- **Katie Taylor** — holds execution, discipline, and what remains when
  noise disappears. ("Identity Core — Lived, Not Stated.")
- **Deepak Chopra** — interrogates identification and the relationship
  between the operator and the roles being carried.
- **Francesca Albanese** — holds the legal/ethical line, interrogates power
  rather than convenience.
- **Tony Robbins** — challenges inertia, exposes where movement has
  stalled rather than merely become difficult.

Each member has its own cognitive advisor / identity profile document
(referenced in the source chat as individual PDFs per member).

## "10 Ways John Can Use His Council" (source document, 10 Feb 2026)

Sent by John to himself (jwebborourke@icloud.com, from
john@velocityai.ie), subject "My Council" — practical, human,
low-friction prompts for daily use, one line to each Council member (see
the six members above), each with a "why it works" note tying it back to
John specifically.

1. **Ask Deepak Chopra:** "I'm changing my life and don't fully trust
   myself yet." *Why it works: lets John start with reflection, not
   pressure — Deepak slows the nervous system and gives language for
   transition.*
2. **Ask Katie Taylor:** "Am I strong enough for what I'm stepping into?"
   *Why it works: reframes strength as daily discipline, not bravado —
   John resonates with that immediately.*
3. **Say to the Council:** "Tell me the truth, even if I won't like it."
   *Why it works: bypasses AI entirely and speaks to identity, which is
   where John actually is.*
4. **Ask Tony Robbins:** "I know what I should do, but I'm hesitating."
   *Why it works: Collins doesn't motivate — he clarifies trade-offs.
   John respects that kind of honesty.* [note: the "why it works" text
   here appears to describe Michael Collins, not Tony Robbins as headed —
   likely a labelling slip in the source document itself, not a
   transcription error — worth flagging back to whoever built it]
5. **Ask Bob Geldof:** "When is confrontation necessary, and when does
   it just become noise?" *Why it works: helps John channel anger into
   action, not suppression or explosion.*
6. **Just say:** "Something here feels wrong but I can't name it." Then
   let the Council respond. *Why it works: this is often the first
   unlock for non-AI users — no framing required.*
7. **Ask Michael Collins or Katie Taylor:** "How do I partner with
   someone who moves faster than me?" *Why it works: directly supports
   his partnership with you [Shane] without making it about you.*
8. **Ask Deepak Chopra:** "Am I evolving or just escaping?" *Why it
   works: how do you tell the difference between growth and running away
   from discomfort?*
9. **Ask Bob Geldof and then Katie Taylor:** "What's the difference
   between courage and impulse?" *Why it works: two very different
   minds, same question — John sees contrast without confusion.*
10. **Say to the Council:** "Talk to me like a man who has to live with
    his choices." *Why it works: "I want no motivation. Just tell me
    what kind of man this path requires" — bypasses AI entirely and
    speaks to identity, which is where John actually is.*

**The key message for John (if you say one thing):** "You don't need to
know how to use it. Just talk to one person you respect and see what
happens."

**Note on item 4:** the "why it works" explanation under Tony Robbins
