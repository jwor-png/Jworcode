## 2. PRSA used to buy an apartment to rent (Paul Murray, Quest Retirement Solutions)
- A non-Standard PRSA with a provider that facilitates direct property can hold a residential property; the apartment is an asset of the PRSA, rent is paid into the fund, costs are paid from the fund, and it cannot be transferred into your own name; RTB registration is needed for the PRSA's rental income tax exemption; consider concentration (large share of retirement savings in one property) and take financial and tax advice first.

## 3. Section 72 life policy (Barry McCutcheon, Royal London Ireland)
- A whole-of-life policy whose proceeds are tax-free if used to pay inheritance tax; can help children pay inheritance tax on a family home and income tax on an ARF inherited by a child over 21. Parents expecting substantial ARFs and a valuable family home asked whether it helps.
- Use for John: estate and retirement plan (with Mark Power or an adviser); a prompt for the "Personal estate and retirement plan" item (executor, EPA, Majella's support).

## 4. EU Inc. (Sarah Collins): relevant to the Noesis group
- EU Commissioner Michael McGrath urged investors, including Stripe co-founder Patrick Collison, to be patient on the draft EU start-up law ("EU Inc."), tabled last March: start-ups could incorporate in any EU country within 48 hours and operate under one set of rules (compared with Delaware); the draft includes common rules on employee stock options and a fast-track online bankruptcy procedure. Negotiations are not halfway done; Irish officials are steering talks under Ireland's EU Council presidency; McGrath expects a compromise at the end.
