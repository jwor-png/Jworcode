# Hireovate — AI recruitment start-up, Dublin

**The company:** Hireovate, a Dublin (Clontarf)-based AI business
founded by **Declan Fitzgerald** earlier this year. Two staff,
€100,000 invested to date. Has its first commercial users on board,
with more due later this year.

**Founder background:** Fitzgerald previously led recruitment at
**Meta Ireland** (between 2016 and 2023) and was also head of
recruitment at **HubSpot**.

**The problem it targets:** the "volume problem" in recruitment —
companies are sending and receiving more applications than ever,
creating a bottleneck. Hireovate's pitch: "we help people transition
their work using the latest AI technologies... engineered executive
recruitment to find what the hidden job market." Instead of candidates
constantly looking through a career site, Hireovate aims to get people
access to "that are going to hire... what [giving] signals they are
sending."

**How it works — "reverse engineered recruitment":**
- Identifies behavioural/activity signals companies are exhibiting
  that suggest they're about to hire (releasing a new product, opening
  new funding, opening a new location).
- Internal recruitment teams are, in Fitzgerald's words, "feeling this
  deluge of candidates being sent their way" — Hireovate's tool tries
  to identify who the actual decision-makers/hiring leaders are and
  reach the recruiter's point of view directly, rather than adding to
  the deluge.
- Also developed a technology (called "Marketscan") that checks a
  user's LinkedIn profile for accuracy, produces a detailed market scan
  that finds roles that are a direct hit for what they are looking for
  but also what industry/location, then asks simple questions ("what
  job do they want, what location?").
- Launched in recent weeks; already engaged with both the IDA and the
  Department of Enterprise, Trade and the Department of Enterprise to
  find a B2B use case.
- On the company side, Hireovate is also "working on how to get those
  people back to work quickly" — working with the Department on
  redundancy-related roles.

**Growth ambition:** next goal is to bring the first B2B customer
onboard and grow to have at least 10 within the next 18 months.

**Relevance:** a direct, named Irish AI-startup competitor/adjacent
player in the AI-adoption space Meridian/Velocity AI/Ambrion AI also

Topline-adjacent competitive entries), and a useful general proof

engaging with small Irish AI start-ups on both consumer and B2B use
cases.
