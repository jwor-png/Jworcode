# Sunday Independent Business Comment p14 (15 Nov 2026), Gina London "The Communicator": "Leadership means nothing without values: just look at the White House". Opinion; US politics framing is the author's. Logged for noting only.

- **Hook (US politics, author's view, unverified):** after the Republicans' midterm defeat (Democrats take House and Senate), President Trump told CBS's 60 Minutes his deportation policy "might have been too tough", then a day later returned to questioning the election and defending the policy, and appointed Stephen Miller as chief of staff. Her point: recognising a problem is not changing behaviour.
- **Workplace lessons:** (1) hierarchy and orders versus influence: two senior executives in a multinational engineering firm; an email warning of a multi-million cost "identified the problem and consequences but did not invite collaboration"; contrast with "We're facing a significant delivery challenge... I need everyone's expertise... What are we missing?" ("pressure versus progress"); an expert who struggles to influence beyond his area. (2) **Values mean nothing without behaviours**: define how values are put into action and test them under pressure (what happens when someone challenges the boss, when a deadline is missed, whether staff can question an established process); a speak-up culture is built by habits, not announcements. (3) **Awareness, desire, discipline**: three stages of behavioural change; communication is trainable ("rehearse replacing accusations with curiosity, instructions with invitations, and defensiveness with genuine listening"). Closing line: the measure of a leader is "what people are willing and able to accomplish together, long after the leader has left the room."
