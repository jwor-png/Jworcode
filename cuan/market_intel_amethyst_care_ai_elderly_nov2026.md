# Business Post p32 (Sun 8 Nov 2026), Emmet Ryan: "Amethyst to raise 1.5m euro for AI elderly support tool"

Source: John's photograph, 8 Nov 2026 (page photographed sideways; "Making it Work" series, with Enterprise Ireland branding, "Helping Irish business go global", so partly a sponsored-style feature). Claims below are the article's, not verified by Cuan.

## What it says
- **Amethyst Care**, an AI healthcare business founded by Rebecca McManus in 2024, based at Dogpatch Labs, Dublin. Staff: 6. Funding to date: 620,000 euro. Aiming to raise 1.5 million euro in its next round.
- **Product:** an AI voice companion in a speaker in the home. It gives medication reminders and daily prompts (for example, did you lock the door, call your son) and can hold a conversation like a friend. A clinician or caregiver logs into a portal to create a patient profile with medications and daily care plans; it tracks all communication two-way.
- **Founder's story:** her father had Parkinson's and Lewy Body dementia and her mother had breast cancer, at the same time. She started on paper, researched online, built a deck and a business plan, then joined an accelerator programme. She worked in tech sales before; did research with Trinity College Dublin; the firm spun out of Trinity in the summer and received Enterprise Ireland PSSF (pre-seed start fund) funding.
- **Market:** a US headquarters in Mentor, Ohio (about 20 minutes outside Cleveland); product trials in Ohio over the last 12 months; plans for several other US states and Ireland over the next 18 months. Pull-quote: "It helps keep people at home and independent for longer."
- **Enterprise Ireland:** the founder says its brand opens doors globally and its advisers helped map the business plan; support went beyond money.
