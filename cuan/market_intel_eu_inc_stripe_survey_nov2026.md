## Main article: Stripe survey of EU founders and "EU Inc"
- **Stripe survey (200 businesses across all 27 EU member states):** 36% of EU company founders have considered launching outside the bloc because of burdensome regulation (up since the previous survey in 2022); 73% say time spent on regulation and compliance is increasing; only 29% believe common EU rules are an advantage; only 21% are confident Europe will become a global technology leader within five years; 52% say more harmonisation of company formation rules would help them sell in more markets.
- **EU Inc:** a fast-track single company structure for start-ups, proposal tabled earlier this year by European Commissioner for Justice Michael McGrath. **Ireland, under its EU presidency, is currently leading talks on it.** Patrick Collison (Stripe co-founder) was among the backers of a paper on how to treat early-stage investors and employee stock options under an EU Inc structure.
- Eileen O'Mara (Stripe vice-chair): "European founders are asking for clear, harmonised rules that enable them to innovate at scale." Stripe suggests judging EU Inc by how many companies incorporate under it after a year and whether they spend less time on red tape.
- Collison set up the **Rhine Group** think tank this summer with Mario Draghi; Draghi's 2024 competitiveness report is cited as the blueprint for reforms. The article also notes Collison is a large donor to Building a Better California, which campaigned against a one-time 5% tax on billionaires (New York Times report, as cited).
