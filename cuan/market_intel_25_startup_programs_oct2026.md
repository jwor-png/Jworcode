# "25 Global Startup Programs for Founders Looking to Raise Capital" — Apex Round / Startup Stealth

Source: Instagram carousel, co-branded Apex Round and Startup Stealth (both verified accounts). UNVERIFIED as a vetted/researched list — a curated promotional carousel, program details (equity terms, funding amounts) not independently checked by Cuan. Framed as "there's something for everyone, from early-stage prioritisation to accelerators that take nothing."

## Full list (24 of 25 captured — slide 7, the closing slide, not captured)

**Tier 1 — the best-known names:**
1. **Y Combinator** — "the gold standard." Funding, mentorship, one of the strongest founder networks, track record of world-class companies.
2. **Techstars** — global accelerator with mentors, funding and customers, plus industry-focused programs and a large alumni network.
3. **500 Global** — early-stage funding + worldwide founder network, global reach across markets and industries.
4. **Sequoia Arc** — build alongside Sequoia partners before Series A, hands-on support, product feedback, network access.
5. **HF0 Residency** — 2-week residency for technical founders, up to $500K, focused support to build/iterate/reach product-market fit.

**Early-stage / accelerator programs:**
6. **AngelPad** — elite early-stage accelerator, strong track record of backing breakout companies.
7. **Betaworks** — AI and frontier technology startup programs, strong community, early-stage investment opportunities.
8. **Moonshot Platform** — equity-free grants, two-year accelerator for young founders solving global challenges.
9. **OpenAI** (startup programs) — residency, grants, DevDay and startup programs for AI builders, access to resources and technical support.
10. **Founder HQ** — Australia's leading accelerator, funding, mentorship, strong founder community across the region.

**Residency / community-first programs:**
11. **The Residency** — build in public alongside ambitious founders, supportive community, resources, exposure.
12. **South Park Commons** — community for founders before they even have an idea, events, resources, supportive network.
13. **Launch House** — founder community with builders, operators and investors, programs/events/hands-on support.
14. **ODF** (On Deck Fellowship) — one-week in-person experience focused on meeting collaborators, exploring ideas, gaining conviction.
15. **Alchemist Accelerator** — 2-week residency for technical founders, up to $500K, mentorship, hands-on support, strong founder network.

**University/corporate-backed accelerators:**
16. **Berkeley SkyDeck** — Berkeley-backed accelerator, funding, world-class mentorship, deep technical network.
17. **Plug and Play** — corporate partnerships across dozens of industries, connecting startups with global corporates/investors/customers.
18. **Station F** — the world's largest startup campus (Paris), global community, resources, funding opportunities.
19. **MassChallenge** — global accelerator taking zero equity, industry-specific programs, mentorship, large global network.
20. **Startmate** — Australia's leading accelerator (region-specific), funding, mentorship, strong founder community.

**Pre-seed / talent-focused programs:**
21. **Antler Global** — find a co-founder, validate an idea, build from zero, global support/funding/founder community.
22. **PearX** — "one of the best pre-seed founder programs," funding, hands-on support, global network of exceptional founders.
23. **Pioneer.app** — build remotely, compete globally, get discovered, community/challenges/launch-and-grow opportunities.
24. **Z Fellows** — small cohort backing exceptional young builders, funding, mentorship, direct access to the Z network.
25. **Founders, Inc.** — intense founder residency built around shipping, hands-on support, experienced operators, network of top founders/investors.
