## The 19 channels, with what each means

| # | Channel | What it means |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Targeting Blogs | Reach audiences through niche blogs they already trust |
| 2 | Publicity | Earn media coverage from journalists and publications |
| 3 | Unconventional PR | Use stunts or surprising ideas to get attention |
| 4 | Search Engine Marketing | Pay for high-intent search traffic |
| 5 | Social and Display Ads | Run targeted ads on social platforms and websites |
| 6 | Offline Ads | Use print, radio, TV, outdoor, or direct mail |
| 7 | Search Engine Optimization | Rank organically for keywords customers search |
| 8 | Content Marketing | Publish useful content that attracts ideal customers |
| 9 | Email Marketing | Build and nurture an email list to convert and retain users |
| 10 | Viral Marketing | Design sharing loops that bring in more users |
| 11 | Engineering as Marketing | Build free tools or resources that attract leads |
| 12 | Business Development | Grow through partnerships and strategic deals |
| 13 | Sales | Win customers through direct outreach and structured selling |
| 14 | Affiliate Programs | Let partners promote your product for commission |
| 15 | Existing Platforms | Build on top of platforms with built-in audiences |
| 16 | Trade Shows | Meet prospects and partners at industry events |
| 17 | Offline Events + Speaking | Host or join in-person events to build trust |
| 18 | Speaking Engagements | Teach or present to audiences that match your market |
| 19 | Community Building | Create a loyal audience around a shared mission or topic |

## The companion "18-Day Customer Acquisition Challenge"

Same 18 of the 19 channels (minus #17/18 merged), reframed as a
one-channel-per-day practical exercise — e.g. Day 2 Publicity: "write
one strong story angle a journalist could cover"; Day 9 Email
Marketing: "create one lead magnet people would trade an email for";
Day 13 Sales: "write a simple outreach message to a potential
customer." Explicitly framed as testing ideas quickly, not committing
to a full channel strategy — "test one channel each day. Find your
traction."
