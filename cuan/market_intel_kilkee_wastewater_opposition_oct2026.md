# Kilkee wastewater treatment plant — opposition continues despite High Court ruling, 17 Oct 2026

**The ruling:** the High Court upheld planning permission for Uisce
Éireann's proposed wastewater treatment plant at Kilkee, Co. Clare
(Dunlicky Road location), and separately refused the opposing
residents leave to appeal that decision to the Court of Appeal.

**Uisce Éireann's position:** the project ends the long-standing
discharge of raw/untreated sewage into the sea at Kilkee — a popular
Limerick day-trip and holiday destination — and will support future
residential, economic and tourism development in the town.

**Opposition position (Save Kilkee Cliff Walk group):** explicitly
not conceding despite the ruling — "we are not going away... the
campaign continues," consulting their legal team on options following
the judgment. Core objection: the proposal is only a **primary
treatment** facility (removes solids by tanker, road-transported
several times weekly) while the remaining treated-but-not-fully-clean
effluent continues to discharge into the sea at the same location
(Intrinsic Bay) as currently — i.e. they dispute it is the genuine
upgrade Uisce Éireann frames it as. They state they've been seeking a
"proper wastewater solution" for 30 years and raised concerns about
treatment level, location, environmental impact and the approval
process itself, none of which they consider resolved by the High
Court's refusal of an appeal right.
