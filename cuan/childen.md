## What it is

A **complete architecture for the protection of children in the age of
artificial intelligence** — design and architecture only, not built out,
not developed as a running product. Built independently, and — per
Shane's draft letter (2 Sept) — **before** the Pope's AI letter/
encyclical *Magnifica Humanitas* was written, arriving independently at
values the letter later asked of the age.

**Ownership, clarified 2 Sept:** **Shane McCarthy is the architect and
sole owner of the Childen estate.** This resolves an open question —
earlier logging didn't establish whose IP this was; it is Shane's alone,
not a joint John/Shane asset in the way ODIN's IP (also Shane's,
captured via Asterial Limited) is structured. Worth being precise about
this distinction rather than assuming Childen follows the same ownership
pattern as ODIN/Asterial.

## The ask

**Not a purchase.** The draft letter is explicit: the Church is not
asked to buy the work from its own funds. The ask is that the Church act
as **convenor** — convening the funds, partners, and will the standard
requires, and **holding the standard in trust** at the centre of what it
convenes, in the tradition of the Church gathering the world around its
schools and hospitals historically.

**Process:** "the accompanying pages say everything that can responsibly
be said in writing. The rest is shown rather than sent, through a
governed process, at whatever pace the Holy See prefers." — i.e. the
substance of the architecture itself is not being handed over in this
letter; it's a request to be allowed to present it.

### Two-pager — "CHILDEN: Child safety architecture for the AI era"
Marked "PREPARED FOR INTRODUCTION TO THE HOLY SEE · PRIVATE &
CONFIDENTIAL." Structure: The sanctuary (opening framing — the Church
built sanctuaries — school, hospital, parish — safe because of what
they were, not who was watching; childhood has moved into a digital
world with no such place ever built there) → Why now (the model
protecting children is failing — age checks bypassed, harm no longer
disputed, a Harvard study estimated social media companies earned
over $11bn in ad revenue from minors in a single year, AI now
manufactures new harm faster than watching systems can learn to
recognise it) → What Childen is → The teaching, answered (Childen built
independently, before the encyclical, arrived at the same values by its
own road) → Why the Church (a commercial owner would face incentive
conflicts the Church doesn't carry; the Church holds no obligation to
shareholders, no duty to maximise returns, no exit event) → The
Convenor point, stated plainly (not asked to purchase; the Church
convenes funds/partners/will, holds the standard in trust) → The
conversation sought (a private meeting, offices identified below) →
Who presents (Shane McCarthy, architect and sole owner of the Childen
estate, Ireland).

**Seven connected domains** (named as covering the whole of a child's
protection): the environment they live in, their life beyond it, the
new harms AI creates, the intelligence, the humans, the governance, and
the reach toward every child. "One design, whole, each part
strengthening the others... held by one owner."

**Six founding commitments** (verbatim from the two-pager):
1. Every child is real — known and protected inside a verified
   environment, so children share their world only with children.
2. Protection acts first — stepping in before harm reaches a child, and
   teaching as it protects.
3. Intelligence is governed — serves human judgement, explains itself
   plainly, and forgets by design, so a childhood mistake never becomes
   a permanent record.
4. Care stays human — the technology's purpose is to bring a child to a
   person, never to replace one.
5. It can be proven — schools, parents and those responsible for
   children can see, and demonstrate, that it works.
6. Children's data is treated with restraint — a founding rule of the
   design, not a policy that can be quietly changed.

**Offices identified for the introduction:** the Inter Dicasterial
Commission on Artificial Intelligence (coordinated through the
Dicastery for Promoting Integral Human Development); the Pontifical
Commission for the Protection of Minors (the Holy See's principal
safeguarding body); the Dicastery for Culture and Education (given the
Church's global educational responsibility); or the office of the
Secretariat of State "judges appropriate for a matter of this nature."

### Six-pager — "CHILDEN: The Study Document"
Marked "PREPARED FOR THE HOLY SEE · PRIVATE & CONFIDENTIAL." Nine
sections: I. The sanctuary that is missing · II. The regulatory moment
· III. How Childen protects children, and the standard the Church could
hold · IV. What is different, in the history of this problem · V. The
commitments, and the teaching they answer · VI. What it means for a
child, and for a family · VII. The architecture, for those who will
examine it · VIII. The commercial world's response, considered · IX.
Custodianship, and the path of examination.

**Regulatory context (Section II) — worth having verified independently
before citing externally, REPORTED not yet checked against primary
sources by Cuan:** UK Online Safety Act — penalties up to 10% of global
revenue, strong age assurance enforced since July 2025. EU Digital
Services Act — penalties up to 6% of global turnover. US child-protection
regime "given force by landmark enforcement" (unspecified). Australia's
under-16 nationwide restriction in force since December 2025.

**Design principle (Section III/IV) — the one-sentence version:**
"instead of watching the child's environment from outside, build the
environment itself, with safety in its foundations." Existing
approaches inspect content after the fact (a losing race against
unlimited AI-generated harm); Childen constitutes the environment
itself — verification is institutional (through trusted
schools/parishes/clubs), not biometric — explicitly avoiding facial
scanning or biometric databases of minors.

**Section VII (the technical-adviser section) — the sharpest framing:**
"Algorethics in practice, at the level of design rather than
declaration" — three properties stated at the level of what/why, not
how: (1) verification is institutional not biometric; (2) intelligence
is governed by design not policy — proportionate memory that ages and
expires; (3) the seven domains are one connected organism, not a
collection of products — "the capabilities are not the asset; the
connectedness is, and the connection map is held" — this is explicitly
named as both the work's strength and its protection against being
reverse-engineered from a capability list.

**Section VIII — anticipated commercial-world response, useful context
for expectation-setting:** predicts warm public words but resistance in
four forms — pressure to keep regulation vague, hollow imitation of the
language without the structure, credibility attacks on the architecture
itself, and quiet acquisition offers "to shelve, not to build." Argues
four structural protections exist regardless: the design cannot be
replicated by an engagement-funded competitor without contradicting its
own revenue model; the engineering isn't public and is disclosed only
in stages under governance; ownership is singular and documented (one
author, one chain of title); and an asset held by a small company can
be outspent, one held in trust by an institution of global standing
cannot.

**Section IX — the governed examination process, worth noting for any
future correspondence:** "one author, one chain of title," examination
offered in stages (what may be read on introduction vs. what's shown
under confidentiality vs. what's delivered on commitment, held at
distinct levels), explicitly nothing about the engineering/mechanism
disclosed in any circulating document.
