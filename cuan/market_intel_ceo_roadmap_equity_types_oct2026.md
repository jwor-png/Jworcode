## Equity Type 01 — Common Stock
- The most basic form of equity. Founders and early employees typically hold this.
- Comes with voting rights, but sits at the **bottom of the payout order** — if the company sells, preferred stockholders are paid first.
- Key line: "You own a piece of the company, but your piece is the last to get liquid."

## Equity Type 02 — Preferred Stock
- What investors typically get.
- Comes with special rights: **liquidation preferences** (paid before common stockholders), **anti-dilution protection** (protects ownership if new shares are issued at a lower price), and sometimes the **right to block major decisions** (M&A, new funding rounds, etc.).
- Worked example given: a $50M exit — preferred stockholders take the entire $50M payout before common stockholders see a cent. Framed as "why a $50M exit can still leave founders with nothing."

## Capital structure order (simplified, from slide 2)
Paid first → paid last: **Preferred Stock → Convertible Notes → Debt → Common Stock.**
