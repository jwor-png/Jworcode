## What emerged from the sequence

ACCEPT → APPRECIATE → REBUILD → IMAGINE → LOOK WITHIN → FIND ENERGY → BE
YOURSELF → CONNECT → HOPE → KNOW YOURSELF → ACT

Condensed: **Know who you are. Appreciate what you have. Decide what
matters. Stop giving unnecessary power to doubt or other people's
judgement. Then do something about the life you want.**

Second conclusion: **people matter** — development shouldn't turn purely
inward; family, friendship, kindness, encouragement, apology, gratitude,
generosity and presence for others stay central.

**Deliberate challenge, not blind acceptance** — two examples:
- "Anything is possible" challenged as literally untrue. Better: **"Hope
  makes action possible when resignation would otherwise stop it."**
- "Don't chase people" doesn't mean relationships need no effort. Better:
  don't exhaust yourself seeking validation/approval, while still investing
  properly in relationships that matter.

## The key filter question

**"What deserves more of my attention now: something that matters, or
something that is merely occupying my mind?"** — proposed as a recurring
decision/personal-management filter, distinct from the Big Bets vs
day-to-day distinction already on `board.md` but related to it.

## How Cuan should use this material (John's own instruction)

Treat as **personal-development evidence, not instructions to adopt every
quotation.** Sort new material against the themes above into one of four
categories:
1. **Reinforcement** — confirms an established principle.
2. **Challenge** — questions an assumption John may hold.
3. **New insight** — reveals a previously unidentified theme.
4. **Action** — important enough that it should change what he actually does.

Objective: gradual identity integration, not accumulation of content.
