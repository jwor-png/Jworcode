## What Meridian Intelligence is

An **executive intelligence advisory** — not a software product, not a
generic AI consultancy. Meridian produces decision-support material for
John personally, amplifying roughly 40 years of frontline commercial
experience across law, finance, property, business development,
restructuring and AI investment. The core positioning line: **"Meridian
informs; it does not represent."** It gives a principal (John) a sharper,
faster, better-grounded basis for a decision — it never signs, files, or
acts on a client's behalf; that always goes to the appropriate qualified
professional.

**Principal:** John Webb O'Rourke, Chairman & Founder — four decades of
commercial pattern recognition, chairs every engagement personally. The
brand deliberately names a person, not a faceless firm.

**Ownership/structure:** 70/30 partnership — John (70%), Shane McCarthy
(30%, technical architecture and execution).

## The Seven Executive Intelligence Domains (external-facing term —
## always "Domains," never "Managers")

1. **Commercial & Deal** — transactions, partnerships, channel
   development, revenue models, deal evaluation, term sheets.
2. **Legal & Governance** — Irish law, corporate governance, directors'
   duties, contracts, employment, insolvency, regulatory compliance, EU
   AI Act, WRC.
3. **Finance & Restructuring** — cash flow, P&L, fundraising, tax
   structuring, distressed business, CVL, investor materials.
4. **Property & Development** — residential and commercial property,
   title, planning, development feasibility, asset structuring.
5. **AI Strategy & Adoption** — EU AI Act compliance, AI governance
   frameworks, adoption roadmaps, the Meridian Partners programme, the
   Golden Generation toolkit. **Most directly tied to Meridian's own
   revenue today**, and the domain currently being built out further for
   an agribusiness/farming-sector focus (see below).
6. **Business Transformation & Growth** — business analysis, value left
   on the table, new products, systems, growth roadmaps.
7. **AI Equity & Investment** — AI as an asset class, equity structuring,
   equity-for-AI-capability arrangements, AI-driven valuations. Marked
   explicitly as professional judgement, not established fact — a
   frontier area, not a settled product.

The seven domains, and the phrase **"Seven Executive Intelligence
Domains,"** are core to the identity — the site should make these
legible at a glance, not bury them in prose.

## What actually differentiates Meridian (the governance floor)

This is the credibility layer, and should be represented on the site as
substance, not small print:
- **Grounding rule** — no figure, ruling or valuation appears unless
  retrieved from a primary source.
- **Confidence tagging** on every claim: VERIFIED / REPORTED / UNVERIFIED.
- **Independent check** on every high-stakes output.
- **Status labelling**: DRAFT / IN REVIEW / DECISION-READY — nothing is
  treated as reliable until it earns that label.
- **Fail-closed near legal/financial/regulatory lines** — timeliness
  never overrides caution.

## Design direction already agreed (from the existing brief)

- **Reference sites John responded to**: Trifecta (editorial,
  magazine-style nav), Primary Studio (elegance + pricing transparency),
  Josh Kremer Consulting (minimalist, fast to skim).
- **Concept**: built around the literal meaning of *meridian* — a fixed
  line of reference. A thin brass "meridian line" motif, a coordinate
  line in the hero (e.g. `53.4239° N · 8.4854° W · EST. 2026`), and the
  seven domains numbered 01–07 like manifest entries.
- **Palette (placeholder, pending real brand assets)**: paper `#FAFAF8`,
  ink `#14181F`, brass accent `#A6791F`, in light mode; dark-mode
  equivalents already specified. Georgia/Iowan Old Style serif for
  display type (quiet, chambers-like authority); system sans for body.
- **A working prototype already exists** (built as an HTML artifact,
  since superseded by a fuller build on a dedicated branch) — worth
  showing the studio as a starting reference, not a locked design.
- **Real branding is still outstanding** — no confirmed logo/palette has
  been supplied into this session; the studio will need the actual
  brand assets from John directly (a `Meridian_Overview_Branded.pdf`
  and/or logo file exist in Google Drive, not accessible from here).
