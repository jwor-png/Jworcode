## What's legible

The question concerns **incapacity, ownership and decision-making** in a family business: if no Lasting Power of Attorney (LPA) exists and capacity has been lost, the alternative is to apply to the Court of Protection to appoint a deputy — described as a slower, more restrictive and more expensive route than planning ahead.
