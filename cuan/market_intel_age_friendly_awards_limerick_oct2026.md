# 2026 National Age Friendly Awards — tenth year, held in Limerick

Awards (organised by Age Friendly Ireland with Limerick City & County
Council, sponsored by IPB Insurance for its centenary year) recognising
local authorities, community organisations and individuals developing
practical responses to an ageing population. Named winning projects:
**Storm Ready Packs** (Longford, Older People's Council — solar power
bank, flask, LED head torch, emergency checklist/key contacts for
power outages); **Greenhills Grange** (Louth — Age Friendly design
built in from the outset: accessible/adaptable homes, supported living,
service/transport links); **Rock the Rails Family Store** (Clare,
Kilrush — mobile clothing rails and informal fashion shows for older
people, framed as a social/mobility-barrier solution). This year's
awards also carried EU-Presidency-associated significance, intended to
showcase Irish Age Friendly approaches for wider European learning.
