# €1.6bn data centre approved for Ennis, Co. Clare — 21 Oct 2026

**The project:** Art Data Centres Ennis Campus — proposed data centre
development in Co. Clare, ~40km west of Limerick.

**Scale, per the post:**
- 145-acre site on the Tulla Road, near Junction 13 on the M18
  motorway.
- Six 200,000 sq ft data centre halls — ~1.2 million sq ft total.
- ~200MW capacity, powered by grid connections and on-site energy
  generation.
- Construction expected to begin 2027.
- Headline figure: €1.6 billion.

**Verification note:** secondary social-media source (Ryan Hart/
ConstructionTrends.co), not the original planning decision or a
primary news outlet — figures and "approved" status not independently
cross-checked against An Bord Pleanála/Clare County Council records.
Treat the €1.6bn/200MW/145-acre figures as reported, not confirmed,
