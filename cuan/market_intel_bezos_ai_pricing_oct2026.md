## 1. "Jeff Bezos says a 3-day workweek and more single-income households are on the way thanks to AI" (Preston Fore, Fortune, 9 Oct 2026)

- **Core claim, headline quote:** "It's going to be difficult to hire
  people" — Bezos predicts AI will boost productivity enough that
  **fewer people will need to work**, enabling a **3-day workweek**
  and a return of more single-income households.
- **Context noted directly in the piece's own framing:** this comes
  **even after Amazon cut 30,000 jobs** — Bezos's prediction is framed
  against that backdrop, not in spite of awareness of it.

several pieces logged today (Innovation Minister Lawless's "AI hitting
the boardroom" warning, the legal-profession displacement concern) —
worth holding as a contrasting, more optimistic framing (shorter
working weeks, not just job losses) against the more cautionary
material already logged. Thin capture — only the headline and framing
