# "Abattoir job gave me skills to cut through financial jargon" — Shane Tobin, True Financial

Source: "How I Made It" column, Sandra O'Connell. VERIFIED named founder profile, mainstream Irish press.

## What it says

**Founder story:** Shane Tobin, from Bunclody, Co. Wexford, struggled academically at school, left after the Junior Cert. Got a job in a local abattoir at 16, working 6am-10/11pm — "an education in itself," discovering an inner drive to master every role before moving to another meat factory and doing the same. At 21, moved into selling income protection products, "answered an ad in the paper for a sales adviser, no clue what it was about" — discovered he was personable, a good talker who could build connections. Spent four years on the road cold-calling, became practically self-employed hitting sales targets, won successive promotions, headed a team of 50+ in his mid-twenties.

**Setback and rebuild:** his employer pulled out of Ireland during the 2009 recession, leaving him jobless with a wife, the first of four children, and forced to sell the family home — compounded by a bone tumour requiring hospitalisation (later confirmed benign). "I had to start again." Rebuilt via a job at a small financial services brokerage, qualified as a financial adviser, worked through the recession years providing financial planning to people in hardship like himself.

**True Financial founded:** by summer 2014, set up True Financial with just two staff, built on a mission to "ditch jargon and make financial planning as accessible as possible," including through technology (already using Microsoft Teams and digital signatures before Covid). By 2021: 16 staff, fast-growing customer base. Grown by acquisition over the past two years — bought Low.ie (2025) and Hello.ie (January 2026) — now 55 staff, generating 5,000+ inbound inquiries a month online.

**AI/HR detail, directly relevant:** the article notes True Financial "has signed up for Ainm to support his HR practices, with good results" — taken "a serious chunk out of our HR administration," saving an estimated **5-6 hours a week**, which the founder (quoted as "O'Donovan" in this section — likely an editing inconsistency in the original article, possibly referring to a different named business owner in the same feature, not Tobin himself) says adds up fast "for a business of our size, with 20 employees."

**Expert caution on AI and SMEs, same article (O'Sullivan, not otherwise identified):** "AI is really good at dealing with repetitive administrative work and finding information quickly. However, HR and recruitment are classed as high-risk uses of AI under the EU AI Act and they have to be careful."

**Closing line, Tobin's own:** "If you stand still, you're left for dust" — delivered, per the journalist, with his leg still jigging as he spoke, a callback to the opening description of his restless energy since childhood.
