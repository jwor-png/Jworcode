## 1. Gina London, "The Communicator": "Apply, rinse and repeat, works for shampoo and getting a message out"

Fourth Gina London column logged (earlier: inbox overload 30 Aug, AI disclosure, speak-up culture 4 Oct, and the presentation framework of 27 Sept).

- **Premise:** writing from a hotel outside Paris (UNLEASH HR conference), she admits she sometimes wonders whether she is just repeating herself. After her AIB session on intentional leadership in Dublin, a woman said she had attended a similar talk at the RDS for the Irish MBA Association; she still enjoyed the session, because different stories and examples helped her apply the ideas differently.
- **Point 1, repetition is how learning works:** understanding something is only the start; applying it consistently takes practice, reinforcement and repetition ("rinse and repeat" on the shampoo bottle).
- **Point 2, intentional beats authentic:** "authenticity" is used as a reason not to change behaviour ("that's just who I am"). She argues that intentionality means considering how words, voice and body language affect others, and adjusting.
- **Point 3, change the conversation in your head:** challenge the internal script ("I'm terrible at presentations", "everyone else knows more than I do"). Ask what evidence supports it, and reframe it ("I'm developing my presentation skills").
- **Point 4, the little things:** look up from your phone, remove your AirPods when someone speaks, notice your expression while listening, ask a question and actually hear the answer.
- **Her story:** she once dreamed of becoming the next Christiane Amanpour; CNN did not renew her contract, which led her to Ireland and her current work.

## 2. Partner content: "Navigating the Bay Area tech waters for Irish companies" (Grace Gavigan, market executive, Enterprise Ireland San Francisco)

- **What:** more than 30 Irish companies were in California last week, supported by Enterprise Ireland, at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 (San Francisco) and SemiCon West 2026; together the events drew more than 30,000 attendees.
- **TechCrunch Disrupt:** seven Enterprise Ireland-supported companies (Ailtire, Swippitt, Darkhunt AI, XBorderCo, Cocorev, Ardessa, FotoNation) showcased technology. The inaugural Irish Shark Tank at Ireland House drew more than 100 attendees (with a waiting list of over 200); judges named include Caoimhe MacRunnels (Reach Capital), Robert Fenton (CEO, Qualio) and Richard A Moran (venture capitalist and author). Swippitt also competed in TechCrunch's Startup Battlefield; XBorderCo founder John McCarthy (not Shane McCarthy) returned to the stage after a reported 200m dollar (180m euro) exit from his earlier venture Taxamo.
- **SemiCon West:** 15 Irish companies exhibited under the Silicon Island banner (Enterprise Ireland, IDA Ireland, Tyndall National Institute), across the semiconductor supply chain; Enterprise Ireland also hosted a reception at Ireland House with Intel, Applied Materials and Oracle among attendees.
