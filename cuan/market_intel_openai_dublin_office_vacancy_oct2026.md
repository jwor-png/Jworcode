# OpenAI's Tropical Fruit Warehouse deal was Q3 2026's largest Dublin office transaction

**The deal:** OpenAI's acquisition of more than **88,000 sq ft** at the
Tropical Fruit Warehouse was the **largest Dublin office market
transaction of Q3 2026**, and alone represented almost half of all
Dublin 2 take-up — accounting for 73% of the quarter's take-up across
the broader city-centre area (1 million+ sq ft under construction,
1.6 million sq ft brought to market in the first nine months of 2026,
per Colliers' latest report). Demand from technology and AI companies
helped drive Dublin's office vacancy rate **down to 13.4% in Q3 2026**,
from 14.3% a quarter earlier and 11.4% a year earlier (when a reserved-
space adjustment is excluded; otherwise 13.4% is still described as a
two-year high point for recovery). Kate Ryan, director and head of
research at Colliers, said "the ongoing flight to quality... is
becoming increasingly evident as businesses prioritise modern,
sustainable office environments that support collaboration and
long-term growth," with AI- and tech-driven headquarters establishment
increasingly cited as a factor.

**Other notable Q3 2026 transactions in the same report:** TikTok
leased 32,616 sq ft at Klaviyo's former space at One Le Pole Square,
Dublin 8; Grey Space (flexible workspace operator) acquired 39,649 sq
ft at Pembroke; Optum Services took 30,070 sq ft at Kilmore House,
Dublin 1; OpenAI itself also holds 88,261 sq ft at Tropical Fruit
(confirming the headline deal).

**Supply picture:** new supply remains uneven — only 1.1 million sq ft
of office space is under construction, 60% of which is either pre-let
or owner-occupied, limiting options for occupiers seeking large blocks
of reserved space; take-up for the full year is expected to exceed
10 million sq ft (broadly in line with the 10-year average of 2.5
million sq ft/quarter). Vacancy is falling but new supply isn't keeping
pace, and speculative construction remains unlikely to begin without
secure funding or a pre-let, pushing rents up (headline rents for prime
office space in the traditional central business district now around
€70/sq ft).
