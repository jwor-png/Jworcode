# Two more startups from the same Enterprise Ireland feature, 18 Oct 2026

## Wondr — healthy sweets, eyeing US/Middle East expansion

Dublin-based (Clondalkin) healthier jelly-sweets business, founded by
**Mark Birrane** in 2021. Six staff, €100,000 raised via Enterprise
Ireland's pre-seed start fund. Products: low sugar, low calorie, high
fibre, fat- and gluten-free, all-natural flavours and colourings.
Originally launched as a protein bar, rebranded to "Wondr" jellies
after consumer research suggested a sweets format would resonate more
than a chocolate-bar format. Went through New Frontiers and Enterprise
Ireland support; now aiming to expand into the US and Middle East over
the next 18 months.

## An Even Better Place to Work — Mayo-based employee engagement platform

Geesala, Co. Mayo-based business, founded 2022 by spouses **Orla and
Brian Bakeberg** (previously ran a consumer-products business in
Botswana/South Africa before relocating to Ireland). Five staff,
€1.1m raised to date. Pitch: "around 80% of employees are disengaged
and nobody is recognising this" — a diagnostic-survey-led platform (42
diagnostics) helping individuals understand how they're feeling and
build communication/leadership skills. Current footprint: some UK
education-sector clients (aiming for Ofsted-wide reach), a division of
Yorkshire Water, still-developing South Africa connections. Raising
€500,000 in a funding round expected to close next year; credits
Enterprise Ireland's Nextwave and We Grow programmes for founder
connections and support.
