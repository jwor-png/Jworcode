## 1. Instagram carousel: "Become a Master of Systems Thinking" (account satya.creat, labelled "AI content", slides 1 to 7 of 8 seen)
Five principles for understanding complex problems, attributed to Donella Meadows (environmental scientist, systems analyst, author of Thinking in Systems; the portrait on the slide is AI-generated, so treat the attribution as the poster's framing, not Meadows' own text):
1. **Pattern thinking:** look beyond individual events to the repeated patterns behind them; ask what conditions keep producing the same results.
2. **Systems thinking:** understand how things are connected (people, processes, resources, incentives); a change in one area creates effects elsewhere.
3. **Root cause thinking:** treat conditions, not symptoms; "the best solution changes what creates the problem".
4. **Long-term thinking:** consider delays, hidden costs and future effects; "a good decision should work beyond the moment it is made".
5. **Leverage thinking:** find the highest-impact change; changing an incentive, rule or process often beats adding effort; "the smartest solution is often the one that changes the system itself".
