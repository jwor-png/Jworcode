## Key distinctions (per the article)
- **Burnout (Maslach Burnout Inventory, three dimensions):** emotional
  exhaustion/energy depletion, depersonalisation (detachment,
  cynicism about work/colleagues), and a low sense of personal
  accomplishment despite effort.
- **Busy vs burnout:** people who are just busy bounce back quickly
  after a relaxed weekend; people with burnout don't recover with a
  couple of days' rest because they're too depleted.
- **Productivity:** burnout often brings a sudden, sustained fall in
  output; busy-but-fine people can still push through and manage.
- **Purpose:** burnout brings detachment and loss of sense of purpose;
  busy people still see the value in what they're doing.
- **Physiological:** chronic stress can alter the
  hypothalamic-pituitary-adrenal axis — the adrenal system is built for
  short bursts of stress, not sustained overload, and weakens under
  constant strain (Briffa's "battery and bank account" analogy: constant
  overdraft eventually needs restoring, not just topping up).

## Suggested countermeasures (per the article)
- Sleep and rest — the primary recharge mechanism.
- Breathing techniques (e.g. slower breathing with an extended out
  breath) to deactivate the stress response.
- Prioritise and schedule tasks explicitly, so the brain isn't holding
  open loops ("staring at the ceiling at 2am" vs scheduled and closed).
- Build in genuine cognitive rest — an activity where the brain is
  actually switched off (painting, meditation, music, sport).

## When to seek help (per the article)
Persistent tiredness, feeling like you've lost part of yourself, annual
leave not restoring you, or a sense you can't manage it all yourself
and it's getting worse (risk of depression/anxiety) — seek counselling
or professional support.
