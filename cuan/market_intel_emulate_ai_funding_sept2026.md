2. **A further, independent data point on the froth/scale question**
   already flagged elsewhere in Cuan's market intel (the Anthropic IPO

**Caveat, stated plainly:** this is a single short news brief with no
detail on Emulate's actual product, revenue, or team size beyond "former
Google DeepMind researchers" — not a like-for-like comparator to ODIN
without much more information. Flagged as a data point on market
sentiment, not as evidence ODIN's own valuation is either justified or
unjustified.
