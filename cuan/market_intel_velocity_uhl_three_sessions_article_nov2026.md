**What it says (the authors' claims, not independently checked):**
- Over the summer Velocity and its "sister brand in AI Governance, Ambrion AI" ran an AI training programme with UHL's senior leadership team: three sessions (June "what's possible", July "what is plausible", then practical demos). Before sessions each leader gave answers on how they work; a personal AI readiness profile was built for each, plus research on how hardware and builders' merchant groups in Ireland, UK, US, Australia and New Zealand use AI, mapped against what the EU AI Act asks of a group like UHL. "Every claim was checked independently by a second model."
- Session 1: anonymised group picture; scenario bank across six business areas; a pocket guide to five questions before trusting any output; habit exercises (briefing AI, chain of verification across two models, several lenses).
- Session 2: prompt as a briefing (four parts); live prompt design on each leader's own tasks; "AI can breathe efficiency but only with the right systems and mechanics around it"; human in the loop. Demo of an MVP finance desk orchestration built with Claude Code whose rule is it never guesses a number (calculations as code, shows working, flags doubt).
- Session 3: four testable demos for UHL: competitor intelligence (weekly, evidence-led, claims linked to source); a training needs studio; a marketing planning engine (a year of activity across the group's brands, people and AI agents on one plan); (fourth demo not read in the screenshots). "In every one of them, the decision stays with a person."
- Governance brought it together: chain of verification, divergent lenses, independent verifiers, cross-model analysis, adversarial teams, policies, guardrails; "you can't put AI in jail, so a name, a person, always sits on the output; if you wouldn't email it, don't paste it; and the proof rises with the stakes." Slide: "Never let it mark its own homework" (take the answer, open a clean session or second model, find what is wrong; three questions: where did this come from, can I check it against that source right now, whose name is on it).
