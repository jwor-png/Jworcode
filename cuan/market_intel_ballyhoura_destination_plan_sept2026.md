# Market intel — Draft Ballyhoura Country Destination Experience Development Plan (public notice)

**Source:** statutory public notice, "Star News" p.9, undated clipping
(period covering 17 September - 15 October 2026), Fáilte Ireland /
Ireland's Hidden Heartlands. VERIFIED as a genuine statutory notice
(standard SEA/Appropriate Assessment public consultation format), not
an opinion piece.

## What it is
Fáilte Ireland has prepared a **Draft Ballyhoura Country Destination
Experience Development Plan**, with an associated Strategic
Environmental Assessment (SEA) Environmental Report and an Appropriate
Assessment (AA) Natura Impact Statement, under EU/Irish environmental
assessment regulations (S.I. 435/2004, as amended; Directive
2001/42/EC; Directive 92/43/EEC as transposed).

**Public inspection window: 17 September to 15 October 2026**, via:
- Fáilte Ireland website
  (failteireland.ie/en/irelands-hidden-heartlands/ballyhoura-country-development-plan)
- Ballyhoura Development website
  (ballyhouradevelopment.com/listing/category/destination-experience-development-plan-dedp)
- In person at Ballyhoura Development offices: Kilfinane (Main St., Co.
  Limerick) and Mitchelstown (Forest Hall, New Sq., Co. Cork, P67 XY80)

Written submissions/observations must be marked "Draft Ballyhoura
Country Destination Experience Development Plan" and sent to Fiona
Dunne, Regional Development Manager, Ireland's Hidden Heartlands,
Second Floor, Fair Green House, Green Bridge, Mullingar, Co. Westmeath,
N91 V5WC, or by email to ihh@failteireland.ie, within the same window.
