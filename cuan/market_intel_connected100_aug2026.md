   cases"** (Irish Independent, 1 Sept). High Court/Court of Appeal

   and deepfakes, and Ireland's exposure as an EU/US tech hub. Relevant
   to Ambrion's governance pitch and Meridian's AI Strategy & Adoption
   domain.

   written off"** (Sunday Independent, 16 Aug). Full county-by-county
   breakdown of derelict site levies waived/collected/owed. Directly
   relevant to Tairseach's derelict-property vertical (already logged
   as LIVE PILOT status) — real, sourced data to strengthen that pitch.
4. **"Frustration grows as Limerick city falls behind in housing
   delivery"** (Business Post Property Plus, 30 Aug). 64% of
   Limerick's approved housing pipeline hasn't commenced; planning
   isn't the bottleneck, land/commencement is. Directly relevant to
   your own Kilmallock/Limerick local knowledge and Tairseach's
   property intelligence more broadly.
5. **"Meta's $18bn US fine won't change much for Irish children"**
   (analysis, Adrian Weckler). Meta's new EU teen-safety settlement —

   Relevant context for Childen's child-safety positioning (see
   `childen.md`) — shows the regulatory landscape it's arguing into.
