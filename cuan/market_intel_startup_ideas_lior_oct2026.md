# "10 Startups That Don't Exist Yet (But Need to in 2026)" — Lior Pozin

Source: Instagram carousel, verified account "lior" (Lior Pozin). UNVERIFIED — an individual's opinion/idea list, not a market-researched or independently tested set of opportunities.

## What it says

Framed as "the clearest openings I see right now. Steal one."

1. **AI Tax Filing** — gathers documents, finds every deduction, files taxes. "Not just another calculator."
2. **AI Error Insurance** — coverage for businesses when their AI agent makes a costly mistake, e.g. a wrong refund or payment.
3. **Adaptive AI Tutor** — adapts to each child in real time, "private-school quality help for any family."
4. **Chip Supply Tracker** — software tracking a chip across the dozen countries it passes through, replacing the spreadsheet.
5. **AI Farm Advisor** — expert farming advice in an app, built for small family farms that can't afford a consultant.
6. **AI Voice Receptionist** — natural-sounding phone assistant booking/rescheduling appointments for small offices, affordably.
7. **Trades Dispatch App** — scheduling and customer updates for plumbers/electricians, no office staff needed.
8. **Flat-Fee Will Service** — walks anyone through setting up a will and files it properly, for a flat fee.
9. **Space Material Mining** — a company that mines basic materials like silicon and titanium in space, to build things there.
10. **Revenue-Only Ad Agency** — builds and runs ads, reports only revenue, no retainers, no strategy meetings.
