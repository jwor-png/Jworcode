# Two minor items, logged briefly

**"Do I really need to start a pension in my twenties?"** (Mark Reilly,
pensions proposition lead, Royal London Ireland) — standard advice for
a new graduate on a company pension: starting a decade earlier can
outweigh contributing for longer from the mid-30s given compound
growth; explains the state's auto-enrolment (AE) scheme mechanics (€1
state top-up per €3 contributed, contribution rate rising 1.5%→6% over
10 years, capped at €80,000 salary) versus a company pension's
marginal-rate tax relief (40% for higher-rate taxpayers) — AE may suit
basic-rate taxpayers better, a company/private pension often better for
higher earners. General personal-finance reference, no direct action —
companion piece to the already-logged pension-optimisation material.

**"Top Property Jobs: Irish Planning Institute, IDA and EirGrid seek
talent"** (Oisín Gaffey) — weekly property-jobs roundup. Notable only
for **EirGrid hiring a Head of Land Management** (Dublin, leading
