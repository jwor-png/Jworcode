# Vale Star / Star News — two items, 21 Oct 2026

## 1. "€628 million in funding for Rural and Community Development and the Gaeltacht"

Minister Dara Calleary (Rural and Community Development, the
Gaeltacht) and Minister of State Jerry Buttimer announced a Budget
2027 package of **€628 million**, a €64m increase. Named allocations:
- **CLÁR scheme** — €12m.
- **Local Improvement Scheme** — increased by €5m.
- **Outdoor Recreation Infrastructure Scheme** — up to €32m in 2026,
  rising to €43m for 2027.
- **Town and Village Renewal Scheme** — €74m.
- **LEADER programme** — €74m, with the successor to Our Rural Future
  due to be published later this month.
- **SICAP** — €160m available in 2027 (up from €23m cited separately
  in the clipping — figures not fully reconciled, likely different
  line items; worth treating the headline SICAP figure with some
  caution until cross-checked).
- **Community Development Programme** — €260m total, up €5m, plus a
  further €1.5-2m for offshore islands/community integration.
- **Gaeltacht/Irish language funding** — up to €160m in 2027 (€17m
  increase), including North-South cooperation funding.

## 2. "3 Counties Beekeeping Association celebrates as Native Irish Honey Bee Society wins Georgina Campbell Award"

Local item: the Native Irish Honey Bee Society (national conservation
group; 3 Counties Beekeeping Association is a supporting member) won
a Natural Food Award at the Georgina Campbell Awards. Pauline Walsh
(Knocklong) quoted; Karin Stierle and Kieran Harnett of 3 Counties
Beekeeping Association pictured with RTÉ's Francis Brennan.
