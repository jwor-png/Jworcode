# Google "Forward" No. 05: "Architecting Europe's AI Future" (captured 9 Nov 2026)

**Source:** Forward, "European Perspectives on Tech & Society", No. 05 (goo.gle/forward-ie), a Google-published magazine issued at the weekend. John photographed the cover and pages 2 to 16 (sixteen photos). Pages 17 onward (including "Investing in Europe", p18) not yet captured. **Vendor-published and sponsored by Google: all claims unverified; treat as Google's framing.** Logged for noting only.

**Cover themes:** Security (sovereign clouds and resilient infrastructure), Sovereignty (integrating AI while maintaining control), Choice (interoperability and digital autonomy). Cover photo: Wendy Tan White and Torsten Kroeger of Intrinsic.

## What it says (page by page)
- **p2 Editorial, Vanessa Hartley (Head of Google Ireland):** "Partnership. Choice. Security." Argues Europe faces a false choice between autonomy and growth. AI could add 1.2 trillion euro to the European economy within a decade. Digital sovereignty is "more than controlling encryption keys": flexibility to run on multiple clouds. Cites Ireland's Office of the Revenue Commissioners (ORC) and Italian green-tech startup Ganiga as examples.
- **p3 Contents:** A Matter of Choice (p4); Konstantinos Komaitis interview (p7); Sovereign Perspectives (p8); Democratising Physical AI, Intrinsic (p10); A Catalyst for Autonomy and Success (p13); "Stop Focusing on What We Don't Have" (p16); Investing in Europe, data centres and cloud regions (p18).
- **pp5 to 6 A Matter of Choice:** Implement Consulting research commissioned by Google: wide AI adoption could lift EU GDP 1.2 to 1.4 trillion euro (8%) over ten years, mainly via productivity. Only about 20% of EU enterprises with 10+ employees use AI. IDC (Sept 2025): 37% of organisations worldwide use sovereign cloud solutions, a combined 44% plan to. Giorgia Abeltino (Google Cloud EMEA government affairs): sovereignty is "tangible, technical and verifiable control as well as open choice". Karen Massin (Google EU): "sovereignty has to be the power to decide under your own terms". Partner-led "Made with Europe" offerings: S3NS (Thales, France, SecNumCloud 3.2 qualified), Schwarz Group and T-Systems (Germany), PSN (Italy), Clarence (Luxembourg/Belgium), Telefonica (Spain), Telecom Italia (Milan region). Defence customers named: Bundeswehr, UK Ministry of Defence (air-gapped). Sovereign Cloud Portfolio box: Data Boundary (EU region, EU-only admin access, keys held outside Google), Dedicated (partner-run, can run if disconnected from Google), Air-Gapped (on-premises, no external connectivity).
- **p7 Interview, Konstantinos Komaitis (Atlantic Council):** defines sovereignty by properties (openness, interoperability, resilience), not borders. Warns of a "sovereignty trap": buying European is not the same as sovereignty; fragmentation into 27 approaches loses ground; over-regulation without an innovation agenda holds Europe back; resilience does not mean isolation; trust needs a predictable legal environment.
- **pp8 to 9 Sovereign Perspectives (voices):** Niamh Smyth (Irish Minister of State for Trade Promotion, AI and Digital Transformation): open and collaborative discussion on digital sovereignty. Mauro Capo (Accenture): winners will be those who deploy AI better, not only faster. Arba Kokalari (Swedish MEP, EPP): the best sovereignty is letting European companies scale globally. Milena Jaburkova (Czech Confederation of Industry): open but firm control and the ability to switch provider. Thibaut Kleiner (DG CONNECT): co-develop, make it here. Caroline Read (UK Deputy Ambassador to the EU): sovereignty is not being overly dependent on any one company or country.
- **pp10 to 12 Democratising Physical AI:** Intrinsic (joined Google early 2026, spun out of X in 2021; CEO Wendy Tan White, Chief Science Officer Torsten Kroeger) aims to make intelligent robotics affordable ("the Android of robotics"). Vision and touch for robots; European partners Trumpf and Martin Systems; most core applications run on-premises, cloud only for updates and training; German RoX consortium on sovereign data handling; humans become "enablers and orchestrators of automation".
- **p13 A Catalyst for Autonomy:** Omniscope (immune data bank, Google Cloud, sovereign controls) and Ganiga (waste intelligence, Gemini and Vertex AI, claimed 95% accuracy).
- **p14:** University Medical Center Freiburg (Google Cloud and T-Systems, patient data sovereignty) and **Ireland's Office of the Revenue Commissioners**: seven-year partnership with Google Cloud to modernise its data infrastructure, a unified AI architecture, Gemini as embedded assistant, all data stored in the EU, Revenue holds the encryption keys.
- **p15 Made with Europe:** country panels (France, Spain, Italy, Luxembourg/Belgium, Germany).
- **p16 Conversation (Marta Poslad, Google, and Daniel Friedlaender, CCIA Europe, at POLITICO Speakeasy, GLOBSEC Prague 2026):** over 60% of European businesses cite regulation as a reason for holding back investment; more than 100 regulations since 2019 affected the digital economy; the AI and digital omnibus simplification drive is "at the edge of a precipice".
