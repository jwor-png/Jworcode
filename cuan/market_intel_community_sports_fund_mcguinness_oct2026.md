# "Community Sports Facilities Fund 2026" — John McGuinness TD

Source: John McGuinness TD (Carlow-Kilkenny) official Facebook page. VERIFIED as a genuine TD's constituency-office notice on a national government fund, not an opinion piece.

## What it says

**Key dates:** registration opens 28 September 2026; applications open 19 October 2026; closing date 26 February 2027.

**Funding tiers:**
- **Local Grants:** up to €250,000 for individual clubs' capital projects; equipment funding capped at €70,000; property ownership not required for equipment-only applications.
- **Regional Tier 1:** €250,000-€550,000 for larger projects involving multiple clubs/sports, particularly all-weather field facilities.
- **Regional Tier 2:** €550,000-€750,000 for larger infrastructure projects, led by a Local Authority or National Governing Body (NGB) — individual clubs cannot apply at this tier.

**What's new this round:** a new grant management system ("SPRAOI"); a simpler application process; reduced lease requirements (from 15 years down to 10 years); stronger focus on transport collaborations and school partnerships; targeting areas that have historically been under-invested in.

**Who can apply:** voluntary/not-for-profit sports clubs, community groups, National Governing Bodies, Local Authorities, schools, diocesan trusts, education and training boards, third-level colleges.

**Contact:** johnmcguinness.ie, 056 777 0672, constituency Carlow-Kilkenny.
