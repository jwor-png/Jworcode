## The flight question — verified

**Confirmed via web search, 2 Sept 2026:** China Eastern Airlines
launched a direct Dublin–Shanghai route on **20 July 2026** — the
longest scheduled non-stop route ever operated from Dublin Airport.
Airbus A350-900, three times weekly (Mon/Wed/Fri as currently
scheduled). Backed by Ireland's Strategic Air Access Fund and Tourism
Ireland as part of a wider 2026 tourism push. Opens onward connections
from Shanghai to Beijing, Chengdu, Guangzhou, Kunming, Hangzhou, plus
Tokyo, Seoul, Bangkok, Bali.
[Sources: [BreakingNews.ie](https://www.breakingnews.ie/ireland/new-direct-dublin-to-shanghai-flight-announced-as-part-of-2026-tourism-push-1881627.html), [DETE/Minister Burke announcement](https://enterprise.gov.ie/en/news-and-events/department-news/2026/april/20260401.html), [Travel and Tour World](https://www.travelandtourworld.com/news/article/uvb4pq66ngzn/)]

**The geography problem worth flagging directly:** the China Uncovered
itinerary **starts in Beijing and ends in Shanghai**. A Dublin–Shanghai
direct flight is well-placed as the *return* leg (fly home from
Shanghai, where the tour ends) but not the outbound leg (you'd still
need to get to Beijing to start). Whether Wendy Wu Tours can book this
as an open-jaw (fly Dublin→Shanghai, transfer/fly internally to Beijing
to start, tour ends back in Shanghai for the flight home) — or whether
their package assumes a different routing entirely (e.g. via a hub like
Amsterdam/Frankfurt/Abu Dhabi to Beijing) — is a real question to put to
them directly, not something to assume either way.
