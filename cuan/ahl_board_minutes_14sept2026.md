# Associated Hardware Public Limited Company
## Minutes of a Meeting of the Board of Directors
### Held via Microsoft Teams on Monday, 14 September 2026, 12:00-12:30

**Present:** John Webb O'Rourke (Chairman), Shane Felle, Joseph Wallace,
Eoin Lynch, John Smyth, Emmet Cooney

All six directors attended.

## Business

The Board convened to consider the draft shareholder letter regarding
the proposed post-AGM meeting on the Company's premises at Magna
Business Park, following comments raised by Joseph Wallace on 10
September 2026 that the previously circulated draft could be read as
indicating the Board had already resolved in favour of a sale.

Having discussed the matter, the Board agreed that:

1. The letter to shareholders would not reference the Board's
   position on a potential sale, and would instead be framed as a
   neutral notice from the Chairman advising of the date, time, and
   purpose of the meeting.
2. The Board's internal position (in favour of proceeding with a
   sale, subject to strong shareholder support, targeted at
   approximately 75%) would not be stated in the shareholder
   communication, and would instead be discussed directly with
   shareholders at the meeting itself.

**Resolved:** That the letter be issued in the revised form agreed,
and that this meeting be recorded as a formal meeting of the Board of
Directors of the Company.

Signed: _________________________
John Webb O'Rourke, Chairman
