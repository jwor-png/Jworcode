Source: John's photograph, 6 Nov 2026 (Comment section, page photographed sideways; text partly legible). Opinion column. Claims are the author's, not verified by Cuan.

## What it says
- In a world of always-on messaging, writing a letter is a radical way of slowing down and getting your message across. Bombarded by emails, Insta and Facebook DMs and WhatsApp, a weekly handwritten letter feels like respite from a noisy world.
- She joined the online Sunday Letter Project, which encourages people to write one letter a week. It does not specify who you write to; it can be a friend, a relative or yourself.
- The physical act of writing a letter does not take long: about 20 minutes. Handwriting is slow, and she describes it as an act of resistance to speed.
- She cites neuroscientific studies, as she describes them, saying handwriting creates a richer memory footprint than typing and slows the pace of thinking.
- She keeps a box of letters from loved ones as her most precious possessions; the cost is a stamp, the return can be an unexpected reply.
- Tied to AI: as AI changes how we live, she is struck that our handwriting, like a fingerprint, is unique, and that the effort of a handwritten letter is "innately human."
