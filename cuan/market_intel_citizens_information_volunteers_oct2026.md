# "Citizens Information volunteers 'downgraded' and let go 'without warning' as centres close" — Azmia Riaz

Source: Irish Independent, named journalist, citing named former volunteers/staff (Enid O'Dowd, Ellen McCafferty), TDs (Jennifer Whitmore, Jennifer Murnane O'Connor), Minister Dara Calleary, and a Citizens Information Board (CIB) spokesperson. VERIFIED mainstream press report.

## What it says

**The pattern:** many Citizens Information Centre (CIC) locations have shut or consolidated since the pandemic, with volunteers reporting being "downgraded" to administrative tasks (answering phones, filing) or let go entirely without warning, despite long service and professional backgrounds.

- **Enid O'Dowd** (retired chartered accountant) volunteered at Rathmines CIC for ~10 years from 2012, running a specialist tax/money advice service. Post-Covid, told she could not return to her role, given no reason; later learned other volunteers had similarly been "kept on but downgraded to just answer the phone or file things." Rathmines CIC closed in 2022; remaining staff moved to a new Usher Street office — O'Dowd calls it "way down the quays," illogical as a public-facing advice location, somewhere she'd "feel uneasy."
- **Structure:** CIB (under the Department of Social Protection) funds a network of eight independently-operated Citizens Information Services (CIS), which operate 90 CICs nationally, 24 outreach locations, 11 prison in-reach services. Minister Dara Calleary confirmed volunteers were not active in CIS during Covid and the services are "actively seeking and supporting new volunteers."
- **Regional closures cited:** Wicklow (Blessington, Laragh closed; TD Jennifer Whitmore says Wicklow's service is "essentially coming out of just Bray" now, down from multiple locations); Carlow-Kilkenny (Callan, Graiguenamanagh, Tullow, Baltinglass outreach locations closed, per TD Jennifer Murnane O'Connor).
- **Whitmore's assessment:** "I don't understand why we are trying to fix something that isn't broken... a fantastic example of a really good public service... having fewer centres would affect those who are 'vulnerable' in some communities" — people who can't access a bus, can't drive, or can't use a phone lose access to in-person advice.
- **Ellen McCafferty** (worked at Rathmines CIC for a decade, continued as paid staff after volunteer numbers were reduced): "I'm still not quite sure what happened there." Describes the Rathmines closure as "a terrible mistake" — many served couldn't use a phone or lacked the digital literacy the new system requires.
- **CIB's own position (spokesperson):** volunteers "remain absolutely integral to Citizens Information, with people volunteering in both frontline roles and as directors of the Citizens Information Service companies." In 2025, CIS assisted **384,000+ people** and responded to **708,000+ queries** on social welfare, housing, health, employment and family matters. Recent initiative: a Digital Help Service for face-to-face assistance accessing public services online; a virtual CIC operates in Wicklow Town Library on Thursdays, 2-4pm.
