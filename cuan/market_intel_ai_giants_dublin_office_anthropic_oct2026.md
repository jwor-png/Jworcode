# AI-native firms taking twice as much Dublin office space as in the previous decade combined

**Headline finding (CBRE data):** "AI-native occupiers'" take-up of
Dublin office space has been **more than twice as high in 2026 as in
any previous year in the last ten years**. In total, AI firms account
for about **7% of total office take-up so far in 2026**; in Q3 2026
alone, AI firms accounted for **38% of total new take-up** in Dublin
(52,218 sq m new take-up that quarter, down 32% on the same quarter
last year — CBRE notes 2025's comparative quarter was the "strongest
third quarter since 2016").

**Named companies and moves:**
- **OpenAI** announced plans (Monday, per the piece) to create 200 new
  jobs in Ireland and set up a new "applied AI lab" at its EU
  headquarters in Dublin.
- **Anthropic** — "the frontier AI lab behind Claude" — was revealed in
  September (per earlier Business Post reporting) to be **searching for
  office space in Dublin with capacity for up to 1,000 staff**.
- Three of five recently completed Grade A buildings in Dublin 2 (each
  offering 50,000+ sq ft) are being let to "AI companies," per CBRE —
  OpenAI and Anthropic are "only part of the picture," with CBRE noting
  "four AI companies" have announced plans to create roughly 790 new
  roles in Dublin between them.
- OpenAI is adding 200 roles (on top of the 250 announced in July,
  taking its total commitment to at least 450). Anthropic plans 200 new
  roles by 2027. The Irish-founded Intercom (referenced as "times")
  plans 100 new roles over the next two years.

**Market context:** CBRE's Colin Richardson (head of research and
consultancy) and Daniel Shannon (head of tenant advisory) both quoted —
AI-native occupiers have taken more office space this year than in the
previous ten years combined; with less modern/large-floorplate choice
in the city core, activity has picked up in Docklands and north-city
schemes; occupiers with larger requirements should start searches early
given limited new supply (only five completed Grade A Dublin 2
buildings over 50,000 sq ft).
