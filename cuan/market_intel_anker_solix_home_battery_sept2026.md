## What it says
Pitches the Anker SOLIX XE whole-home battery backup system plus the
"Backup Auto-Switch" as a way for Irish households to manage rising and
increasingly complex electricity tariffs (dynamic/time-of-use pricing,
EV charging demand, solar adoption). Claimed features:
- AI-powered energy management system, "870 dynamic tariff providers"
  across Europe, real-time electricity price info used to
  automate charging/discharging around cheaper off-peak windows.
- XE unit: 7.5kW battery, 10,000 charge cycles claimed, IP66-rated,
  operating range -20°C to 55°C, LiFePO4 (C5-Mani) chemistry, thermal
  management claimed.
- Backup Auto-Switch: whole-home backup during outages, 0ms switchover
  claimed, output up to 7.5kW for 10 seconds and 10kW continuous
  (as printed), quick-connect wiring, DIN rail mount.
- Smart Meter Gen 2: single-slot design, auto-correcting wireless
  connectivity claimed.
- Modular "Flex Series"/"eXtreme Series" home battery line, stackable
  up to 6 modules for 42kWh total, 5kW rated output (per unit, as
  printed).
- Company framing: "Brand Mission — to enable everyone everywhere with
  energy independence, to power a sustainable future together," quoted
  from Aaron James Darvill, strategic sales director at Anker SOLIX.
