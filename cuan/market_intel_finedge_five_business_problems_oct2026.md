# FinEdge: "5 Business Problems Every Founder Must Know How to Solve" (Instagram infographic, captured 27 Oct 2026)

Source: Instagram post by finedge.theedulaw (FinEdge), about two days old at capture, screenshotted by John 27 Oct 2026. Educational infographic; no data or sources, so treat it as a checklist of frameworks, not evidence.

| Problem | Tool the graphic names | How to use it (as stated) | When to use it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 Falling sales | Funnel leak analysis (example: 1,000 leads, 300 calls or demos, 90 proposals, 30 closed deals) | Track conversion at each stage; find the biggest drop-off; fix one weak stage before changing everything | Sales slowing and you do not know whether the issue is lead quality, sales process, pricing or closing |
| 2 Cash flow crunch | 13-week cash flow view (weekly inflows, outflows, closing cash, mark the gap weeks) | List expected receipts and fixed payments; mark the weeks with cash gaps; delay, reduce or renegotiate before the crunch | Revenue looks healthy on paper but the business is constantly short of cash |
| 3 Low profit margins | Margin waterfall (revenue, direct costs, gross profit, operating costs, net profit) | Break profit into major cost buckets; find the cost line eroding margin the most; improve pricing, procurement or efficiency at that point | The business is selling but money is not staying in it |
| 4 Customer churn | Churn diagnosis map (activation, value, support friction, renewal trigger) | Review drop-offs by stage; call lost customers and ask what failed; fix one retention driver at a time | Customers sign up but do not stay, renew or reorder |
| 5 Operational bottlenecks | Throughput bottleneck map (demand, intake, execution, review, dispatch) | Map each step end to end; measure wait time, not only work time; increase capacity at the slowest step first | The team is busy all day but work still moves slowly |
