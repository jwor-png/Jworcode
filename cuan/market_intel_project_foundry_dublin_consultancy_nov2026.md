# Emmet Ryan: "Dublin consultancy looks to double revenue on AI wave" (Project Foundry)

Source: Business Post (business pages); date not visible on this page, photographed in the same sitting as the 1 Nov 2026 Business Post clippings, so very likely the 1 Nov 2026 edition (unconfirmed). Captured by photograph from John, 1 Nov 2026. Company profile with the founder's own claims; figures not independently verified. Other stories on the page (a farming profile, an automation piece and a pharmacy story) were only partly visible and are not logged.

## What it says

- **The company:** Project Foundry, a Dublin-based project management firm, founded in **2015** by **Declan Ryan and David Laird**. **30 permanent staff** and over **100 contractors**. Revenue **16 million euro in 2024**; it anticipates annual revenue above **30 million euro by the end of 2028**.
- **What it does:** "We bridge the gap between strategy and execution." It turns a client's strategy into tangible plans and large transformation programmes, usually working with the client's chief information officer or their team: typically a client presents three problem statements and Project Foundry brings a team to deliver them, including building a transformation or programme office with governance and best practice. Clients increasingly come for the technology side.
- **Origin:** Declan Ryan previously worked at Ergo in technical roles and saw a gap in Ireland for specialist project and programme services; the idea took shape on a break travelling.
- **Growth history:** phase 1, 2015 to Covid, took revenue to about **4 million euro**; David Laird stepped out in 2020 with a **share buy-back**; since Covid revenue grew to **16 million euro**, headcount rose and new services were added.
- **AI strategy:** with AI disrupting the industry, Ryan is launching **its own platform** and has a **partnership with ServiceNow**. "We're not going to sit still; we want to be part of that revolution in AI both internally and in helping our clients."

## Relevance (Cuan's read)

- **The Noesis project management system:** a live Irish comparator in the same space (project and programme delivery with governance) that is building its own AI platform; useful context for how the group's own system might be positioned, and a reminder that incumbent firms are moving into AI-led delivery.
- **Meridian, Velocity AI and Ambrion AI:** a benchmark for an Irish services firm (30 staff plus 100 contractors, 16 million euro) and its CIO-led buying route; ServiceNow partnership as a channel model.
- **Caveats:** founder interview; no independent check on revenue or the 2028 target.
