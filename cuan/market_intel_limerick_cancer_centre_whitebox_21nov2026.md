# Local newspaper (Limerick), c. 21 Nov 2026: "New Cancer Centre for Limerick" (Ballygrennan, Moyross)

Source: photographed newspaper article sent by John (21 Nov 2026, evening; paper title not shown). As reported, unverified by Cuan.

## What it says
- A 30,000-square-foot Oncology Centre at Ballygrennan, Moyross (West Limerick) has been completed in under 18 months and within budget by **WhiteBox Property Developments** (Managing Director **Martin White**, Adare).
- Handed over to **Aedifica**, a Belgian-listed property investment company specialising in healthcare real estate across Europe. Once fitted out it will be run by **CROAL**, a UPMC and Bon Secours joint venture, for cancer-treatment services.
- Described as the first significant private-sector investment in the Moyross regeneration area and the first element of Whitebox's wider healthcare campus: a 90-bed nursing home (starting Q1 2027, to be operated by the Mowlam Group), a primary care centre (planning permission secured), further master-planned lands, and 115 new homes for healthcare staff (with Limerick City and County Council and Clúid: cost-rental, affordable and social housing) plus a neighbourhood centre (coffee shop, drive-through pharmacy). The site is meant to serve Limerick, Clare and North Tipperary.
