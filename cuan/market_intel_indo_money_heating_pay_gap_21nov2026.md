Source: three photographed pages sent by John (21 Nov 2026, evening). As reported, unverified by Cuan.

## 1. "Nine mistakes that force your heating to work harder and drive up your bills" (Meadhbh McGrath, p36 and continuation)
1. **Insufficient insulation** (Sinéad Crowley, interior architect, west Cork): walls, floor, roof; focus on one area first, often the attic; borrow a thermal imaging camera from the library; seal sockets, light fixtures, chimneys (chimney balloon) and extractor fans; keep the attic hatch airtight and insulated.
2. **Neglecting windows** (Lynne O'Loughlin, interior designer, ATU Galway): reseal old windows; stick-on thermal film from about 10 euro on Amazon as a temporary fix; ideal is Passivhaus-rated triple glazing.
3. **Wrong curtains:** do not overlap radiators under windows; heavy thermal floor-length curtains where there is no radiator; close at dusk, open by day for solar gain.
4. **Orientation:** maximise solar gain; warm tones in north-facing rooms (Celine Gill, Limerick-based interior designer: warm lighting and rugs make a room feel warmer).
5. **Cold materials:** good heat conductors (tile, stone, concrete) suit underfloor heating; check wood and laminate are compatible; natural materials (wool, cork, timber, linen) feel warmer.
6. **Blocking radiators** with furniture or covers.
7. **Radiators too big** use more energy than needed: size correctly.
8. **Poorly spaced seating** in open-plan rooms: bring people together.
9. **Not going A-rated:** heat recovery systems and heat pumps need learning (timers, temperatures lower upstairs; do not open doors and windows).
- Use for John: home and property (Bantry, family home); low priority; if heating bills are a concern, start with the attic, windows and curtains.

## 2. Money Coach (Morgan O'Connell, p37): "A male work colleague is earning 10k more than me for the same job"
- Equal pay for equal work or work of equal value; EU Pay Transparency Directive (adopted 2023) and Ireland's Employment Equality Acts 1998 to 2015.
- **Ireland's Gender Pay Gap Portal: mandatory reporting due 30 November** for employers with 50 or more employees: hourly pay, bonuses, benefits in kind and pay distribution by quarter.
- Advice to the reader: ask HR for a full pay review without revealing the payslip she saw; focus on how pay is determined, when last reviewed, benchmarks and explanations; research market value; escalate through union, solicitor or the Workplace Relations Commission if unsatisfied.
