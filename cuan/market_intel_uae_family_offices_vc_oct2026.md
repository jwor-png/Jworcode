# "58% of UAE family offices are actively writing cheques into startups" — Taha Memon

Source: Instagram carousel, account tahadawat, tahamemon.com. **UNVERIFIED as a researched source** — a self-promotional lead-generation post (ends with "Comment Fci for our market entry guide"), figures and firm details not independently checked by Cuan. Treat ticket sizes, AUM figures and "how to approach" claims as REPORTED, not confirmed, and verify directly before relying on them for an actual approach.

## Headline claims

- **$400bn** sitting in UAE family offices, claimed as an underused/overlooked capital source relative to institutional VC.
- **700+ family offices registered in DIFC** (Dubai International Financial Centre) alone.
- **58%** of ME family offices are actively deploying into venture/growth-stage companies (cited to "Campden Wealth 2025" — 83% of ME family offices in PE/VC generally).
- Claimed structural advantages over institutional VC: no 7-year fund-cycle pressure, no LP committee approval, can move from first meeting to term sheet in **2-6 weeks**, can hold indefinitely (not exit-driven), can follow on indefinitely (Series A→B→C from the same office is described as common).

## Named family offices profiled (ticket sizes/sector theses as claimed — unverified)

1. **CE-Ventures (Crescent Group)** — family office of HH Sheikh Hamdan. $83bn AUM claimed; CE-Ventures itself $500M+ deployed. Portfolio: Vezeeta (HealthTech, MENA), Kitopi (cloud kitchens, unicorn), Anomali (cybersecurity, US). Thesis: HealthTech, FoodTech, enterprise/B2B SaaS, logistics, consumer tech with GCC relevance; strong preference for Series A/B operational proof. Ticket: $5M-$30M primary. Not publicly listed/no cold form — route via DIFC-registered intermediaries or existing Series A co-investors.
2. **DAMAC Capital** — family office of Hussain Sajwani (DAMAC Properties founder). $10bn AUM claimed. Described as "most aggressive AI investor among UAE family offices by deal count" — confirmed positions claimed in Anthropic ($50M, 2024), xAI, Mistral AI, plus $1bn+ in data centre infrastructure (UAE, US, UK). Thesis: foundational AI, LLMs, data infrastructure, GPU compute — explicitly not consumer tech/SaaS. Ticket: $25M-$200M, pre-IPO/late-stage growth. Approach: principal-level intro only, via ADGM-registered advisors or Anthropic/Mistral ecosystem co-investors.
3. **YBA Kanoo Group** — 200-year-old Gulf conglomerate (shipping, real estate, machinery, travel since 1890). $20bn AUM claimed. Direct VC since 2019; portfolio includes CorrosionRADAR (industrial IoT, Cambridge spinout), Retalio (retail tech), Singapore Gulf Bank. Thesis: industrial tech, maritime, logistics, cross-border trade infrastructure, B2B SaaS for industrial sectors. Ticket: $2M-$20M. Deep GCC + South Asia network.
4. **Abu Dhabi Capital Group** — $20bn AUM claimed, ADGM-registered. Portfolio includes Boom Supersonic, ALEF Education (UAE national curriculum EdTech, government-backed), Investera. Thesis: deep tech, aerospace, EdTech, sustainable mobility — UAE-aligned sectors where government is a potential customer; co-invests with sovereign entities on strategic mandates.
5. **2PointZero (Al Ghurair family)** — $27bn AUM claimed, DIFC-based. Described as the most flexible ticket range of any named UAE family office: $1M-$200M per deal, no sector exclusions if fundamentals are strong. Active in AI, mining, digital assets, crypto infrastructure, FinTech.
6. **Royal Group** — HH Sheikh Tahnoon bin Zayed. **$164bn AUM claimed — described as the largest UAE family office by AUM.** Deploys through G42 (AI, sovereign AI infrastructure), International Holding Company (IHC, Abu Dhabi-listed, $250bn+ market cap), and direct mandates. Anchor LP in many Abu Dhabi VC funds; strategic co-investor for companies needing UAE/Abu Dhabi government alignment. **Explicitly stated as "not approachable cold" — intro only through the G42 ecosystem or ADGM connections.**

## How the post frames access (its own claimed "rules," not independently verified)

1. Set up a DIFC or ADGM entity *before* any outreach (DIFC: 700+ family offices registered, proximity matters; ADGM: startup-friendly SPV structure).
2. Target specific events where principal-level introductions happen organically: DIFC FinTech Summit, Abu Dhabi Finance Week (ADFW), SuperReturn MENA, SALT Conference Abu Dhabi.
3. Route through DIFC-registered advisory firms with existing relationships: EFG Hermes, Arqaam Capital, Shuaa Capital, Alvarez & Marsal ME.
4. Reference specific portfolio companies directly relevant to the pitch, not a generic deck.
5. Identify co-investors with shared portfolio companies — named UAE VCs cited: Global Ventures, Wamda Capital, Middle East Venture Partners.
6. Principal-driven decision-making (1-2 family members or a CIO, no consensus requirement) means one strong relationship can outweigh twenty warm introductions; sector conviction tied to the principal's own business background matters more than the deck itself (e.g. YBA Kanoo backs industrial tech because they run shipping; DAMAC backs AI because Sajwani is personally convicted on it — "pitch the person, not the slide").
7. Structure flexibility: family offices can use convertible notes, SAFEs or direct equity, and can discuss valuation at the first meeting.
