# Oisin Gaffey: "Irish wealth managers warn investors off Anthropic" (News, p6)

Source: Business Post, 1 Nov 2026, page 6 (sub-headline: market "spooked" by tech share rollercoaster and "cash burning" as first AI firm set to float). Captured by photograph from John, 1 Nov 2026. Figures as reported and as read from the photo; not verified against the filing. Cuan itself runs on Claude, so this is logged neutrally as press commentary, not advice.

## What it says

- **Recommendation:** Irish wealth managers have recommended that clients avoid Anthropic's IPO, calling the company "not good value" and questioning its "cash burning" and business model. Aidan Donnelly, head of global equities at Davy: "I can't make the argument for it. Do I think it is good value? No I don't."
- **Valuation:** Anthropic was valued at **$183bn** just over a year ago (**$13bn** Series F). Investors now expect it to seek **$1.5 trillion to $1.75 trillion** when it floats. Donnelly says the lab "is not making any money. It's burning though shed loads of cash" and that the revenue runways it touted are "unreliable". There is "very very little" appetite among Davy clients; the IPO is expected in **mid-November**, with no exact date.
- **Filing figures:** Anthropic filed IPO documents last Monday, a step towards being the first company focused primarily on developing advanced AI models to go public. It had an **$80.7bn (72.2bn euro) net loss on revenue of $16.3bn** in the first six months of 2026, against a **$13bn net loss on revenue of $1.2bn** in the same period last year, per the filing as reported.
- **Retail involvement and SpaceX:** Donnelly sees the involvement of retail investors as a warning sign and notes similarities with Elon Musk's SpaceX listing in June (IPO price $135; above $200 soon after; low of $108 within two months). "If [Anthropic] was going to be an absolutely great thing, there's no way retail would be allowed into it, it would be kept among institutions."
- **Market backdrop:** around **$3 trillion (2.7 trillion euro)** wiped off the Nasdaq 100 in 24 days since its last peak. Donnelly: "the animal spirits have been quenched, people are getting spooked."
- **Others:** Mike O'Sullivan (Unio Wealth Management): the prospectus enlivened discussion of AI infrastructure economics but came as key players signalled a slowdown in chip-related revenues, which "might well temper" the IPO; he is focused on diversification and bonds are "having a nice rally". Bernard Walsh (Fairstone Ireland): received "a number of queries"; would not advise clients to buy as a standalone investment, which "was not a judgment on Anthropic" but reflects how Fairstone invests. AI "isn't a one way bet". Cantor Fitzgerald, a bookrunner, says its Irish clients were "certainly expressing interest".
- **Credit:** last Friday Blackstone told investors Anthropic might not receive an investment-grade rating immediately after listing; it told investors in Anthropic's latest **$63bn chip financing deal** that the company was expected to get a "high" junk rating from major agencies. Anthropic was contacted for comment.
