# "YC Request for Startups (Fall 2026)" — 13 named ideas

Y Combinator's own published "Request for Startups" list — a primary,
credible source (not a social-media invention), directly relevant
given the already-logged `market_intel_yc_2026_batch_oct2026.md`
(YC's batch composition trends) from earlier today.

## The 13 ideas, as listed

1. **The Privatutor** — teach young children to read, write, and do
   arithmetic at the quality of a private tutor, at consumer scale.
2. **The Future of American Defense** — low-cost interceptors,
   next-gen sensors, drones, satellite logistics, advanced
   manufacturing.
3. **A Cloud for Small Software** — a cloud built specifically for
   purpose-built tools that will only have one or a handful of users.
4. **Multiplayer AI** — AI that's multiplayer by default, where
   multiple people can watch the same live agent session, redirect it,
   and [collaborate — text partially obscured].
5. **Compute at Sea** — move compute offshore into [floating?]
   facilities operating together as one global cloud.
6. **AI-Powered Consumer Products for 1 Billion People** — [building]
   for a billion people as intelligence gets good enough and
   [cheap/accessible — partially obscured].
7. **AI for the Aging Population** — voice [interfaces?]/caregiver
   coordination software built for elder [care].
8. **New Operating Systems for the F[uture?]** — between AI agents,
   robots, and humans.
9. **The Best Time to Build in Crypto[?]** — agentic commerce, trading,
   and instit[utional-grade infrastructure — partially obscured].
10. **Data for the Real World** — new ways to collect dense
    physical-world data to enable precise modeling for energy,
    agriculture, logistics, and conservation.
11. **Proving You're Human** — rebuild the trust layer of the
    internet, knowing that's a verified human on the other end of a
    call, message, or transaction.
12. **AI-Native Compliance Infrastructure** — compliance infrastructure
    that consolidates fragmented tools and reduces reliance on
    specialized headcount.
13. **Self-Maintaining APIs** — agents that don't just announce API
    changes but scan customer codebases and open a PR with the fix.

Several items (4, 6, 9) were partially obscured by the video overlay
in the captured frame — logged as closely as legible, not guessed at
beyond what's shown.

## Cross-reference

Two items are directly, materially relevant to live Cuan threads:

- **#12, AI-Native Compliance Infrastructure** — "compliance
  infrastructure that consolidates fragmented tools and reduces
  reliance on specialized headcount" is almost exactly the shape of
  the Noesis governance work already underway (the ten-task mandate,
  company records/compliance calendar, CRO strike-off enforcement risk
  already logged). Worth noting YC is explicitly naming this as a
  category it wants funded right now — relevant market-validation
  context if Meridian or a future product ever considered packaging
  that governance discipline as a sellable tool.
- **#10, Data for the Real World** (physical-world data for energy,
  agriculture, logistics, conservation) is directly adjacent to
  **Tairseach's** property-intelligence thesis (energy/data-centre
  land, derelict property, site sourcing — all physical-world data
  problems) — useful as independent confirmation that YC sees this
  category as live and fundable right now, reinforcing the "why now"
  framing already in Tairseach's own one-pager.
