# "Prime site in Carrigaline has asking price of €3.2m"

Source: Cork print newspaper article (masthead not fully visible), agents Cushman & Wakefield. VERIFIED as a reported listing, asking price not a confirmed sale price.

## What it says

- An 18-unit, two-bed apartment site in the heart of Carrigaline has launched to market with a guide price of €3.2m — 4.5 acres, where the guide price equates to €711,111 per acre.
- Site has approval for a mixed-use development (86 housing units, including 18 two-bed apartments, 17 three-bed duplex units, 11 two-bed duplex units, 9 two-bed units, 17 two-bed own-door ground-floor apartments, 12 one-bed own-door ground-floor apartments, 19 one-bed apartments), a public plaza, car and bicycle parking, 47 and 174 respectively, pedestrian access, four commercial units.
- Of the 86 homes, 17 will be set aside for social and affordable housing as required by planning legislation.
- Context comparables cited in the same piece: Boar's Head Ltd obtained planning permission for the mixed-use scheme last October on a 4.62-acre (1.9ha) site in Carrigaline, valued at €2.5m, potentially accommodating up to 70 residential units. Boar's Head's remaining land in two lots (2024: 4.5 acres to Cushman & Wakefield, the westernmost plot deemed dependent on a drive-thru/fast-food chain McDonald's, currently under compulsory purchase) sold for an overall guide price of €2.9m; Love Shipton Group/Barry Collins (local Super Valu retailer/hotelier) was the purchaser.
- Aldi earlier bought 8 acres from a German supermarket budget chain last October, submitting a master plan for their own site as part of a €10m store, which returned surplus land — 5.21 acres — to market in 2023 at a guide price of €2.9m. Several developers have been active in Carrigaline; housing demand is high, including Astra Construction, Ruden Homes and Bridgewater Homes.
