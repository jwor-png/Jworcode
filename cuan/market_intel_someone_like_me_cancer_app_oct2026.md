# "Someone Like Me, Your Cancer Community" — Vanessa Mooney's app and podcast

**The founder:** Vanessa Mooney, 47, background in graphic design, a
cancer patient herself (breast cancer, diagnosed Christmas Eve 2019,
full mastectomy, chemo/radiation during Covid; mother died of lung
cancer ~2016, sister Veronica also died of lung cancer, Jan 2026,
diagnosed two months before Vanessa's own diagnosis).

**The app:** free, private peer-support app for cancer patients,
survivors, family members and carers — "for people at every point of
cancer": newly diagnosed, in active treatment, finished treatment and
living with fear of recurrence, or living with secondary/metastatic
cancer. Family/loved ones get their own separate space.

**How it works:**
- Matches people nearby by cancer type and location; connection only
  happens once both people have consented (a "digital wave" at each
  other), then becomes a private 1:1 chat.
- Can filter by cancer type (primary vs advanced cancers can choose to
  connect only with others at the same stage).
- No open forum, no group chat/social-media-style exposure — explicit
  design reaction against Facebook-style groups: "I am terrified of
  Facebook and all the group chats... there's so much misinformation
  out there."
- **AI moderator built in** to monitor conversations — flags/reviews
  content like unproven cure claims ("this tea cured my cancer") or
  people trying to sell products on their profile, checked against the
  app's own guidelines. **A concrete, live example of AI content
  moderation in a sensitive health-support context** — directly
  relevant to Meridian's AI Strategy & Governance domain as a working
  case study, separate from the Midwestern Cancer Foundation angle
  below.
- "Shared story" feature — people can post milestones (e.g. "I
  finished chemo today"), others can only reply with preset supportive
  responses (congratulations, keep going, well done) — no open-ended
  commentary.
- Also includes curated information content (how to talk to your kids
  about a cancer diagnosis, returning to work, diet/nutrition,
  exercise) — explicitly positioned against AI chatbots for this:
  "it's the bible I wish I'd had... Not ChatGPT, where you can get sent
  down a dark hole."

**Validation/traction:** two years in development. Live about a week
at time of writing, sponsored by Breast Cancer Ireland, a small pilot
run by St James's Hospital before Christmas ("that was phenomenal,
then we built it properly"), now being piloted by **St Vincent's
Hospital** with their patients. 200+ people already on the app at
publication; Vanessa also created an award-winning podcast, "Someone
Like Me," as a precursor.
