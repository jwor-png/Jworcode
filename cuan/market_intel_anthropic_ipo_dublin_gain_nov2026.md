# Charlie Taylor: "Anthropic's IPO is Dublin's gain"

Source: Business Post, 1 Nov 2026 (page 16, Tech). Captured by photograph from John, 1 Nov 2026, sideways with shadow; figures and quotes as read, partly legible. This is the same Charlie Taylor byline as the 1 Nov Markets piece, so that earlier clipping is very likely also the Business Post of 1 Nov.

## What it says

- **The filing:** on Monday Anthropic ended months of speculation by filing its initial public offering documents. The New York filing says it plans to list on **Nasdaq as soon as next month**. The process began confidentially in June. The company behind Claude is set to be the first standalone frontier AI lab to go public, ahead of OpenAI, which has pushed back its own listing until next year.
- **Valuation:** valued at **$183bn** just over a year ago (**$13bn** Series F). Investors now expect it to seek **$1.5 trillion to $1.75 trillion**, a little below the **$2 trillion** some backers suggested over the summer.
- **Financials (as reported):** Anthropic says it is profitable on an adjusted basis, with sales for the three months to June up **1,368%** year on year. It lost **$80.7bn** in the first half, though most of that was a **non-operating charge linked to convertible bonds**; its **operating loss was $693m**. It has committed more than **$600bn** to computing deals with partners including Amazon and Google, "an enormous commitment even for a company growing as quickly as it is".
- **Dublin:** Ireland was Anthropic's first port of call in Europe (office opened at the end of 2024). It already employs over 100 people locally, announced a further 200 jobs in March, and the Business Post revealed in September that it was seeking premises for up to 1,000 employees. Guillaume Princen, who leads Anthropic in Europe, told the Business Post at the International AI Summit in Dublin that talent was the key draw and that Ireland ranks highly for AI-ready employees. Anthropic has close ties to Stripe (Princen previously led Stripe's growth across Europe; Stripe payment tools have been built into Claude). The piece also mentions Anthropic's new "Duet" app that lets users delegate small tasks to an AI agent (partly legible).
- **Why it matters to Ireland:** a wealthier Anthropic is good for Ireland Inc, even if the State arguably should not rely so heavily on a handful of US multinationals to fill its coffers; staff gains depend on where the stock sits as shares unlock, and newly listed tech companies tend to drift below IPO price (SpaceX the example). Public markets are less patient than private backers and may push for prudence, which could slow Dublin growth plans; hiring decisions are still made in San Francisco, and shareholders will have a say once it lists.
- **Market mood:** Matt Kennedy (Renaissance Capital, to Reuters): "Anthropic is entering an IPO market that is far more challenging than the summer."
- **Sidebar:** $183bn valuation a year ago; $1.75trn upper limit of what investors expect when it floats.
