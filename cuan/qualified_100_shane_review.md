# Qualified 100 — Full List with Classification Reasoning
## For John's review before a final draft goes to Shane (18 Sept 2026)

Source: `prospect_list_combined.md` (125 companies), refined per the 18 Sept
refinement pass. Excludes row 55 (United Hardware — existing client, not a
prospect). **106 rows shown below** (32 + 44 + 24 + 24-in-reserve = 124,
minus United Hardware) so John can see the full picture, not just the 100,
before deciding what to cut.

**How to read the maturity tags** (OECD/G7 SME AI maturity classification,
per `revenue_streams.md`): **Novice** (no real activity yet) → **Explorer**
(experimenting/early adoption) → **Optimiser** (embedding AI into real
operations) → **Champion** (AI is a competitive differentiator). None of
this list reaches Champion — that's expected at this market tier.

**Reasoning key, applied consistently:**
- Tier 1 rows are ranked Explorer or Optimiser because there is a *named,
  concrete fact* — a tool, a partnership, a public statement — not because
  the company is large or well-known.
- Tier 2 rows are Novice/Explorer because the evidence is real but
  *automation, not AI* — digitisation, robotics, ERP, CRM. Genuine budget
  and change-appetite already proven, just not yet AI-specific.
- Tier 3 rows (both selected and reserve) are Novice by default, because
  **no company-specific evidence of anything was found** for any of them.
  Their inclusion is a *proxy* judgement only — reachable single
  decision-maker, real scale, regulatory/governance relevance, sector
  balance — not an evidenced-commitment judgement. That distinction should
  stay visible in whatever goes to Shane, so nobody mistakes "selected" for
  "already qualified."
