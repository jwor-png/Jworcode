Produced 20 July 2026 by Meridian legal research pass. Decision-support only; formal advice from Mark Power governs. Primary-source fetching was network-blocked this session; positions are multi-source REPORTED unless marked VERIFIED; one item FAILED CLOSED (exact SME Regulation number).

## Headline points
1. CCR: guarantee reporting began 1 Feb 2025, new guarantees only (VERIFIED, multiple sources). Pre-2025 guarantee should generate no CCR guarantor data. Test Tom's "on your credit" claim in writing.
2. "Never explained" is not freestanding; force comes via Roche & Buttimer undue influence route (personal non-commercial relationship, no shareholding/involvement, bank on notice, must recommend ILA or guarantee can be set aside).
3. Decisive fact: does BOI hold a signed ILA confirmation (Everyday Finance v Flood 2024: ILA confirmation defeated undue influence findings).
4. SME Lending Regulations 2015 (SI 585/2015): pre-signing written explanation and warning duties on the lender; breach grounds bank complaint and FSPO complaint (guarantors have standing where complaint concerns how guarantee was procured). Exact regulation number to be pulled from statute by solicitor.
5. Statute of Frauds 1695 s.4: writing/signature formality only; not a defence here.
6. Cases: Bank of Ireland v Smyth [1995] (informed consent, family home); Ulster Bank v Roche & Buttimer [2012] IEHC 166; Everyday Finance v Flood [2024]; Pepper Finance v Coleman [2025] IEHC 747 (unconscionable bargain arguable where bank knew no ILA). Non est factum: narrow, rarely succeeds.
7. Practical pressure: demand executed originals, pre-signing explanations, ILA confirmations, CCR position. Banks failing to produce documents is real leverage; FSPO route runs alongside settlement talks.
