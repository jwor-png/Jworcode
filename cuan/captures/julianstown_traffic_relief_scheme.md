## Technical Assessment (from Luke McNamee, CS Consulting)

**Analysis of the four options:**
- Options A, B, D all have **disadvantage**: no convenient connection to eastern section of R150
- **Option C variant recommended** as preferred solution
  - Offers possibility of mostly restricting through-traffic to village centre
  - Only local access vehicles would need village access
  - Opens possibilities for reclaiming road space for pedestrian/cyclist use (e.g., narrowing bridge carriageway)

---

## Recommended Submission Points (if going ahead)

1. **Express very strong support** for the Traffic Relief Scheme project
2. **Request the new road provide an effective alternative route** for ALL traffic currently travelling through Julianstown (e.g., traffic from Laytown towards Duleek or Balbriggan)
3. **Emphasize essential requirement**: route alignment must provide convenient connection to R150 Laytown Road
4. **Encourage MCC/SYSTRA** to allow for comprehensive reorganising of public realm along Julianstown Main Street, to significantly reduce space allocated to motor vehicles and deter unnecessary through journeys
5. **Suggest strict enforcement** of weight limit for vehicles still travelling through the village
