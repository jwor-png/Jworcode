# Shane's Session 2 Material -- The Briefing Method (UHL Training, 20 July 2026)
*Captured from Shane's Ambrion slides at John's request, to enhance John's own AI practice. Companion to `cuan/personalisation_framework.md` (the five personalisation dimensions from the same session).*

## The core teachings

1. **AI driven, human led.** AI strengths: speed, pattern recognition, drafting and summarising, repeatable analysis. Human strengths: judgement and nuance, contextual understanding, relationship building, accountability and decision making. The advantage is people working with AI in a better operating rhythm, not AI instead of people.
2. **Operator to Orchestrator.** Operator: ask, take what comes back, move on. Orchestrator: brief properly, chain the steps, check the work, decide what is good enough. Directing, not asking.
3. **A prompt is a briefing.** You would never hand a job to a new colleague with four words and walk away.
4. **The four parts (miss one and you get something vague):**
   - **Context** -- who you are, what the business is, what is going on
   - **Task** -- what you actually want done
   - **Standard** -- what good looks like, and what to avoid
   - **Output** -- what shape you want it back in, and how long
5. **The demonstration:** "Write me an email about the price increase" versus the full briefing (Head of Purchasing, 115 stores, 6% negotiated down from 9%, honest, direct and warm, no corporate padding, under 200 words). Forty seconds longer to write, twenty minutes of fixing saved.

## Exercise One: John's five core weekly briefings (drafted by Cuan from the known week, for John to confirm or amend)

### Briefing 1 -- The Weekly Revenue Drive (Mondays, and daily at the 12:00 council check)
- **Context:** John Webb O'Rourke, Managing Partner of Meridian Intelligence and Ambrion AI with Shane McCarthy; revenue is creed number one; live pipeline: 24-target training list, demo shortlist, channel partners; the December 2026 deadline is the driver.
- **Task:** review the pipeline, name this week's three revenue moves, draft the outreach for each, and flag anything going cold.
- **Standard:** warm personal approaches, one ask per message, momentum closes, no cold-blast tone; nothing commercial committed without Shane's alignment.
- **Output:** a three-move plan with ready-to-send drafts, WhatsApp-ready where relevant, inside 30 minutes.

### Briefing 2 -- The Board Paper (UHL, AHL, any committee)
- **Context:** John's role for the entity (ARC Chair UHL, Chair AHL), the meeting date, the pack received, what the record shows from prior meetings and emails.
- **Task:** produce the chair's summary, the questions to raise, and any follow-up emails arising.
- **Standard:** grounded in the actual documents and email record, discrepancies flagged not smoothed, defensible line by line; last year's format where a precedent exists (e.g. the August 2025 Audit Committee Summary Report).
- **Output:** one-page summary plus draft emails, in John's voice, ready for review the evening before the meeting.

### Briefing 3 -- The Account Position Statement (Lynch-type debtors, invoices, AHL accounts)
- **Context:** the full email record of the account reconstructed first; John's own prior statements are the anchors; discrepancies between statements get surfaced before anything goes out.
- **Task:** produce the statement or chasing email with the arithmetic verified from the record.
- **Standard:** courteous, relationship-preserving, thanks first, exact figures, one clear ask; nothing claimed that the record does not support.
- **Output:** email-ready plain text, plus the reconciliation table for John's own file.

### Briefing 4 -- The Meridian Client Brief (the product itself)
- **Context:** the client, the commissioning conversation, which of the seven domains apply, the governance floor (VERIFIED/REPORTED/UNVERIFIED tags, fail closed, independent check).
- **Task:** run the parallel specialist passes, adversarially verify, consolidate.
- **Standard:** decision-ready or the gap named; every figure sourced or labelled assumption; professional gates flagged (legal, tax); the audit trail written.
- **Output:** branded PDF plus WhatsApp summary block, status label on the front.

### Briefing 5 -- The Weekly Reset (Friday or Sunday evening)
- **Context:** the master list, the creed order, the week's emails and WhatsApps, family and health commitments first onto the diary.
- **Task:** re-sort everything open into the creed order, mark done, dead and new, and set the coming week's critical path.
- **Standard:** honest status (no item silently dropped), life balance non-negotiables scheduled before commercial fill, flags where John's word is needed.
- **Output:** the refreshed master list pushed to the knowledge base, plus a ten-line week-ahead summary.

*These five now run as standing templates. Saying "run briefing 2 for the AHL board call" is a complete instruction.*
