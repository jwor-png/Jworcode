- **Location signal:** John's read of the output: **"Deebert where the hotel is looks to be the likely spot."** John planned to run the outcome past **"the Diviner"** who had familiarised himself with the locality.
- **9 Apr 2025 ("Kilmallock Natural Water"):** John shared Shane's report in confidence with **Ger (ger@dsb.ie)**. John agreed on all points.
- **Comparable to study:** **Ishka Irish Spring Water, Ballyneety, Co. Limerick** -- John wanted to learn their business case/model.
