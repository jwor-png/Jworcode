# Captured — Instagram "8 Prompts Every Founder Needs" (@yourclaudehacks)
*Shared by John, 31 July 2026. Generic single-shot prompts, kept for reference.*

1. **One-Page Business Plan** — "Act as a McKinsey consultant. My idea is [describe]. Build a complete one-page plan — problem, customer, solution, revenue model, competitive edge, and 90-day action plan."
2. **Market Validation Report** — "Act as a market analyst... market size, top 3 competitors and their weaknesses, the gap I can own, and your honest verdict."
3. **Revenue Model Designer** — "Act as a startup CFO... 3 revenue model options — subscription, one-time, hybrid..."
4. **Go-To-Market Blueprint** — "Act as a growth strategist... How do I get my first 10 paying customers?"
5. **Competitor Teardown** — "Act as a competitive analyst... top 5 competitors — strengths, weaknesses, pricing, positioning gaps."
6. **Financial Projections Builder** — "Act as a startup CFO... 12-month financial model — monthly revenue, key expenses, burn rate, break-even month."
7. **Risk Assessment Report** — "Act as a business risk analyst... 5 biggest risks — market, execution, financial, legal, competitive."
8. **Investor Pitch Summary** — "Act as a pitch coach... 300-word investor summary... every sentence earns its place."

## Note for context, not a critique of John's judgement
These are single-shot, single-perspective prompts — one model, one pass, no verification, no source-checking, no adversarial challenge. Useful as a quick personal sense-check or first draft. They are exactly the category of "AI-generated bullet points" the Meridian positioning (see `meridian/launch/`) is explicitly built to be more rigorous than: multi-lens, verified, human-judged, decision-ready — not a single confident answer from one pass.
