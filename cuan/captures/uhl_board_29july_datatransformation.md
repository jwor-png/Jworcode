## What UHL is actually asking the board to endorse
Not a budget approval — a **direction and staged-approach endorsement** for a Member Data, Product Data and Insights Platform. Core opportunity: turn ~160 members' fragmented EPOS data + central product data (Plytix PIM) into a scalable insights capability (revenue insight, stock efficiency, pricing, category management, supplier negotiation, member benchmarking).

## The competitive driver (the "why now")
Explicit in the paper: **UHL risks falling further behind Chadwicks and HPC**, who already have near-live EPOS/sales/stock/margin data enabling better pricing, buying and supplier commitment decisions. This is stated as the core commercial risk of inaction.

## The hard constraint — this is the opening for AI/Ambrion
A prior EY proof-of-concept already identified the two binding constraints: **data quality** and **member engagement/adoption** — not technology. ~160 stores run varied EPOS (est. 50-70% on one of Intact / Agility / CORE, multiple versions, some on no IT system at all), with members reluctant to even disclose which system they use. This is a change-management and adoption problem as much as a data platform problem.

## The staged plan (management's recommended option, Option 2 of 3)
- Shortlist vendor(s): within 3 months
- Design complete + member engagement begun: within 6 months
- Onboard 25 members (intra-day data feed): within 12 months
- Scale to 50-75% of membership: the following 12 months
Rejected options: pause/defer (loses momentum), or big-bang (too risky given fragmentation).

## The five things the board is actually asked to decide
1. Should participation in the data programme be a mandatory condition of membership?
2. Endorse the staged approach.
3. Support continued vendor market engagement (the RFI is live, 4-week response window).
4. Set the threshold for proceeding to the first implementation wave.
5. Note no budget is set yet — indicative vendor costs come back first, budget approval later.

## Vendor Challenge Brief — the technical/commercial shape
- Primary contact: Alan O'Brien (alan.obrien@unitedhardware.ie). Status: draft for issue, 4-week vendor response window.
- Preferred architecture (not fixed): centralised, cloud-based, **EU data residency**, English-language support.
- Central systems already in place: **Plytix (PIM)**, **SAP Business One (ERP, on-prem, UHL-only, not used by members)**.
- Integration target: at least 80% of member EPOS systems within 24 months.
- Priority use cases named: member revenue insight, stock efficiency, share-of-wallet, order value & customer segmentation, UHL spend visibility, supplier negotiation evidence, category management, pricing benchmarking, member benchmarking.
- Evaluation weightings: EPOS integration 20%, data quality/governance 20%, strategic fit 15%, insights/analytics 15%, PIM/supplier engagement 10%, delivery/adoption 10%, security/EU residency 5%, cost transparency 5%.
- Cost buckets flagged (no budget yet): discovery/design, cloud platform setup, EPOS integration framework, first-25-member integration, data quality/standardisation, PIM integration, insights/reporting build, member onboarding/change support, licensing, run/support, scale-up costs, **optional services explicitly include "AI/ML"**.
