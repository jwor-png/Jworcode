# Master Plan — John's Daily Operating Frame

Requested 9 September 2026: "a masterplan for myself and my operating
creed across all areas with focus first every day on my revenue
generating projects, all Shane related, Meridian etc." Explicitly
**not** a template or worksheet — John was clear he brain-dumps
first thing every morning as ideas arrive, unstructured, and won't
work to a form. So this plan lives on Cuan's side: it's the standing
priority order Cuan applies when triaging whatever John dumps, not a
new discipline for John to follow.

This does not replace `operating_creed.md` (the six disciplines,
reviewed daily/every second day) or `revenue_streams.md` (the 8-stream
portfolio tracker). It sits above both as the ordering logic: when
John's morning dump touches more than one thing at once — which it
almost always does — this is the order Cuan sorts it into and surfaces
it back in.

---

## How this actually works day to day

John does nothing differently. He keeps brain-dumping first thing,
in whatever order things come to mind, exactly as now. What changes is
on Cuan's side:

1. As John's dump comes in (photographed notebook page, voice note,
   typed message), Cuan tags each item by which tier below it falls
   into.
2. Anything in Tier 1 gets surfaced back to John first and logged
   immediately — it doesn't wait behind personal errands or lower-
   priority items just because it was mentioned later in the dump.
3. Everything still gets logged, in the correct file, regardless of
   tier — the ordering only affects what Cuan foregrounds first when
   reporting back, not what gets tracked at all. Nothing gets dropped
   for being "lower tier."
4. This is a standing instruction, not a one-off — Cuan applies it by
   default from now on, without John needing to ask "what should I
   focus on" each time.

Common thread: Shane is the technical/execution partner on all of
these, and per `people_map.md`, nothing here commits without his
alignment. That's not a bottleneck to route around — it's the actual
governance rule staying in force even inside a revenue-first ordering.

## Relationship to the six Operating Creed disciplines

The tiering above is about **daily attention order**, not a
replacement for the six disciplines John already reviews in
`operating_creed.md`:

1. Move Revenue Weekly — Tier 1 and 2 above are the daily feed into
   this discipline
2. Protect My Health and Family — always protected, see above
3. Act With Integrity — cuts across every tier
4. Resolve My Past Professionally — mostly Tier 2 (Legacy Financial
   Matters) and Tier 3
5. Protect My Present Responsibly — cuts across every tier
6. Build My Future Deliberately — mostly Tier 3, plus the strategic
   end of Tier 1 (ODIN, Meridian equity)

The weekly/every-second-day review of `operating_creed.md` still
happens as before. This master plan just governs what gets surfaced
first on any given morning, so John doesn't have to hold the ordering
in his head himself.
