# Wills and probate in Ireland, plus Indo Money Q&A (Irish Independent, Sat 24 Oct 2026)

Source: Irish Independent, Indo Money, p34, Saturday 24 October 2026. Captured by photograph from John, 24 Oct 2026. Source-tagged as printed; not independently verified.

## 1. "Writing a will in Ireland, here's how to avoid the most common mistakes" (Sinead Ryan)

- **Why it ran:** the probate office handled two very large estates recently. Sabena Power (horse owner and breeder, widow of David Power, founder of one of the firms that became Paddy Power) left about 900m euro (printed as 859,488,937.02). Anne Madden le Brocquy (artist, widow of Louis le Brocquy) left over 6.5m euro.
- **Key lesson:** Ms Madden le Brocquy wrote at least four wills between 2019 and 2025. In Irish law the final valid will is the only one that matters. Updating a will is advisable; the writer is on her fourth.
- **Intestacy (no will):** the court decides who administers the estate. A surviving spouse inherits two thirds if there are children, or the whole estate if none. Children have no automatic right to inherit, and a parent is not required to divide an estate evenly.
- **Spouse's legal right (even with a will):** at least a third of the estate where there are children, or half where there are none. The only person a testator cannot exclude is the spouse.
- **Executor/administrator:** a burdensome, unpaid duty (unless a professional, such as a solicitor). The role can be rescinded while the nominating person is alive.
- **Probate costs (as printed):** personal applicant pays 200 euro for estates up to 100,000, 1,300 up to 1m, plus 800 for every 500,000 above; through a solicitor the administration fee is 100 up to 100,000, 650 up to 1m, plus 400 for every 500,000 above (costs halved with a solicitor, though the solicitor is also paid). Extras: certificates 15 to 40 euro, property/document searches 20 to 120, court fees 30 to 50.
- **When probate is not needed:** jointly owned assets pass to the survivor automatically; accounts under 20,000 euro not in a joint account can be paid to the spouse without probate (institutions differ).
- **Timing:** Dublin probate office was processing applications from mid-July. Hardship payments can sometimes be made on an emergency basis. Challenging a will is rare; a caveat can freeze a grant. Wills become public once probate is granted.
