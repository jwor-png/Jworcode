# "You need more than a State pension to retire on" — The Echo, 8 Oct 2026

Opinion piece by **Laura Farrell, CEO of the Retirement Planning Council
of Ireland (RPC)**, written in the wake of this week's Budget 2027
State pension increase (described as "modest," €10/week referenced
directly in the text).

## Key points

- Core argument: the State pension "was never designed to act as a
  complete, comfortable retirement income on its own" — it's a
  baseline, not the full picture, regardless of whether the annual
  budget bump is €10, €15 or €20.
- RPC's "two essential pillars" of successful retirement: (1)
  **financial clarity** — understanding pensions, savings, timing,
  managing expectations; (2) a **deeply personal pillar** — maintaining
  purpose, new routines, health, relationships, deciding how/where to
  live the next chapter.
- Argues pre-retirement planning should be "a standard, expected part
  of working life" rather than an afterthought squeezed into the final
  months before finishing up, and pushes **Mid-Career Planning** as the
  right stage to engage, both for workers and the employers who support
  them.
- Explicit call to "business leaders across Cork and Munster" to invest
  in comprehensive retirement preparation access for staff, early in
  their careers.
- Notes RPC provides lifelong follow-up support at no extra charge to
  its members, framed as a "trusted sounding board."
- Closing line: "Don't wait for the next budget to think about your
  future — take control, seek guidance early, and start building the
  retirement you truly want today."

No hard national statistics cited in this piece beyond the general
budget context — it's an advocacy/opinion column, not a data report.
