# Shane McCarthy LinkedIn article, "What happens at 5:01? A question for every board setting its 2027 budget" (3 Nov 2026, shared by John 3 Nov, screenshots)

Source: LinkedIn article by Shane McCarthy (CEO, Founder, AI Architect, Systems Builder), 3 Nov 2026; two likes and one comment (Padraic O Maille: "Scarily exciting Shane") at time of capture. Not opened by Cuan (LinkedIn blocked in this session); read from John's screenshots.

**Argument:** 2027 budgets are being set; AI has not been given a proper place; two investments need serious attention: (1) the capability to use AI properly (licences, tokens, usage allowances, training, support, room to experiment; costs planned, measured and owned; staff working around unapproved tools lose leadership visibility of data), and (2) cybersecurity, resilience and defensibility. Central question: "If something happens at five o'clock, what happens inside your company at 5:01?" (who gets the alert, who decides, who can switch off access or contain an agent, contact suppliers, protect data, communicate; who acts if the CEO is unavailable; how fast the board gets an accurate picture). "A 2027 budget should give people the capability to use AI, and give leadership the confidence that the organisation can adapt, defend itself and recover when something goes wrong."
