# Market Intelligence — "'Over the summer we just had fun building the
business' — teen entrepreneurs already attracting interest of tech
giants" (Laura Lynott)

Newspaper clipping photographed by John, 9 Sept 2026. John's own
framing: interesting for how quickly an AI career/business can
develop, contrasted with his own position as part of the "Golden
Generation" seeing the same opportunity from the other end of a
career.

## The founders and company
- **Krios** — an AI-based startup founded by three Leaving Cert
  students at Coláiste Spioraid Naoimh, Bishopstown, Cork:
  **Ahroon Santhosh (17, CEO)**, **Abraham Sabu Kurian (18, CTO)**, and
  **Michael Sweeney Hefferman (17, Head of Growth)**.
- All three sat their Leaving Cert exam this year while running the
  business simultaneously and have already had acquisition-interest
  meetings with major tech companies — turning those down to keep
  building rather than sell early.
- **What Krios does:** an AI platform aimed at field sales reps —
  giving them AI-driven support/tools to work more efficiently while
  out in the field, rather than relying on manual prep and processes.
  Already has real, paying business relationships (not just a school
  project) — the founders describe juggling actual client work and
  business responsibilities during their study break.
- **Origin story:** Ahroon was inspired by his uncle, a butcher in
  Cork, who "would do all the store management... all the customer
  relations" himself — Ahroon saw the manual, labour-intensive way his
  uncle ran deliveries and admin and wanted to build something to solve
  that kind of problem at scale.
- **How the team formed:** through the **Student Enterprise Programme**
  and a **mini company programme** — the trio met through Transition
  Year business programmes rather than being pre-existing friends,
  underlining the value of structured school entrepreneurship
  programmes as a genuine pipeline.
- **Funding/validation so far:** entered the Student Enterprise
  Programme and related school/college enterprise competitions, with
  prize money and support secured along the way — **exact figures and
  programme names not confidently legible from the clipping (small
  print, photographed at an angle) and not restated here as precise
  facts; worth a clean re-read of the original article if exact
  numbers are ever needed.**
- **Ambition stated directly:** the founders want to keep pursuing the
  business alongside school/college rather than choosing one over the
  other, with stated interest in expanding beyond Ireland in time.
  **REPORTED, not independently verified** — some surrounding detail
  in the clipping (e.g. references to other sectors/family businesses)
  was not clearly legible and has been left out rather than guessed.
- **On why now/why this generation:** the founders' own framing —
  being in business is attractive because of "hanging out with my
  co-founders and with the people I meet through the business," not
  purely a financial motive; they also stress networking and just
  finding people interesting to talk to as a genuine driver, alongside
  ambition.
