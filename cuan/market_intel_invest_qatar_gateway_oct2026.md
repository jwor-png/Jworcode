# "Invest Qatar Gateway" — QIA-backed founder funding platform

Source: Instagram carousel, falah_al_deen (verified account). UNVERIFIED by Cuan independently — a social-media summary, not the primary Invest Qatar source material, though the organisation (Invest Qatar, the Qatar Investment Authority) is a genuine, named, verifiable public entity, more credible on its face than a personal lead-gen post. Figures should still be confirmed against Invest Qatar's own site before relying on them.

## What it says

**Who's behind it — Qatar Investment Authority (QIA):**
- QIA manages one of the largest sovereign wealth funds on earth — **$600B+ assets under management** (as stated in the post).
- In 2026, QIA expanded its startup-focused **Fund of Funds to $3B**, and Invest Qatar built a platform ("the Gateway") to put it within reach of founders directly.

**The platform — Invest Qatar Gateway:**
- A single online platform that walks founders through funding, grants, incentives, relocation, housing, and support programs — instead of chasing a dozen agencies separately.
- **New this month (per the post):** "A VC Funding Module that lets founders pitch fund managers directly through the Gateway."

**Who's on the platform — 15+ VC funds, one directory:**
- Backed by QIA's Fund of Funds and QSTP's (Qatar Science & Technology Park) Tech Venture Fund.
- Named funds reachable through the Gateway: **A-Typical Ventures, B Capital, Greycroft, Shorooq, Speedinvest, Rasmal Ventures, Human Capital, The Radical Fund.**

**Beyond funding — the rest of the ecosystem already built:**
- **Incubators & accelerators:** MCIT's Digital Incubation Center, QDB's Scale7, Qatar FinTech Hub, QSTP.
- **Academia & R&D:** Qatar Foundation, Hamad Bin Khalifa University, Qatar University, the QRDI Council.

**Three steps on the Gateway (how to actually use it):**
1. **Register on the Gateway** — free for founders, unlocks the funding, grants, and incentive modules.
2. **Filter funds by focus area** — compare investment criteria across the 15+ participating VC funds.
3. **Submit your pitch directly** — no cold intros needed, pitch fund managers through the platform.
