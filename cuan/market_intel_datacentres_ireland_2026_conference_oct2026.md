# Data Centres Ireland 2026 — RDS Dublin, 18-19 November 2026

Source: official event brochure. VERIFIED — primary event material, "correct as of 24/09/2026." Organised by Stepex. Described as "the must-attend event that brings together a dedicated exhibition and multi-streamed conference covering every angle of building, running, and securing your Data Centre, Server/Comms Room, or Critical Infrastructure."

**Opening times:** Wednesday — registration/exhibition 9:00-17:00, conference 9:00; Thursday — registration/exhibition 9:00-16:00/16:45, conference 9:30-16:00.
