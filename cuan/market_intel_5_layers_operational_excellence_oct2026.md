# "5 Layers of Operational Excellence" — Eric Partaker

Source: Instagram, Eric Partaker (business coach, "Top Voice," citing Harvard Business Review as the underlying source). UNVERIFIED as presented (a social-media infographic), though attributed to HBR-sourced framework — treat the underlying research claim as REPORTED, not independently checked.

## What it says

A five-layer concentric-circle framework, read from the centre outward:

1. **Standardization (core):** write clear processes, define roles, create checklists, document best practices, train consistently.
2. **Automation:** connect your tools, use smart tools, automate recurring tasks, eliminate manual steps, minimize manual errors, set up triggers & workflows, set up dashboards.
3. **Measurement:** define key metrics, track performance, tie metrics to decisions, compare results to goals, share results with your team.
4. **Continuous Improvement:** run regular reviews, run retrospectives, document key learnings, repeat what works, scale what works, adjust systems, learn from failures, improve weekly, involve your customers.
5. **Innovation (outer layer):** host idea sessions, gather feedback, encourage bold thinking, spot bottlenecks, prioritize high-impact tests, run quick experiments, run small pilots, track ideas in motion, protect time to explore, align team on fixes, recognize & reward progress, share what you learn.

Core framing: start at the centre (standardization) and work outward — you can't meaningfully automate a process that isn't standardized, can't measure what isn't automated/tracked consistently, can't improve what isn't measured, and can't innovate sustainably without a foundation of continuous improvement underneath it.
