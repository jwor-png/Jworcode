## What it says
A detailed explainer on the **Fuel Allowance** and related supports:
- **Fuel Allowance:** €38/week, paid to one person per household,
  late September to late April (28 weeks), total €1,064/season. Paid
  weekly or in two lump sums (September and January) on request.
- **Qualifying conditions:** must live alone or with specified qualifying
  people; must be on a qualifying payment (State Pension Contributory/
  Non-Contributory, Invalidity Pension, Disability Allowance, One Parent
  Family Payment, Farm Assist) unless aged 66+, in which case no
  qualifying payment is required.
- **Means test:** capital thresholds — first €20,000 not assessed if
  under 66, first €50,000 not assessed if 66+; own home never assessed;
  second-property rental income is assessed (net of mortgage) if not
  rented out. Room-rental income up to €269.23/week (€14,000/year) is
  disregarded if the room is used a minimum 28 consecutive days and the
  tenant isn't an employee/immediate family member.
- **Income limits:** single under 66: €499.30/week; couple under 66:
  €698.70/week; rising to €534/€1,068 (single/couple) if 66+, or
  €767.70/week if the qualified adult is 66+.
- **Working Family Payment recipients** automatically get Fuel Allowance
  since 1 January (if no other household member already has it) — no
  separate application needed.
- **New from this month:** people moving from Disability Allowance or
  Blind Pension into work keep Fuel Allowance for 5 years (announced in
  last year's Budget, only now in effect).
- **Telephone Support Allowance:** €2.50/week (€130/year), paid
  automatically alongside Fuel Allowance + Living Alone Increase, no
  separate application.
- **Household Benefits Package (HBP):** helps with electricity/gas and
  TV licence; not means-tested for those 70+; under-70s need a
  qualifying social welfare payment and specific household-composition
  rules (living with a spouse/partner requires an increase in their
  qualifying payment; shared accommodation with other adults, e.g. a
  parent, is allowed without their income counting).
