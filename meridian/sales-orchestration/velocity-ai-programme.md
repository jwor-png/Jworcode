## What the three sessions cover

### Session 1 -- The Landscape (Half day)
*Why this conversation is happening now and what it means for your organisation*

- The AI moment we are in -- what has actually changed and why it matters now
- The regulatory wall: EU AI Act, Irish AI Bill 2026, National AI Office (August 2026)
- Four risk tiers every Irish organisation needs to understand
- What boards and senior leaders are now legally accountable for
- Common mistakes organisations are making right now
- Interactive: where does your organisation sit today?

### Session 2 -- The Opportunity (Half day)
*What AI can actually do for your business -- demonstrated, not theorised*

- Live demonstrations of AI applied to your sector
- The four phases of AI adoption (Static Foundation / Bolt-On Trap / Architecture Rebuild / Native Operation) -- where are you and where do you need to be?
- Identifying your highest-value AI use cases
- What to commission, what to avoid, and how to evaluate what you are being sold
- Interactive: your organisation's AI opportunity map

### Session 3 -- The Action (Half day)
*Building your AI governance framework and 90-day plan*

- The five director duties under the EU AI Act
- Building your AI policy, inventory, and governance structure
- Appointing your accountable executive
- Your quarterly board AI dashboard
- 90-day action plan: what your organisation commits to before it leaves the room
- Interactive: leave with a working draft

## Grant Funding Strategy

### For Velocity AI -- becoming grant-approved

To allow clients to draw down grant funding, Velocity AI needs to be recognised as an approved training provider under the relevant schemes. Priority actions:

**1. Skillnets Ireland -- Training Provider Registration**
- Apply to become a registered Skillnet training provider
- Once approved, companies in Skillnet member networks can receive 40-60% funding toward the €10,000
- Key Skillnet networks aligned to the 50-target list:
  - Retail Skillnet (buying groups, FMCG)
  - Food and Drink Skills Ireland (agri-food, dairy)
  - National Construction Skillnet (construction, engineering)
  - Financial Services Ireland (FSI) Skillnet (financial services)
  - Legal Island / Professional Services (law firms, accountancies)
- Contact: skillnets.ie -- Training Provider enquiries
- Timeline: registration can take 4-8 weeks; pursue immediately

**2. Enterprise Ireland -- Approved Training Provider**
- EI client companies have access to training and development supports
- Being on EI's approved provider list means EI clients can fund the programme through their EI relationship
- Contact: Enterprise Ireland regional offices
- Requires: company registration, programme outline, delivery credentials

**3. IDA Ireland -- Management Development Programme**
- For IDA-supported multinational clients
- Less relevant for the 50-target list (primarily Irish-owned) but worth registering for

**4. QQI Accreditation (longer term)**
- Makes the programme formally accredited at a national level
- Significantly increases credibility and grant eligibility
- 6-12 month process -- begin after Skillnets registration is complete
