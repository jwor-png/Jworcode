## How Meridian routes a sales brief

**Step 1 — Identify the venture.**
Every brief names which track it belongs to. If it spans more than one (e.g. a prospect is relevant to both Ambrion and Meridian), name both and consolidate.

**Step 2 — Stage the deal.**
Use the five-stage pipeline (below). Name the stage. What is needed to advance to the next stage is the output, not a description of the current stage.

**Step 3 — Route to the manager.**
Commercial & Deal handles structure, pricing, terms, and counterparty profiling. AI Strategy & Adoption handles Ambrion and Meridian partner material. Business Transformation & Growth handles client analysis and growth opportunity briefs.

**Step 4 — Return the action.**
One clear next action for John. The brief, the material, or the outreach draft ready to use. Not a list of options unless John asked for options.

## The five-stage pipeline

Applied across all four tracks.

| Stage | Name | What it means | What advances it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Identified | Person or organisation named as a prospect or partner | First contact initiated or meeting requested |
| 2 | Engaged | Contact made; interest confirmed or pending | A meeting held, a brief submitted, or an expression of interest returned |
| 3 | Qualified | Scope confirmed; they have a need John can meet | A proposal or scoped offer sent |
| 4 | Progressing | Proposal in play; active negotiation or follow-up | Terms agreed or a signed engagement letter |
| 5 | Active | Engagement running | Delivery underway; review cycle set |

A separate category: **Stalled** -- in the pipeline but no movement. Cuan flags stalled items after 14 days with no advance.
