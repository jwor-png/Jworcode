# AI Adoption in Ireland — Prospect Universe

Opened 6 August 2026. Sits alongside the Velocity AI / UHL training
workstream (`velocity-ai-programme.md`, `uhl-model-target-list.md`) but is
broader in scope: a structured prospect universe across all three AI
businesses, rather than a single €10k-training product line.

## Objective

Identify Irish companies and organisations actively investing in,
adopting, or preparing to adopt AI — building a prospect base for the AI
businesses John Webb O'Rourke and Shane McCarthy are developing, rather
than relying solely on introductions from the existing personal network.

## Scope of the exercise

- Started at ~100 Irish companies/organisations, expanded to ~200.
- Sectors covered: private, public, semi-state, large enterprise,
  medium-sized enterprise, and appropriately-scaled SMEs.
- Data sought per organisation: sector, approximate size, employee
  numbers, turnover/revenue or other scale indicator, evidence of AI
  adoption/investment, likely AI requirement, suitability as a sales
  prospect.
- Particular emphasis on organisations large enough to have meaningful AI
  requirements and the budget to buy professional AI services.

## Mapped against three propositions

**AmbrionAI** — AI governance, EU AI Act readiness/compliance, board and
senior management governance, AI policies/risk registers/governance
frameworks, executive and organisational AI education/training.

**Velocity AI** — AI implementation, AI strategy, business
transformation, identification and development of practical AI use
cases, integration of AI into business processes.

**Meridian Intelligence** — executive decision intelligence, strategic
decision support, market intelligence, scenario intelligence, governance
and verification around consequential business decisions.

## Progression logic

AI capability → identifiable market → 100 prospects → 200 prospects →
prioritised companies → specific decision/use case → direct commercial
approach.

Work has since narrowed from the broad 200-list toward companies of the
right size/profile with a realistic, specific commercial application.
