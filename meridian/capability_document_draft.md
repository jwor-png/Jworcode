## The core thesis: the money is in the stack, not any single manager

Most people advising on AI have a knowledge surface and nothing else. Meridian has four layers that multiply each other:

1. **Knowledge surface** -- the seven managers, a forty-year experience base.
2. **Defensibility layer** -- the governance floor, grounding, cross-model check, append-only audit log. *This is the differentiator that lets John actually charge.*
3. **Production layer** -- finished Word, PDF, deck, spreadsheet, in John's voice and to a real design standard.
4. **Delivery layer** -- mail, calendar, web retrieval, connectors.

> "Anyone can generate an opinion. Meridian generates an opinion that shows its working and can prove it. A service that shows its working is worth more than one that asks to be trusted. That single fact is what turns this from a clever assistant into something sellable."

## The cash-first order (Shane's recommendation)

Short-term quartet: **5** (AI Act briefs, demand is live and panicked), then **4** (turn 5 into a repeatable SKU set), then **6** (due-diligence dossiers, easy to sell, compound John's reputation), then **2 and 3 together** (the multi-domain review delivered as a finished pack, the demo that sells everything else).

Medium-term scale: **8 and 10**, where John stops trading hours for money.

## The honesty section (what is real vs what is not)

> "The engine is real. The revenue motion around it is the next build, not a done thing."

**Wired and provable today:** the seven managers, the governance floor, the parallel agent fan-out, the cross-model critic, the live retrieval, the voice and design skills, the mail and calendar and connector reach.

**Capability-in-principle, not yet proven in production:** the commercial wrapper. None of it has been run end to end on a real paying matter yet. The productised SKUs in 4 and 5 are designed but not packaged with pricing. 8 and 10 need one clean reference case before they sell.

## The discipline (the most important point)

> "Do not let John read this as 'Meridian can do everything,' because the value is the opposite. The reason it makes money is that it stays narrow and deep: decision support John stands in front of, proven and defensible, produced fast. The moment it tries to be the adviser of record or the implementation shop, the defensibility that is the whole premium evaporates. The discipline is the product."

This is the boundary, stated from inside Meridian itself. It also answers the portfolio overlap question: Meridian stays the decision-support layer John fronts. It does not become the implementation shop. That is Velocity's ground.
