## What it says (REPORTED)
- Stage 1, $0 to $1M, **Sales**: proof of concept. Goal customer acquisition; focus pure sales and product-market fit; question: can you get people to pay you?
- Stage 2, $1M to $5M, **Operations**: you proved you can sell, now can you fulfil? Goal delivery and retention; focus operational infrastructure and client satisfaction; question: can you deliver quality without breaking?
- Stage 3, $5M to $10M, **Talent**: you can no longer do it all yourself. Goal build the leadership bench; focus **high-caliber hiring and executive placement**; question: can you recruit, place and motivate the right leaders?
- Stage 4, $10M+, **Systems**: turn individual hustle into repeatable engines (scalable sales organisation, operations and fulfilment, talent acquisition). Question: **does the business run without you?**
- Stage 5, beyond $10M, **M&A**: when to consider acquiring. Trigger: has organic growth started to slow? Strategy: roll up core competitors or acquire adjacent capabilities. Golden rule: until organic growth slows, do nothing but keep growing organically.
- Slide 7, roadmap at a glance, closing with the engagement question "Which stage is your business currently in?" (a prompt for comments).

## Check
- This is a generic staged-growth model, not research. No data or source is given, and the dollar thresholds are asserted.
- The Talent stage is framed around "executive placement", which suggests the author's own business is recruitment. That is an inference from the slide wording, not confirmed.
- It is US start-up framing: founder-led growth to $10M. The model ends with the owner as a **buyer** (M&A). It has no stage for governance, capital structure, succession or selling the business. Stage 4's question, whether the business runs without you, is the nearest it comes to succession, and it treats it as an operational test and not a decision. The "do nothing until growth slows" rule is a simplification: acquisitions and sales are also driven by succession, opportunity and defence.

## Meridian read
