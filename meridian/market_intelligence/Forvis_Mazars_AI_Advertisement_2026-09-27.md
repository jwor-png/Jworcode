## WHY THIS MATTERS MORE THAN THE EY COLUMNS

The two Grit Young pieces were **opinion**, published free, describing a capability Ireland needs. **This is paid media.** A firm has committed budget to placing "make better decisions with AI" in the national business press under its own brand.

**Opinion says a category exists. An advertisement says somebody is buying.**

**And the competitive position is closer to home.** EY does not serve the Irish mid-market economically, which is why Meridian's answer to EY was that the gap is structural and safe. **Forvis Mazars is mid-tier, and mid-tier Audit, Tax and Advisory firms serve exactly the owner-managed businesses Meridian is built for.** That is a materially different competitive read and it should not be filed beside the EY notes as though it were the same thing.

---

## WHAT THEY ARE ACTUALLY SELLING, WHICH IS NOT WHAT MERIDIAN SELLS

Read past the headline and the offer is precise: bringing together **data, analytics and AI** to improve **planning, performance and patient outcomes**, identifying **practical opportunities for AI** and turning them into **solutions**.

**That is data analytics consulting and implementation.** It builds the pipeline, the dashboard and the model. It is delivery work, it is capable, and it is a different product from a written position on a single decision.

**The distinction is the one already recorded against EOLAS on 7 August: one supplies, one decides.** Meridian has met this boundary before and resolved it before. The language is the same, the product is not.

---

## THE STRATEGIC LESSON, AND IT IS UNCOMFORTABLE

**They picked one sector and went deep.** Healthcare and medtech. Two named people, a stated sector specialism, and a claim to "a deep understanding of healthcare".

**Meridian is horizontal.** Seven domains, any business, any owner. That is genuinely differentiating in the product, and it is genuinely weaker in the marketing, because a buyer in a specific sector reads a sector-specific advertisement as being about them and reads a horizontal one as being about everybody.

**Meridian does not need to abandon the horizontal engine to fix this.** It needs one sector to lead with, and the evidence in the knowledge base points at one already: farm and family business succession, the agri sector, and the Irish owner-managed business heading for a transition. Every post that has landed best has been in that territory.

**Recorded as a strategic observation, not a recommendation to act on today.**

---

## THE LINE WORTH TAKING ISSUE WITH

> **"Better decisions start with better insight."**

**That is the assumption Meridian was built to challenge, and it is wrong in a specific and arguable way.**

Insight is an input. Most boards and owners are not short of insight, they are short of a position. The gap between knowing more and deciding better is not filled by more data, it is filled by somebody weighing the evidence across every domain that could change the answer, arguing against their own conclusion, and then putting their name to it.

**Better decisions do not start with better insight. They start with somebody prepared to be accountable for the answer.**

That is the strongest line available and it is entirely Meridian's own. It has appeared in the knowledge base three times already, against EOLAS on 7 August, in the Executive Intelligence Review post, and in the routing material.

---

## COMMERCIAL ACTIONS

1. **Re-grade the competitive map.** The threat is not Big Four. It is **mid-tier Audit, Tax and Advisory firms extending into AI-enabled decision support**, because they already hold the relationship with Meridian's buyer and they already have the trust that comes with signing the accounts.
2. **Watch for more of these.** One advertisement is a signal. A second from a different mid-tier firm inside a quarter is a category forming, and that changes the pricing conversation.
3. **Consider a lead sector** without narrowing the engine. Agri, family business and succession is where the evidence points.
4. **Do not respond to this publicly by name.** See the post note below.
