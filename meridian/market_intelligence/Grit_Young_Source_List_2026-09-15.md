## THE PROBLEM WITH THE CURRENT WEBSITE PARAGRAPH

The block currently says EY Ireland argued that **"Ireland's highest-value AI opportunity is not hosting data centres, but expertise in capital allocation, risk management, regulation and governance."**

**That sentence comes from the newspaper column, not from anything on ey.com that has been located.** The EY-published pages carry the same argument in different words: capital allocation as a defining challenge, and Ireland holding or building the necessary capabilities, with infrastructure and planning as the constraints. **They do not obviously contain the "not hosting data centres" contrast**, which is the sharpest and most quotable part and therefore the part most likely to be checked.

**Two honest ways forward.**

**Option A, preferred. Restate the paragraph in EY's published words and link EY.** Fully checkable in one click, and it removes any dependence on a clipping.

> **REPORTED · EXTERNAL COMMENTARY**
> EY Ireland has described AI infrastructure as one of the defining capital allocation challenges of the next generation, cutting across technology, energy, real estate, supply chains, regulation and financing, and has argued that Ireland already holds or is building many of the capabilities needed to benefit, with infrastructure and planning as the critical constraints. Attributed to Grit Young, EY-Parthenon Partner and EY Ireland Technology, Media and Entertainment and Telecommunications Industry Leader. Published by EY Ireland. Reproduced as external context for Meridian's position, not as a claim by Meridian and not as any endorsement of Meridian by EY.

**Option B. Keep the sharper sentence and cite the newspaper properly**, with masthead, date and author, and link the EY page separately. Honest, but a reader cannot verify it without the paper.

**Either way, retag from UNVERIFIED to REPORTED.** The author, her role and her published position are established from EY's own website, so UNVERIFIED now understates what is held and makes Meridian's own tagging look miscalibrated on the very page that explains the tagging.
