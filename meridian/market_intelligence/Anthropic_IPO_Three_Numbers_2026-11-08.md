## What the article says (REPORTED, the author's)
Anthropic this week published its official IPO paperwork. Three numbers not in the filing raise open questions:
1. **Short-term growth.** The filing leaves a space for the October revenue run rate, which could weigh on investor sentiment. The company's short-term growth "has already been through a wild swing this year". In the first half of the year **83 per cent of revenue came from consumption**, making it highly dependent on short-term usage. A summer lull may be partly seasonal, which the filing notes, but seasonality is normally seen in mature businesses.
2. **Future capacity.** The filing leaves an empty space for total committed compute spending. Details show **more than $650 billion (€580 billion)** in deals with other companies; some estimates put the total near **$1 trillion**, with commitments stretching to 2045. Moody's recently put the five largest hyperscalers' total off-balance-sheet commitments at **$2.8 trillion**.
3. **Price cuts.** The third question asks whether recent price cuts are fuelling a boom that will carry the technology deeper into personal and business use. The photographed text breaks off here.
