## What it says (REPORTED, the article's)
- **Company:** Amethyst Care, an artificial intelligence healthcare business. Founded 2024 by Rebecca McManus. Based in Dogpatch Labs, Dublin. Six staff. Funding to date €620,000. Aiming to raise **€1.5m** in its next funding round.
- **Product:** an AI voice companion using a microphone speaker in the home. It talks to the person through the day, gives medication reminders, asks whether they locked the door or remembered to call a son, and can hold a conversation. A clinician or caregiver logs into a portal, creates a profile, builds medication and daily care plans, and tracks daily care with two-way communication.
- **Origin:** McManus's father had Parkinson's and Lewy body dementia and her mother had breast cancer. She worked in tech sales, then did research with Trinity College Dublin before building the product.
- **Stage:** about 24 months building and doing trials, the trials taking place in Ohio over the last 12 months. The company just spun out of Trinity over the summer and was awarded Enterprise Ireland Pre-Seed Start Fund (PSSF) funding. US headquarters in Mentor, Ohio, about 20 minutes outside Cleveland.
- **Plans:** focus on Ohio for the next 18 months, then several other states and Ireland. Goal: be global. McManus says Enterprise Ireland's help went beyond money (advisers, a business plan, prioritisation).
