# Meridian — The Map
### The anatomy of your engine: seven managers, fifty skills

This is what your system is made of. You chose to build it as managers. Each manager is a domain drawn from your own experience, and under each one sits a set of skills, the specific jobs it can do. You talk only to Meridian at the front; Meridian works out which managers a question touches, runs them, checks the work, and hands you one clean answer.

The **tier** on each manager tells you which ones carry the mandatory human gate before anything is acted on.

---

## 1. Commercial & Deal · *Standard tier*
Turns a commercial intent into a structured, negotiable position.
- **Market research** — sizes the market, players, demand, comparable plays.
- **Deal modelling** — builds the structure and economics, tests the variants.
- **Contract drafting** — drafts commercial terms, hands binding ones to Legal.
- **Partner profiling** — track record, standing, fit and risk of a counterparty.
- **Competitor analysis** — maps the field and where your position is strong or exposed.
- **Pricing strategy** — sets or stress-tests pricing against market, cost and willingness to pay.
- **Term sheet drafting** — drafts a term sheet, or reads one you have been handed and flags the terms that bite.

## 2. Legal & Governance · *High-stakes, the heaviest specialist*
Irish law throughout. The grounding rule is absolute here.
- **Statute retrieval** — pulls current Irish statute (Statute Book) and EU law (EUR-Lex), quotes the operative section.
- **Case law search** — finds and reads judgments (Courts Service, IRLII, BAILII).
- **Compliance checking** — tests a position against regulation, including the EU AI Act and WRC.
- **Doc drafting** — drafts legal documents, grounded to retrieved authority.
- **Jurisdiction locking** — confirms Irish law as the frame, flags any cross-border element.
- **Directors' duties** — the personal-liability lens on any corporate action, top priority here.
- **Regulatory filing** — what must be filed, with whom, by when.

## 3. Finance & Restructuring · *High-stakes tier*
Board-level finance and restructuring, tuned to SME and mid-market.
- **Cash flow modelling** — builds and stress-tests cash flow, the first read on whether a business is sound.
- **Creditor schedule** — assembles the creditor position for a distressed situation.
- **Investor memo** — investor-facing materials, every figure grounded.
- **Tax analysis** — works the tax position against Revenue, routes legal interpretation to Legal.
- **Valuation** — values the business or asset, shows method and inputs.
- **Restructuring scenarios** — models the routes through distress, including CVL and the alternatives.
- **Succession planning** — the financial and ownership mechanics of a handover.

## 4. Property & Development · *High-stakes tier*
Residential, commercial, leasehold, freehold. Nothing about title or a comparable is recalled, only retrieved.
- **Title search** — the title position from the PRA and Land Registry.
- **Planning research** — planning position and history from the planning authority.
- **Feasibility analysis** — whether a development stacks up: cost, value, constraint, return.
- **Comparable transactions** — real comparables, each grounded, never estimated.
- **Lease review** — flags term, rent review, break, repair, use.
- **Development appraisal** — the development economics end to end.
- **Asset structuring** — how a property asset is held, routing the legal and tax mechanics to those managers.

## 5. AI Strategy & Adoption · *Standard tier*
Your forward-facing commercial manager, where the Meridian Intelligence service offer is shaped.
- **EU AI Act retrieval** — current text and obligations from EUR-Lex.
- **Readiness assessment** — where an organisation stands on AI readiness and risk.
- **Policy drafting** — AI governance policy and framework documents.
- **Education programme** — staff education and AI-literacy programmes.
- **Partner onboarding** — onboards a Golden Generation partner and their client materials.
- **Governance audit** — audits an organisation's AI governance against the framework.
- **Roadmap build** — a structured AI adoption roadmap, sequenced and costed.

## 6. Business Transformation & Growth · *Standard tier*
Where businesses come to be made better, not just advised. The most likely entry point for a live matter.
- **Process audit** — how the business actually runs and where it leaks time, cost or value.
- **Revenue gap analysis** — the money left on the table: pricing, underused assets, missed lines.
- **New product scoping** — scopes a new product or service, with the case for it.
- **Systems review** — the systems the business runs on and what no longer serves it.
- **Operational improvement** — findings turned into specific, sequenced changes.
- **Growth roadmap** — the improvements as a costed, sequenced plan.
- **Benchmark analysis** — the business against its sector, every comparison grounded.

## 7. AI Equity & Investment · *High-stakes, frontier caveat*
AI as an asset class. The numbers are grounded hard; the thesis is marked as judgement.
- **Asset class research** — AI as an asset class and the live movement of institutional capital into it.
- **Equity structure modelling** — the splits, mechanics and dilution of an arrangement.
- **AI valuation analysis** — an AI-driven valuation, method and inputs shown.
- **Investment memo** — the memo, every number grounded.
- **Portfolio exposure** — exposure to AI assets across a portfolio.
- **Term negotiation** — the terms of an arrangement, with Commercial and Legal on the binding parts.
- **Market intelligence** — the current state of the AI-investment market, grounded and dated.
- **Equity-for-AI-capability** — AI capability contributed in exchange for equity: the contribution valued, the equity it earns, the terms.
