# Six Slices to Add
### Written, placed, and ready to paste

Here are the six new skills, ready to go in. Each one names its home manager and gives you the exact line to paste. Open the manager file, find the `## Sub-agents (the slices)` list, paste the line at the bottom, save. That is the whole job for each one. Use the template in document 03 if you want the seven steps beside you.

Three of the six land in **Legal & Governance**, which makes sense: it is the heaviest manager and closest to your instinct as a solicitor.

---

### 1. Read-this-for-me
**Home:** Legal & Governance — `meridian/managers/02-legal-and-governance.md`
Your most daily move, made a first-class skill: drop in a document, get back what matters.

```
- **Read-this-for-me** — takes a contract, lease, term sheet or email dropped in, reads it against the retrieved authority, and returns what matters, what bites and what to push back on, flagging anything binding for a qualified solicitor before it is relied on.
```

---

### 2. Meeting-prep brief
**Home:** Commercial & Deal — `meridian/managers/01-commercial-and-deal.md`
One grounded page before you walk into any room.

```
- **Meeting-prep brief** — before a meeting, assembles one grounded page: who is being met, their likely position, where the leverage sits, and the two or three things that could go wrong.
```

---

### 3. Argue-the-other-side
**Home:** Legal & Governance — `meridian/managers/02-legal-and-governance.md`
*(Naturally cross-cutting. Placed in Legal for now; if you later want it on every answer, lift it into the orchestrator file, per document 03.)*
Native language for a former solicitor, and it hardens every output you already get.

```
- **Argue-the-other-side** — on command, red-teams Meridian's own recommendation, sets out the strongest case against it, and names where it would break, so no position is carried that has not been attacked first.
```

---

### 4. What-else scan  ·  *already live in the master engine*
**Home:** Business Transformation & Growth — `meridian/managers/06-business-transformation.md`
Turns every job into the next piece of business. This is the one built live on 29 July, and it is the model for the shape of all the others. Paste it into your copy to match the master.

```
- **What-else scan** — at the close of any matter, surfaces the adjacent work, services and follow-on opportunities the client now needs next, so every piece of work opens the next one, and routes any real deal, structure or funding need to Commercial, Legal or Finance.
```

---

### 5. Say-it-to-the-client
**Home:** AI Strategy & Adoption — `meridian/managers/05-ai-strategy-and-adoption.md`
*(Also naturally cross-cutting; placed here because this manager already shapes client-ready material.)*
Straight into the Meridian Intelligence service offer.

```
- **Say-it-to-the-client** — takes any grounded, technical output and renders a clean, plain-English page to hand straight to a client, the accuracy intact and the sources kept.
```

---

### 6. Obligations and deadlines schedule
**Home:** Legal & Governance — `meridian/managers/02-legal-and-governance.md`
Nothing slips, and the liability lens is on it.

```
- **Obligations and deadlines schedule** — pulls every date, statutory clock, filing and obligation out of a matter into one clear schedule, with the directors' duties and liability flags on it, and the hard dates sent to a qualified solicitor to confirm.
```

---

**When all six are in:** your engine goes from fifty skills to fifty-six, and you did it yourself. Add them one at a time, wake Meridian after each, and ask it a quick question that would use the new skill, so you see each one fire. That is the proof step, and it is the satisfying part.
