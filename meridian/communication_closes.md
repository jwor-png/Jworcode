# Communication Closes -- Standard Library
*Adopted by John, 1 July 2026, for use across Meridian and the Sales Orchestration going forward. Source: Threads post by richluckyg_ ("7 closes that actually move things forward"), adopted as John's standard. These replace the passive default "Let me know if you have any questions." Purpose: every outward email ends by creating momentum, not waiting for it.*

*House-style note: one close used an em dash; rendered here without it, per John's no-em-dash rule.*

---

## The seven closes

1. **"I'll follow up Thursday unless I hear from you first."**
   Sets a timeline. Removes the burden of them having to remember to reply. *Best for: active pipeline, keeping a live thread moving.*

2. **"One thing I need from you before I can move forward: [specific ask]."**
   Clears the path. Makes it easy to respond with exactly what you need. *Best for: when you are blocked on one input.*

3. **"Happy to talk through this, and I'll send a calendar link if easier."**
   Offers a better format without creating extra work. *Best for: complex matters better handled by voice.*

4. **"No need to reply unless something looks off."**
   Gives explicit permission to stay silent. *Best for: respecting a senior inbox (board members, CEOs, busy principals).*

5. **"If this is approved, I'll [specific next action] immediately."**
   Connects their response to real momentum. People approve faster when they see the consequence. *Best for: approvals and sign-offs.*

6. **"The decision point is [specific choice]. Your preference by [date] keeps us on track."**
   Names the decision and the deadline. Impossible to passively ignore. *Best for: forcing a clean decision from a stalled thread.*

7. **"I'll take silence as agreement unless you flag something."**
   Default-forward. Bold but professional. The phrase senior operators actually use. *Best for: established relationships where you already have standing. Use with care on a first or cold approach.*

---

## How to apply
- **Pick one close per email**, matched to the situation above. Never stack them.
- **Default across the Sales Orchestration:** closes 1, 5 and 6 for the Personalized Intelligence Experience demo outreach and pipeline follow-ups.
- **Board and senior contacts (UHL, AHL, IoD):** close 4 respects the inbox; close 6 when a decision is genuinely needed.
- **Governance:** the close does not change the DECISION-READY rule. Nothing external commits without Shane's alignment where a venture is involved. These are how John closes, not what he commits to.
