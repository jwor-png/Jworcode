# WEBSITE REVIEW — meridianintelligence.ie

*11 September 2026. Reviewed from screenshots supplied by John. Partial: five screens seen, more to follow. Status: IN REVIEW.*

**Recommendation: do not promote the site on LinkedIn until items 1 and 2 are fixed.** Both are ten-minute jobs.

---

## STOP ITEMS

### 1. An internal instruction is published on the live site

On the Governance Floor page, inside the external commentary box:

> **"[SOURCE PENDING: do not publish until the original piece is located and linked]"**

**This is a note to the author that has been published to the world.** It appears directly beneath the page that sets out the grounding rule, the independent check and confidence tagging.

The damage is specific and it is not cosmetic. A reader arrives at the page that promises "no figure, ruling or valuation appears unless it has been retrieved from a primary source", and immediately below it finds an unsourced item carrying an instruction not to publish it, published. **That is the single most quotable thing on the site for anyone who wants to undermine it**, and it undermines the one claim the whole practice rests on.

**Fix: delete the bracketed line, and either remove the EY block or replace it as set out at item 3.**

### 2. A placeholder is published on the investor page

> "forty years of pattern recognition across **[ENGAGEMENT COUNT]** engagements to date"

An unfilled template field on the page addressed to investors. It reads as unfinished, and on a page asking people to request investor materials that is a poor first impression.

**Fix: insert the real number, or rewrite the sentence so no count is needed.** "Across four decades of engagements" carries the same weight and needs no figure. **Do not insert a number that cannot be evidenced**, because the investor page is exactly where an unsupported figure will be tested.

## 3. THE EY BLOCK CAN NOW BE UPGRADED, AND SHOULD BE

The block currently reads as UNVERIFIED external commentary with no source. As of today it does not need to.

**What is now established from EY Ireland's own website, not from the clipping:**
- **Grit Young**, EY-Parthenon Partner and EY Ireland Technology, Media and Entertainment and Telecommunications Industry Leader, in post since 2024.
- EY Ireland newsroom material in which she argues long-term success belongs to countries that develop **the skills, talent, governance, financing expertise and trusted institutions** to make AI work, and that Ireland already has or is building many of those capabilities.

**Suggested replacement, tagged REPORTED rather than UNVERIFIED, with a link to ey.com:**

> **REPORTED · EXTERNAL COMMENTARY**
> EY Ireland has argued that long-term success in AI will belong to countries that build the skills, talent, governance, financing expertise and trusted institutions to make it work, and that Ireland already has or is building many of those capabilities. Attributed to Grit Young, EY-Parthenon Partner and EY Ireland TMT Leader. Reproduced as external context for Meridian's position, not as a claim by Meridian.

**Why this is better.** It is linkable, checkable in seconds, published by EY itself, and it names governance and financing expertise, which is closer to Meridian's actual domain set than the data centre framing. And it lets the confidence tag on the page be REPORTED, which demonstrates the tagging system working rather than showing an untagged gap.

**Discipline, unchanged:** nothing on the site may imply EY endorses, recommends or is associated with Meridian.

## 4. TWO GOVERNANCE POINTS THAT ARE NOT WEBSITE FAULTS

### Shane is named publicly as partner

> "Technical architecture and execution led by **Shane McCarthy**, partner."

Naming an individual as partner of a commercial practice on a public website is a representation about him and about the structure of the business. The Meridian shareholding agreement is still an open item.

**This is not a criticism of the wording. It is a check: has Shane seen and agreed this page?** The standing rule is no commercial or technical commitment without his alignment, and a public partner designation is exactly that.

### The United Hardware roles are now publicly tied to the practice

> "Chair of AHL PLC. Independent Non-Executive Director and Chair of the Audit and Risk Committee of United Hardware."

Both are accurate and there is nothing wrong with stating them. But the site now publicly links the audit and risk chairmanship of United Hardware to a commercial AI practice that John owns, alongside an investor page.

**The disclosure to the United Hardware chairman was to be made this week and Meridian's record does not show it as done.** It should be made before the site is promoted, not after. Promoting first and disclosing second is the wrong order and it is entirely avoidable.

## 5. SMALLER OBSERVATIONS

- **The footer line is right.** "Meridian informs; it does not represent" plus "Meridian does not provide legal, tax or investment advice" is the correct pairing and matches the engine.
- **The Governance Floor page is the best thing on the site.** Grounding rule, independent check, confidence tagging, status labelling and fail-closed, laid out plainly. No competitor publishes this. Once items 1 and 2 are fixed it is the page to send people to.
- **The logo still carries the "INTELLIGENT AI. REAL IMPACT." tagline**, which was deliberately cropped out of the LinkedIn assets in August as off-message. Worth aligning so the brand reads the same everywhere.
- **"An operator-led framework, not a report factory"** is a strong line and it is consistent with the LinkedIn series.

The drafted post says the site states what Meridian will not do. **That is true and the Governance Floor page carries it well.** The post can go out as written, but only after items 1 and 2 are corrected, because the post drives traffic directly at the page where both faults appear.

**Sequence: fix the two placeholders, upgrade or remove the EY block, confirm Shane has seen the partner line, make the United Hardware disclosure, then post.**
