## THE PRICING LOGIC — say this before any number

Meridian is not priced on time. It is priced on **the consequence of the decision it is answering.**

> **The rule: the fee sits roughly two orders of magnitude below the value of the decision.**
> A €500,000 decision earns a €5,000 brief. A €5m transaction earns a €20,000 one.

This is the sentence that does the selling, and it is the sentence that stops the conversation becoming a day-rate negotiation. If a client is weighing whether to spend €4,500, the honest answer is that their decision is probably not big enough for Meridian yet, and saying so builds more trust than discounting.

**The comparison that makes it obvious.** A mid-tier consultancy strategic review is €40,000 to €100,000 and takes six to twelve weeks. Meridian delivers a comparable depth of multi-domain analysis in days, at a fraction of that, because the analysis is produced by a governed engine and the senior time goes into judgment and verification rather than production. That is the whole value argument in two sentences.

## 1. DECISION BRIEF — €3,750 founding / €4,950 list

**The one-line pitch:** *One decision. Five days. A written position you can take to your board.*

**What it answers.** A single, specific, high-consequence question with a yes or no at the end of it.

**Already delivered in this shape:** Should United Hardware buy a standalone garden centre? Should Stargas enter cylinder testing? What instrument should Barber Republic take Ed Lawton's £50,000 on?

**What the client gets.**
- A structured written brief, typically 8 to 15 pages, branded and print-ready
- A clear recommendation with the reasoning attached
- The three to five risks that would change the answer
- Every claim labelled verified, estimated or unverified
- The questions the client should be able to answer before committing
- A 45-minute call to walk it through

**Scope boundary.** One decision. Up to three of the seven domains. One round of clarification. Additional domains at €1,250 each.

**Why it is priced here.** It is the entry product and its job is to prove the practice on a real question. At under €5,000 it sits inside a managing director's own signing authority, which means it does not need a board paper to buy. That is deliberate, and it is the single most important commercial property of this product.

## 2. EXECUTIVE INTELLIGENCE REVIEW — €9,750 founding / €12,950 list

**The one-line pitch:** *Your whole situation, interrogated from seven senior angles at once, in ten days.*

**What it answers.** Not one question but a position. Where is this business exposed, where is value being left on the table, and what should the board do about it.

**Already delivered in this shape:** the Arcade Trader four-domain consolidated report with growth model and value-building analysis. The Fleming Medical seven-domain investigative analysis, which corrected an earlier optimistic scoring, found value concentrated in three obscured segments, and closed with the five questions the board had to answer before any capital event.

**What the client gets.**
- All seven domains interrogated, eight to ten specific questions each
- A consolidated executive intelligence brief with a synthesised position
- The five key risks, ranked
- A programme of recommended actions with sequencing
- The human gate: the questions the board must be able to answer before committing
- A 90-minute presentation to the client's board or leadership team

**Scope boundary.** One business or one division. Ten working days from full information. Follow-on work quoted separately.

**Why it is priced here.** This is the flagship and the reference product. Fleming Medical is the proof that it produces something a board genuinely cannot get elsewhere in under six weeks. At €12,950 list it is roughly a quarter of what a mid-tier firm charges for less, delivered in a sixth of the time.

## 3. TRANSACTION INTELLIGENCE — €15,000 founding / €19,500 list

**The one-line pitch:** *Due-diligence-grade analysis before you sign, structured the way the other side's adviser will attack it.*

**What it answers.** Should we do this deal, at what value, on what instrument, and what will we regret in eighteen months.

**Already delivered in this shape:** the Barber Republic funding structure work. A funding ladder across bridge, pre-seed, seed and Series 1 to 3, dilution modelled across rounds, two investor scenarios, instrument recommendation and investor protections, plus a rollout funding roadmap. It converged independently with the founder's own separately written documents, which is what gave the recommendation its authority in the room.

**What the client gets.**
- Everything in the Executive Intelligence Review, applied to the transaction
- Valuation and dilution modelling across the funding or exit path
- Instrument analysis and recommendation, with the alternatives and why they were rejected
- Term-by-term negotiating position, including what to concede and what not to
- Counterparty read: what the other side will push for and how to answer it
- Standing availability through the negotiation window

**Scope boundary.** One transaction. Ten to fifteen working days. Negotiation support beyond the window moves to Intelligence Partner.

**Why it is priced here.** A corporate finance adviser on the same transaction charges a retainer plus a success fee running to multiples of this. Meridian is not replacing that adviser. It is making sure the client walks into the room already knowing their position, which is worth considerably more than the fee and costs a fraction of getting it wrong.

**Optional success element.** On transactions above €2m, consider a reduced fee plus a success element of 0.5 to 1 per cent on completion. Offer it, do not lead with it. It signals confidence and it materially raises the ceiling on this product.

## 5. AI EQUITY STRUCTURING — bespoke, fee plus equity

**The one-line pitch:** *AI capability contributed for equity, structured so it survives a funding round.*

**What it answers.** How do we contribute AI capability into a business in exchange for a shareholding, and how do we structure it so a Series A investor accepts it rather than unwinding it.

**This is Manager 7 and it is the line almost nobody else is offering.** Barber Republic is arguably the first live instance: milestone-based sweat equity for build work, alongside an investor's cash on a convertible, with the two carefully kept distinct.

**Commercial shape.** A structuring fee at Transaction Intelligence rates, plus an equity participation negotiated case by case. Slowest to convert and hardest to sell cold, which is why it is line five and not line one. But it is the line that turns Meridian from a fee business into an asset business, and every engagement in it compounds.

## HOW TO PRESENT PRICE — five rules

1. **Never lead with the number. Lead with the decision.** "What is the decision worth to you if you get it right, and what does it cost you if you get it wrong?" The price answers itself after that.
2. **Give three options, not one.** Decision Brief, Executive Review, Intelligence Partner. Buyers who are given one option decide whether to buy. Buyers given three decide which to buy.
3. **Anchor high, then place.** Mention the mid-tier consultancy comparison of €40,000 to €100,000 over six to twelve weeks before naming your own figure. Every number after that sounds reasonable.
4. **Hold the price. Move the scope.** If a client cannot reach €12,950, they do not get a discounted Review. They get a Decision Brief at €4,950. Discounting the product teaches the market the price was invented. Reducing the scope teaches them it was real.
5. **Say no to the ones that are too small.** A client with a €50,000 decision is not a Meridian client yet. Telling them so, and pointing them somewhere sensible, is worth more in reputation than the fee is in cash. It is also the single most credible thing a new practice can do.
