## PART 1: THE PRODUCTS (all seven, approved)

### 1. Decision Brief
**One decision. Five days. A written position you can take to your board.**
Answers one specific, high-consequence question with a yes or no at the end of it. Up to three domains, five working days, an 8 to 15 page brief and a 45-minute call.

It is the entry product and the door into everything else. It exists to prove the practice on a real question, and it is built to sit inside a managing director's own signing authority, so a client can say yes without taking it to a board.

### 2. Executive Intelligence Review
**Your whole situation, interrogated from seven senior angles at once, in ten days.**
Answers not one question but a position: where the business is exposed, where value is being left on the table, and what the board should do about it. All seven domains, ten working days, a consolidated brief, the five key risks ranked, a sequenced programme of actions, the questions the board must answer before committing, and a 90-minute presentation to the board or leadership team.

It is the flagship. It is for the business that suspects there is a problem it has not yet put a name to.

### 3. Transaction Intelligence
**Due diligence grade analysis before you sign, structured the way the other side's adviser will attack it.**
Answers whether to do the deal, at what value, on what instrument, and what you will regret in eighteen months. Ten to fifteen working days. Valuation and dilution modelling, instrument analysis with the alternatives and why each was rejected, a term by term negotiating position, a read on the counterparty, and availability through the negotiation window.

It does not replace the solicitor or the accountant. It means the client walks into the room already knowing their position.

### 4. Intelligence Partner (retained)
**A standing seat at the decision table, not a project.**
Answers everything, as it arises, across a year. Three tiers: Board Seat (one Decision Brief a quarter), Intelligence Partner (one brief a month and one Executive Review a year), and Chair's Counsel (unlimited briefs within reason, direct access to John).

This is the rung that makes Meridian a business rather than a series of engagements, because a retained relationship compounds and removes the need to win every client from scratch.

### 5. AI Equity Structuring
**AI capability contributed for equity, structured so it survives a funding round.**
Answers how AI capability can be contributed to a business in exchange for a shareholding in a form a later investor will accept rather than unwind. Bespoke scope and timing.

It is the slowest to convert and the hardest to sell cold, which is why it sits last, but it is the line that turns Meridian from a fee business into an asset business. Not for LinkedIn.

### 6. AI Dependency and Resilience Review
**What happens to your business if your AI stack fails, is withdrawn, or turns adversarial — mapped and costed before it happens, not after.**
Maps AI and supplier technology dependence across the business, the contractual and regulatory position on each, and what the realistic adverse cases actually cost. Narrower in domain count than the Executive Intelligence Review (principally AI Strategy, Legal & Governance, Finance) but technically deep.

**APPROVED for inclusion, 7 Oct 2026** — previously parked pending the "Ambrion boundary" with Shane. John's own standing safeguard: he will not take on an engagement where a genuine conflict (e.g. a client's own AI stack including Ambrion/Velocity AI, which John and Shane hold a commercial stake in) would arise — assessed case by case, not excluded structurally. The underlying boundary question with Shane remains open in principle, to be addressed directly if and when a live case presents it.

### 7. Board Paper Review
**The papers for the next board meeting, read beforehand, with the unanswered questions handed back before the room does.**
A bounded, single-meeting-scope product — faster turnaround than a Decision Brief.

**APPROVED for inclusion, 7 Oct 2026** — previously parked over proximity to John's own board roles (AHL Plc Chair; UHL Independent Non-Executive Director and Chair, Audit & Risk Committee). John's own standing safeguard: engagements screened case by case for any overlap with AHL/UHL's own sector relationships or counterparties before acceptance, rather than excluding the product.
