On a public profile that will be read by boards, and used to win succession work sitting beside real solicitors, the wording has to be exactly right and defensible.

## WHAT IS DELIBERATELY NOT IN IT

Each of these was considered and excluded for a reason.

- **AI literacy programmes, workshops, executive briefings, adoption roadmaps.** These are Ambrion's products, funded through Skillnet. Claiming them personally collides with the boundary settled on 5 August, and it puts John into the crowded end of the market rather than the scarce end.
- **"Helping organisations embrace AI with confidence."** True, generic, and indistinguishable from several hundred other Irish profiles. It also positions him as an AI adviser, which is precisely what the two chosen positions are not.
- **Any claim about being among the few, the first, or uniquely placed.** Unverifiable. State the facts and let the reader reach the conclusion themselves; it is more convincing arrived at than asserted.
- **Meridian's pricing, product names or the seven domains.** The profile is the person. The practice is a link.
