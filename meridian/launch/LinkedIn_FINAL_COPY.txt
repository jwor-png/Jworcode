=== ABOUT (1921/2600) ===
Forty years, the first fifteen of them in legal practice. I practised conveyancing and probate, and I am a Fellow of the Irish Institute of Legal Executives. The rest has been spent in and around boardrooms. I chair AHL PLC, and I am an independent non-executive director and chair of the Audit and Risk Committee at United Hardware.

Succession is where those two halves meet. I handled succession in the farming community, which is the hardest version of it. The land, the home and the business are one asset, one child usually takes it and the others do not, and the kitchen table is harder than any of the legal work around it. More recently I have been working on succession planning for the member businesses of United Hardware, rural and family-owned, second and third generation.

Most owners put it off for years, not from avoidance but because no single adviser covers the whole of it. The accountant sees the tax. The solicitor sees the instrument. Very few see the business, the family and the paperwork together.

The other thing occupying me is AI at board level. The EU AI Act has made AI governance a board obligation with directors' duties attached, and most boards have nobody who can tell them where they stand. I am not a technical specialist and have no ambition to become one. What I bring is knowing which questions to ask, and when an answer is sound rather than merely confident.

Both come back to the same thing. Leaders rarely lack information. They lack a defensible answer at the moment a decision has to be made. That is why I built Meridian Intelligence: one high-consequence decision, one written position, evidence checked to source, and the things that would change the answer named. It informs; it does not represent.

If you are weighing a succession, a decision too expensive to get wrong, or looking for a director who can hold the AI conversation at board level, I am glad to talk.
