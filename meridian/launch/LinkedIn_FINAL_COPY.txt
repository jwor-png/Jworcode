=== ABOUT (1561 chars) ===
Forty years, the first fifteen in legal practice: conveyancing, probate, and a Fellow of the Irish Institute of Legal Executives. The rest in and around boardrooms. I chair AHL PLC, and I am an independent non-executive director and chair of the Audit and Risk Committee at United Hardware.

Succession is where the legal and commercial halves of my career meet. I handled it in the farming community in practice, where the land, the home and the business are one asset and the kitchen table is harder than the legal work. It is the decision owners put off longest, because no single adviser covers the whole of it. The accountant sees the tax. The solicitor sees the instrument. Very few see the business, the family and the paperwork together.

The other thing occupying me is AI at board level. I am not a technical specialist and have no ambition to become one, but I am not a spectator either. I use AI seriously every day, and I built and run a governed intelligence system of my own, so I know from the inside what good looks like and where these systems quietly go wrong. The EU AI Act has made AI governance a board obligation with directors' duties attached, and most boards have nobody at the table who can tell them where they stand.

Leaders rarely lack information. They lack a defensible answer at the moment a decision has to be made. That is why I built Meridian Intelligence.

If you are weighing a succession, a decision too expensive to get wrong, or looking for a director who can hold the AI conversation at board level, I am glad to talk.
