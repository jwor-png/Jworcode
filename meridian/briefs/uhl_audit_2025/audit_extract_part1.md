- **Section 20 Leases:** removal of operating/finance lease distinction for lessees; more leases on-balance sheet (asset and liability, similar to extant finance lease accounting); recognition exemptions for short-term and low-value leases; higher threshold for low-value assets vs IFRS 16 means fewer on-balance leases; micro-entities: no equivalent change to FRS 105. Key benefits: improved transparency over indebtedness; access to capital; consistency with international principles reducing 'GAAP differences'.
- **Section 23 Revenue from Contracts with Customers:** single comprehensive five-step model for revenue recognition for all contracts, based on identifying distinct goods or services promised and the consideration the entity will be entitled to in exchange; micro-entities: similar amendments to FRS 105 with additional simplifications. Key benefits: consistent accounting across entity sizes and contract types; more reliable information about nature, amount and timing of revenue and cash flows.
