**Document:** Group Risk Review Framework Update, **page 3 of 10**. Screenshot 16:49.

**Executive Summary (verbatim):**

"As requested by the Board and with input from the Audit Committee, work has commenced on the development of a Group Risk Review Framework. Initially, we have looked at developing a new Group Risk Policy. This policy should set out the guidelines for how risk will be identified, evaluated and controlled across the organisation. Attached is the initial draft risk policy that has been prepared. This policy is drawn from similar policies in other organisations. It is relatively simple, compared to some that were reviewed, but it should provide a good level of control and comfort.

The main body of work will be in drafting the associated Risk Assessment Register (sample layout below). The register should document all significant risks that pose a health and safety, financial or reputational risk to the organisation or its staff and customers. Each risk will be assessed and depending on the level of net risk, the appropriate upward reporting will be determined. While there may be a number of significant risks identified in each function / business channel, only those that are deemed material at Group level might be surfaced up for Board review. The other local risks should still be tracked, assessed, and managed at a functional level.

Work on the initial risk register will be undertaken over the coming months and a version will be circulated for review ahead of the next Board meeting."

**Statement of policy (verbatim):**

"United Hardware will adopt best practice in the identification, evaluation and control of risk to ensure that risks are avoided, transferred, mitigated or accepted. United Hardware will embed a full and effective consideration of risk within the planning and the management of both new and existing activities across the organisation. United Hardware is not prepared to take risks that cannot be controlled or mitigated that may result in serious harm to our staff, our customers, our financial position or our reputation. United Hardware's acceptable net risk threshold for risks are clearly defined, and will be managed through a risk assessment process. A contingency plan will be put in place in case of a severe business disruption."

**1. General principles**

**1.1 Scope:** "The purpose of this policy is to summarise United Hardware's approach to risk, and to define the framework within which staff, management and the Board of Directors are expected to operate. The Risk Policy is applicable to all elements of United Hardware's work. This policy will inform the organisational risk assessment, the main tool through which we will manage risk. Appendix A provides definitions of key terms used throughout the document."

**1.2 Responsibility:** "The Chief Risk Officer is responsible for ensuring that the policy in this document is implemented efficiently and effectively. All management and staff are expected to facilitate this process. Appendix B provides the detailed responsibilities for each group."

**Appendix A-Definitions:**

- **Risk** is defined as "the uncertainty surrounding events and their outcomes that may have a significant effect, either enhancing or inhibiting operational performance; achievements of organisational aims and objectives; or meeting expectations of stakeholders."
- **Gross Risk** is defined as the risk before controls or mitigating measures have been put in place.
- **Net Risk** is the residual risk after controls or mitigating measures have been put in place to reduce the risk.
- **Likelihood** is the likelihood of the risk in question occurring.
- **Impact** is the severity of the effect on the organisation if the risk in question occurs.
- **Risk Owners** are the individuals and functional teams identified as having responsibility for the management, control, monitoring and review of the identified risks in the organisation's risk register.
- **Governance Risk** includes the risks related to an organisation's ethical and legal management, the transparency and accuracy of company performance, and in involvement in other ESG initiatives.
- **Strategic Risk** can be defined as the inability to achieve the Institute's strategic goals or objectives as set out in the Strategic Plan and risk of not availing of opportunities when they arise. (Again "the Institute" — template carry-over.)
- **Compliance Risk** is defined as the risk of legal sanctions, material financial loss, or reputation loss the organisation may suffer as a result of its failure to comply with laws, its own regulations, code of conduct, and standards of best/good practice.
- **Operational Risk** is defined as the risk of loss resulting from inadequate or failed internal processes, people and systems or from external events.
- **Financial Risk** can be defined as the exposure to losses arising as a result of the need to improve the management of the Institute's financial assets.
- **Environment Risk** is considered to be external when an organization has little or no control over if, when or how it might occur
- **Reputational Risk** is defined as exposure to losses arising as a result of bad press, negative public image and the need to improve stakeholder relationship management.
- **Control activity:** An action taken to minimise the negative consequences of a risk. A control differs from a process activity as a well-designed control should either prevent a negative consequence from occurring in the first place or detect that the negative consequence has occurred and initiate corrective actions.
- **Mitigating actions:** A mitigation action is a specific action, project, activity, or process taken to reduce or eliminate long-term risk. Mitigating actions may be 'one off' in nature rather than reoccurring and may involve changes to operating procedures such as the introduction of a new control.
