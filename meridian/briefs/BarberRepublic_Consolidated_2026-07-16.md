## 3. Funding structure intelligence (the new work)

**The fork in the bridge instrument, and it changes the offer to Johnny's funders:**

- **Convertible loan note (John's WhatsApp structure: option to convert to agreed equity OR be repaid with a coupon).** Maximum flexibility, exactly the choice architecture proposed. Cost: a CLN is debt; shares issued on conversion of a loan do not qualify for SEIS/EIS, and any repayment option or interest disqualifies the instrument. Funders get no tax relief. (REPORTED: SeedLegals, Harper James, Vestd, Lewis Silkin commentary; consistent across sources.)
- **Advance Subscription Agreement (ASA).** SEIS gives individual investors 50% income tax relief, EIS 30%, which materially de-risks their cheque. Conditions: the money MUST convert to shares (no repayment leg, no interest, no refund), longstop maximum 6 months under HMRC guidance. (REPORTED, same sources.)
- **The practical answer: split the bridge by funder appetite.** Equity-minded funders come in by ASA and capture SEIS/EIS relief. Fixed-return funders come in by CLN with the coupon, knowing relief is unavailable. Both documents are short and standard. SEIS eligibility of the company itself (trade, age, gross assets) needs confirming for a UK NewCo.
- **Mandatory gate:** instrument choice, SEIS/EIS eligibility and the note terms go to a UK solicitor and tax adviser before anything is signed. This is a binding-obligation, tax-position decision: Meridian informs, the professionals paper it.
