How Meridian Works

A business challenge comes in. Meridian routes it to every domain it genuinely touches, and those domains work in parallel rather than in sequence. Each produces its analysis under a strict grounding rule: no figure, authority or claim is presented unless it is sourced and confirmed. The findings are then independently verified, cross-checked before they are relied upon, and consolidated into a single Executive Intelligence Brief: the strategic opportunities, the principal risks ranked, the practical recommendations, the immediate priorities and the longer-term considerations. Every brief is reviewed personally by John before it is presented, and carries an honest status: decision-ready, or with the outstanding points named.

How the Domains Work Together

The strength of Meridian is not any single domain but the way they combine on a real decision. Two recent illustrations:

A new business-line feasibility. A group considering launching a specialist buying operation. Five domains worked the question together: Commercial modelled the opportunity and the analogues, Finance built the scenario economics and break-even, Legal mapped the competition-law and structural constraints, AI Strategy designed the technology layer, and Business Growth set the rollout. The findings were independently verified, corrected where needed, and delivered as one decision-ready brief with every professional gate named, inside a single day.
A major property and funding decision. A significant purchase to be funded by a linked sale. Four domains combined: Property tested valuation, condition and planning; Finance modelled the funding, the borrowing capacity and the tax; Legal set out the transaction mechanics and the compliance risks; and Commercial framed the negotiation. An independent, higher-tier review then re-checked every load-bearing figure, correcting one material point, before the consolidated brief issued with its open questions clearly flagged.

In each case the client received, in a day, the breadth a large firm would assemble over weeks and several separate advisers, delivered as one accountable, verified view.
